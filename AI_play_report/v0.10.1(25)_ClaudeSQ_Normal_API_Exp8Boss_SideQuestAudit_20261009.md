## What decided the run
1. **`depthLimit Xf-3` again carried the middle**: D4 gates 1–5, D5 gates 1–4, D6 gates 1–4, D7 gates 1–4 each fell with zero build calls.
2. **EXP is the hidden clock at D8.** I reached D8 at Lv31 (day 22.5). Gate 2 was rated 4% even with plans, so I farmed. EXP per 12 h at Lv31: D8 `1f-3` 25k, D8 `2f-3` 31k, D8 `beforeBoss` 46k, **D7 `beforeBoss` ~120–130k**, sorties about 8k (they Retreat/Defeat). Farming D7 for 2 days gave Lv33 and the next plan then worked. The Lv32→33 jump moved gate 5 from 10% to 51% with the same engine.
3. **The `pipe.sh` combo plan (HP + attackers, 28 calls) was far better than the single-gate GA.** At Lv33: gate 4 87%, gate 6 41–51%, boss 40–46% (with Fortification). Earlier I spent about 4 game days on a gate-2-only GA (17 calls, 40%) and combo GA (rated ≤8% on gate 5). Run `pipe.sh` first, then `lever` on all gates f=4..7 before choosing.
4. **I again lost time by stepping blind**: D6 boss fell within 3 blocks (up to 18 h unseen). Poll after each 6 h block when the boss is open.
5. **Sorties at D4+ are useless** (Retreat/Defeat; a Defeat stops the batch and costs a charge). They paid only for D1–D3.

## Tips for another AI
1. Reuse the 9-call opening (`open.py` in the run folder). D2 boss: `pipe.sh`-style plan C (12 calls), then 3 sorties are not needed.
2. Rate first: `lever.mjs save d f N` with `PLAN=` for f = gate floor (boss = 7). A base rating under 10% means either a plan or EXP; a plan under 50% usually means EXP.
3. EXP farming: set `destination` to the previous dungeon with `depthLimit beforeBoss` (D7 gave 4–5x the EXP of D8 gate depths). A level jump is worth more than a 60-call plan late.
4. Element weights: scan 6 sets per wall (`fire/ice/thunder`); the best one differs per wall (D5 ice 1.5, D7 thunder 0.5, D8 equal). Check `roomtab` for `win 0`.
5. `party/1 {deityId}` error `deity_in_use` now returns `details.heldByParty`; release with `party/N {deityId:"none"}` (2 calls total).
6. D8: the whole chain (gate 4 → boss) fell in 1.1 days once the plan was on at Lv33. Step 3 h and read the Global Diary for `bossFirstClear` (dungeon 8).
7. Exports (`commit/setting/backup/export`) are 1 call each and feed all offline tools; use them only before a plan.

### Balance reading
1. **Hard tier (0–25%)**: consecutive-wins 0/3, treasure-super-rare 0/2, poor-kid 1/4 — same three as the 300-day follow-up (9%, 2%, 45%). Samples are tiny (2–4 each).
2. **Sleeping and healing now complete (5/5, 4/4)**; in the 300-day run (build 22) they were 43% and 38%. Build 25 lengthened sleeping to 16 h and softened healing's multipliers (x1.2/1.4/1.6), which fits, but 9 samples are too few to say how much.
3. **Easy tier (100%)**: squander, exercise, AFK, savings, losers; embezzlement/donation 67–75%. At 90k–270k banked gold per party, gold quests cannot fail.
4. **Frequency is the real constraint, not difficulty.** 42 quests in 32 days for six parties; the bag is 1,287 "nothing" + 13 tickets (about 1% per roll). The played party PT1 got 3 (it sat on Clear-Gates almost the whole run); PT4 never left a gate and got 0. Reward (1 jewel) is invisible in a speed run.
5. **PT1 failures** (embezzlement and consecutive-wins at D8-level targets) match the earlier "quests scale with the dungeon" finding: failures cluster on the played, deep party (2 of 3) versus side parties (8 of 39).
6. Early quest timeline sample: PT1 was handed `consecutive-wins-1` (16 h limit) right after the D1 boss at day 1.5, while a Gate was about to reappear; it failed.

### Bugs and concerns
1. **Sortie batch `numberOfSortie: 6` stops after the first Defeat and silently returns fewer entries** (3–4 outcomes for 6 requested; charge accounting then looks inconsistent: `chargeStock` stayed 5–6 after batches). Report the number consumed, or refund unused charges.
2. **`sortie` EXP is not reflected in `party.experience`** right after the call (D3 six Clears: EXP unchanged, +11k only after the next step). Maybe applied lazily; if intended, document it.
3. **Gate list `[]` while `disclosedOutcome` is `Defeat`** after the boss falls (same as Side). The Global Diary `bossFirstClear` is the only cheap check.
4. **Side quest stats count only per-id aggregates**: no timestamps, no `expired` vs `God Battle cancelled` (0 cancelled this run, consistent with no God Battle overlap, but unverifiable).
5. **A quest rolls the instant a Clear-Gate list becomes empty** (e.g., right after a boss Clear, PT1 got `consecutive-wins`), then the next dungeon's first gate starts; the quest cannot be completed in the new dungeon's first hours. Consider not rolling when the next dungeon's gate chain restarts.
6. `read/observation/party?partyNumber=N` still 404 for locked slots (documented earlier; `compact.partyInfo` lists existing parties).
7. Positive: `destination_locked` and `deity_in_use.heldByParty` now carry reasons (earlier reports asked for these).
