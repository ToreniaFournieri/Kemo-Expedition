## What decided the run

1. **Per-character gear optimisation beat the generic GA (biggest finding).**
   - At D7 the boss forecast was **0/2000 clears** after many GA/hill-climb attempts. Selfin's sheet read M 858 × 17, against about 1,800 × 35 in earlier runs, because the general optimisers never gave the caster the T7 grimoires and wands.
   - Running the deterministic proxy optimiser on character 5 only (`proxyopt.mjs … "5" "fire:1.5,…"`) took boss-only damage from 0.43 to 0.96 of boss HP and the full-route boss forecast from 0% to **19.9%**. The boss fell within the next block.
   - At D8 I ran `proxyopt` for the attackers, then `hpopt` for Kemo and Laika, on the very first D8 checkpoint. Gate forecasts went **2nd gate 0.7% → 79%, 3rd 77%, 4th 56%, 5th 20%, boss gate 10%, boss 5%**, for 22 calls. D8 needed about 3.5 days instead of weeks.
   - Re-running the same two optimisers three days later raised the boss gate from 9.8% to 18.5% and the boss from 10.5% to 15%.
2. **Fertility (move-first +1) and Mirage are strong 1-call levers.**
   - Mirage took the D2 boss from 0% to 18% boss-only kills (D2 boss was still lost for 2.5 days before I applied it).
   - Fertility raised D5/D6 gate odds from 34% to 45% in the twin. It is only free if no side party holds it; PT3 held it by default.
3. **HP optimiser for supports.** `hpopt` with defence weight lifted PT1 HP from 8.8k to 11.5k at D6 (progress 0.54 → 0.67), and from 13.7k to 20.9k at D7.
4. **Oath/Nimble on the Cervin caster** raised the D7 boss-room reach from 11% to 31% and kept 11 slots (Devoted/Oath +2). Devoted on Lop helped at D6 (gate 3 from 11% to 36%).
5. **Side-party gear pooling (`SIDE=1`)** was needed from D5 on; PT2–4 farmed D2 for Illusion Breaker arrows (6309).

## Useful tips for another AI

1. Prefer deterministic per-character optimisers (`proxyopt` for attackers and casters, `hpopt` for supports) over a general GA. The GA objective goes flat when success is near 0%, and it misses a starved caster. Always check `pstats.mjs` — if a caster shows M far below about 1,500 × 30, fix it first.
2. Judge a plan with `dmgplan.mjs` (boss-only damage fraction, kills and reach) before spending API calls. Then confirm with `lever.mjs … f=7` using 1,000+ runs: boss-only kills overstate full-route clears.
3. Plans go stale quickly. FULL side parties re-equip on every block, so items in an old export vanish (`item_not_owned`, `slot_empty`). Export, plan and apply in one go.
4. **Do not change class while a character has an empty slot.** `changeBuild` main class then fails with HTTP 400 (see bugs). Fill the holes with any item first, or change class before removing gear.
5. Do not run several optimiser jobs at once on a 4-core box. I let about 14 jobs pile up and every search crawled until I killed them; three at a time finished in 2–4 minutes.
6. **Never `pkill -x node` while the play session runs.** It killed the Electron API (restart with the same `--user-data`; login returns 503 for about 20 s, then recovers the exact state). Also avoid `pkill -f` patterns that match your own command line.
7. `deity_in_use` means a side party holds it. Read `/read/build/party/{p}` for the others and set the holder to `none` (1 call).
8. A gate list that is empty, or only `godGate`, means the boss is down. Confirm with an export (`defeatedBossExpeditions[8]`). Clear does not always write a diary entry.
9. Check slots at levels 10/20/30: Kemo gained an empty slot at 30 and the optimiser filled it only after I included it.
10. The client `t.py` retries `stale_revision` automatically, and each retry is another logged request (17 here). Commit right after a read that refreshes the revision rather than after an export.

## Opportunities for future runs

- **Apply `proxyopt` + `hpopt` from D2/D3 onward**, not only at D7/D8. I found them late; the D4–D6 walls (9 days) and the D2 boss (4.5 days) are where the 4–8 day gap to SlotBurst's early game lies.
- **Test deity levers on every wall first** (1 call, Mirage, Fertility, Fortification, Precision, Attrition). Mirage would have saved about 2.5 days at the D2 boss.
- Reduce build churn: 303 of 552 requests were builds. A multi-slot equip or "apply set" endpoint would remove about 100 calls. Dry-run plans against a fresh export to avoid failed halves (6 failed calls this run).
- Model the instant sorties: PT1 held 3–6 stored sorties for most of the run and I never used them. The forecasts at the walls were too low to justify them.
- D6 and D7 had many rooms (3f-4, 4f-4, 5f-3, 5f-4) that cost attrition. A route-level objective that multiplies per-room survival would guide the GA better than boss-only or single-gate objectives.

## Bugs, errors and improvement requests

1. **`changeBuild` crash with empty equipment slots (confirmed bug).** `POST /commit/build/character/3/changeBuild {mainClassId:"sword-saint", simulation:true|false}` returned `400 invalid_request … "Cannot read properties of undefined (reading 'category')"` (calls 251, 272, 273) while Lop had two empty slots in the middle of the array (7 and 8) after a gear plan. After filling both slots with arrows (`equip … remainsMode:true`) the same call succeeded (HTTP 200). Even `simulation:true` fails, so the validation path dereferences an undefined item. Expected: success, or a clear warning.
2. **No retained kill log.** Clear entries appear only if the boss drops a boss rare, and PT1 keeps only 12 diary entries. Every previous report also lost the boss-kill log. Suggest pinning the first Clear of each dungeon, or exposing `bossFirstDefeatedAt` in the observation.
3. **Gate list semantics at D8.** After the kill the list becomes `[]` with no `godGate`, while earlier dungeons show `godGate`/`godEntry`. An empty list is ambiguous (it also appears when a boss gate is momentarily closed), so the native flag must be checked in an export. A `bossDefeated` flag per destination in `read/expedition/{p}/setting` would remove the export.
4. **Opaque `deity_in_use`.** The error does not name the party that holds the deity. Suggest `details.heldByParty`, or listing it in `validOptions`.
5. **`stale_revision` after every export.** `commit/setting/backup/export` returns binary and no revision, so the next commit is rejected once (17 logged retries). Return the current revision in a response header.
6. **Bossrare entry with a 24/24 Return and room-24 `draw`.** A `bossRare` diary entry (01:43) had `finalOutcome: Return`, room 24 `battle_Boss` `draw`, and `legacyIncomplete: true` (no events, `damageDealt: 0`), yet it lists tier-8 rewards. It is not clear whether a draw can grant a boss rare or whether the log is truncated by the AFK-emulation path.
7. **Version metadata.** `package.json` still says `0.9.8` while the spec and `build_number.txt` say v0.10.1 (3). The play session reports the version from package metadata.
8. **Runtime restart.** After the Electron process died, `logIn` returned `503 runtime_unavailable` for about 20 seconds, then `401 login_required` until a new login. This was my own `pkill`, not a game fault, but a retry hint (`Retry-After`) would help.
9. **Local tooling mistakes, not game bugs:** a wrong `RACE` spec for Felidian/Oath races crashed several offline runs; the `dmgspec` race-field order is `cid:race:main:sub:lineage:pred`; an auto-loop stop condition tested `godEntry` instead of `godGate` and stopped too early once.
