## What decided the run

1. **Early days were slightly slow and the D5 gates cost 2 days.** D3 to D5 were each 0.3 to 0.4 d behind ClaudeItem; D5 alone took 2.4 d (2.36 to ~4.8). Elite gate 5-2 and 5-5 were at 3-50 % per run and the gate needs a consecutive streak. A 12 h 3f-3 farm moved the gate stage on, but the floor-5 gate (3 in a row, forecast 3-15 %) needed a Restoration deity switch (forecast 33 % vs 15 %) and 4f-3 drops.
2. **I misread `run.py`'s "BOSS OPEN / no gates" twice.** It means the boss is *fightable*, not dead. At D1 I changed destination too early (a 409), and at D4 I looped 5 more times before planning the boss (`boss.sh`). About 0.4 game days and 30 calls lost in total.
3. **D6 boss: farm + `d6eval.sh` + hpopt.** I farmed 2 x 12 h at 4f-3 (tier 6 from 990 to 1,877), applied the breaker plan I3 (3 x item 6309) and then the `mdopt` plan. The route died at 5f-2 / 5f-4 from HP attrition, not at the boss; the boss fell on the 4th attempt after the `mdopt` plan (route forecast 4 % to 10 %).
4. **D7 was the big loss: 6.5 d vs ClaudeItem's 4.2 d.** I arrived at Lv27 (7 %) and then farmed 3f-3 (the best EXP and tier-7 depth by the emulator) for 4.3 d to reach Lv30 at 11.4 d. EXP per 12 h was only 36 % of a level at Lv27-28 and about 25 % at Lv29. Live runs were ~47 per 12 h against ~73 forecast (the sortie charge stock is 3 at 2,520 s loops; k=6 is capped by the stock).
5. **Lv30 slot again decided D7.** After the re-plan (plan C: 99 % at gate 4, 27 % at gate 5) the elite gate 5 and boss gate fell in 3 loops groups (~1.5 d), and with `fire:3` proxyopt weights the boss route went 6 % to 24 % in the forecast. The boss fell on the first batch after that (the plan file name I typed was wrong, so the old plan actually fought it).
6. **D8: 3.5 d.** Gate 2 took 2 d at ~55 % per run (needs 6 in a row; progress reached 5/6 twice). A thunder-weighted proxyopt (`fire:0.5,ice:0.5,thunder:3`) lifted the forecast from 12 % to 52 %. Gates 3 to 5 and the boss gate then fell in two 12 h batches. Boss-only kills were 100 %; route reach was 20 %, and the boss fell on the second batch (6 sorties).

## Useful tips for another AI

1. `run.py` "BOSS OPEN" = the elite gates are done and the next gate shown is `bossGate` (needs 2) and then `entryGate`/`godEntry`. When `entryGate`/`godEntry` (or `godEntry` alone at D8) is shown, the boss is fightable; when `godGate` appears, it has been killed. Run `boss.sh` at that point and verify with the Global Diary (`read/observation/diary?partyNumber=0`, `diary.global.bossFirstClear`).
2. Check the forecast of **every element weight** on the gate you are stuck at (`proxyopt` with fire-, ice- and thunder-heavy weights, then `lever.mjs f`). It was worth 6 % to 24 % at D7 (fire:3) and 12 % to 52 % at D8 (thunder:3) for 5 calls. `plan.sh` default weights (1,1,1,1) miss this.
3. Cheap deity lever: `lever.mjs ... "rest|Goddess of Restoration|"` found 15 % to 33 % at D5. Setting a deity held by another party gives `409 deity_in_use` (set that party to `none` first). Deity ids are lowercase (`restoration`, `fertility`, `mirage`).
4. A party at HP 0 gets `party_exhausted` on a sortie until the elapsed rest restores it; `run.py` skips those using `controls.sortie` and `currentHp`.
5. The sortie charge stock is the limit: 2,520 s loops give ~3 sorties each; `cad.mjs` says 360 s to 5,040 s loops give the same EXP per 12 h, so use 2,520 s (36 calls per 12 h).
6. `cad.mjs` now prints `xp/12h` (patched in this run; the number is `level x 1e6 + exp` delta, so compare policies only). It over-forecasts runs (~73 vs live ~47 per 12 h) and EXP.
7. For D7, EXP farm depth by emulator: 3f-3 (xp 108-114k) > 3f-4 (90k) > 4f-3 (87k). Do not farm deeper: defeats (0 EXP) win.

## Opportunities for future runs

- **Skip the D5/D6 gate grinds by arriving with a better plan.** ClaudeItem reached D6 at 2.96 d with the same recipe, so the early game is luck-heavy; a GA run per gate at ~12 h would help less than the weight sweep in tip 2.
- **Difficulty Offset (untested here).** After a boss is first cleared that dungeon can be farmed at a `difficultyOffset` of +2, +4, ... (Spec 8.3: +enemy level, extra item and super-rare tickets). D6 +6 (enemy level 35 ~ D7's 34) with the D6 gear might give D7-like EXP and more drops with a lower defeat rate; I did not try it. Worth one emulator check (`cad.mjs` has no offset parameter yet).
- **Level before D7.** Lv30 took 4.3 d of D7 time. Farming D6 at 4f-3 gave about the same EXP per 12 h as D7 3f-3 in practice; the real fix is a higher arrival level (kills at D6 with `all` depth do not give much).
- **Calls:** 753 elapsed + 724 sortie + 321 build + 203 observation + 33 export + 17 changeExpedition. Obs every 6 loops and `plan.sh` only at stalls would save ~60 calls; my D8 loops had no skip logic hits (the party mostly sorties at HP >= 60 %).

## Bugs and improvement requests

No game bug blocked the run.

1. **Spec/API doc drift (documentation).** `Specification_9.1.3_API.md` 2-5-1 `diarySetting` still lists `bossThreshold`; the API returns `epicThreshold` since build 32 (ClaudeItem already saw this).
2. **Gate vocabulary is confusing for clients.** The expedition observation shows `bossGate` for the 2-run pre-boss stage, `entryGate`+`godEntry` when the boss itself is open, and `godGate` once it is dead. A `bossAvailable` / `bossDefeated` field on the party would remove the guesswork that cost me two mistakes (and made `run.py` misleading).
3. **Draw is expensive and silent.** Room 5f-3 at D7 is a `draw` ~50 % of the time (an enemy the party cannot kill in time, with a magical self-damage line `BOSS->...:magical:none` in the log). A hint in the room forecast (`simulationRun`) that the room is a draw wall would save planning effort.
4. Repeats from earlier reports: `maximumHp` and `level/experienceRatio` (added in build 34) worked and were used by `run.py`; a multi-character equip commit would still cut the ~320 build calls.
5. `t.py` hard-codes the account name via `BOKEMO_PLAY_USER`; not a bug, but `signup` should be an argument in the shared tool.
