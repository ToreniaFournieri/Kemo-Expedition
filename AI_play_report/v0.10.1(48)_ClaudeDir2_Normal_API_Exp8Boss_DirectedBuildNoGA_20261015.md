## Concept result: how useful is directedBuild (without the GA)?

134 `directedBuild` calls (all 200), 133 `applyBuildChanges`, 306 min real in total, plus 415 tunings (75 accepted).

| Direction / objective | calls | verdicts |
|---|---:|---|
| `free` / `success` | 116 | 31 veryGood, 18 good, **65 noisy**, 2 worse |
| `free` / `bossDamage` | 13 | 8 veryGood, 1 good, 3 noisy, 1 worse |
| turtle / balance / meleeBurst / rapidRanged / magicBurst (success, D6, same seeds) | 5 | all `worse` (-8 to -12 pt), `forcedRecomposition true` |

What it is good at (class layer, 1-2 calls, 2-250 s real):
1. **Arrival re-plan.** One `free` call took the route at a new dungeon from 71 to 99.4 % (D2), 69 to 98 % (D3), 88 to 96 % (D5, Lv30). This is the best use: it replaces the GA at arrival.
2. **Boss damage prep.** `free` / `bossDamage` raised the boss-only share 0.42 to 1.0 (D1), 0.26 to 0.84 (D2), 0.77 to 0.99 (D3), 0.64 to 0.98 (D4), 0.44 to 0.97 (D6). Reliable, 80-200 s.
3. **`objectiveGuard` solves the route-vs-boss conflict** that every earlier run hit with the GA. D3: boss build (kill 92 %, route 0 %) then `free` `success` with guard `{"bossDamage":0.6}`: route 0 to 7.8 %, boss share stayed 0.87; with a tuning, route 22 % and the boss fell on the 3rd sortie. D6 Lv32: guard 0.9 gave route 3 % with boss kill 31 % and the boss fell.
4. `applyBuildChanges` with `basis` + `verdict` works; 0 failures.

What it cannot do:
1. **No equipment search.** Step 1-3 only change class/race/order/deity/lineage/predisposition and call the game's auto-equipment. At the walls (D4 elites, D5 boss gate at Lv25, D6 boss gate) the result was `noisy` (4.2 to 4.2 % at TB 480, pop 64, gen 200, `allTargets`, 377 s). The old GA's equipment/jewel moves (+13 to +47 pt at the same walls) have no replacement. `autoEquipmentScope allTargets` did not help.
2. **Fixed directions** (turtle/balance/meleeBurst/rapidRanged/magicBurst) are always `worse` once the party is already tuned: they force a recomposition. Only `free` is useful.
3. 65 of 116 `free` `success` searches were `noisy` (nothing applied, 3-5 min real each): in the gate loop (re-plan each 3-8 observations) most of the real time went to searches that changed nothing.

**Best recipe found (the hill-climb):** alternate `stagedEquipmentTuning` on ALL 6 characters (`4,5,2,3,1,6`, apply accepted) with `directedBuild free success` (apply good/veryGood), repeat while it gains.
D6 boss gate (Lv31): route 12 % to 19.7 % (tuning) to 25 % (tuning) to 44 % (directed) to 51 % (tuning + directed) in about 5 minutes real and 20 calls; the gate then opened within 12 sorties after 200 sorties of failing loops. Tuning of characters **1 and 6** (lord / guardian) was the biggest source of gains (defense element, 0.54 to 0.77 boss share at D3).
This is the poor-man's GA, and it works in minutes, but it was found only at call 3,290. Starting it at the first wall would have saved most of D4-D6.

## Useful tips for another AI

1. **Do not run the gate loop with a fixed re-plan period.** Re-plan when a wall is detected (same gate floor for 2-3 observations), and use `rp.py` = tuning(all 6) then `directedBuild free` then tuning then `directedBuild free`, stop when the rate gains < 2 pt. (Script in `client/rp.py`.)
2. **Boss prep (`client/boss2.py`, no GA):** tuning `bossDamage` (all 6) then `directedBuild free bossDamage` (high, TB 200), then `free success` with `objectiveGuard {"bossDamage": 0.6-0.9}`, then tuning `success`. Attack only when the success meter is >= 3-5 %. D4 and D6 fell on the first / within 60 sorties this way.
3. **Never attack at route 0 %** (my `full2.sh` did at D3). `full3.sh` only attacks after `READY`.
4. At the opening, the hand opening (9 calls) with `free` bossDamage at the D1 boss gave 0.38 d / 86 calls (Tune5 0.30 / 69).
5. **Farm for levels earlier when a boss gate has a streak requirement** (2-streak at D5/D6 needs route >= ~30 %). Lv30 turned D5 gate route from 3 % to 96 % (depth all). Probe depth (`4f-3` 87 % / 1,574 EXP, `5f-3` 35 % / 1,956, `5f-4` 35 % / 2,025 at Lv25) and farm `5f-3`.
6. Valid tuning ids are 1-6 (all six); 1 and 6 matter.
7. `directedBuild` seeds: pass `seed` and `verifySeed` to compare directions fairly (`verification.before` is then identical).

## Bugs, concerns, suggestions

No game bug blocked the run; no non-200 requests except one expected `invalid_request` (depth `6f-3` is not a valid `depthLimit` value, my mistake).

1. **`directedBuild` has no equipment search** (concern): the 9.1.3 text says the direction can only remove candidates `free` would consider, but nothing says equipment is not searched (only `autoEquipmentRun`). Suggest stating it in 2-3-3, or adding an optional `equipment` component (the GA's real strength) so one call can replace tuning + GA.
2. **`noisy` is the default outcome at walls** (65/116) and `verification` alone does not say "converged". Suggest a field such as `converged: true` / `evaluationsWithoutImprovement` so a client can stop re-planning.
3. **Boss-gate streak requirement is invisible to the simulation.** `simulationRun` reports `blockedByGate {floorRoom 6f-4, current 0, required 2}` but `success` is the single-run rate. A "streak probability" (rate^required) in the overview would show that 12 % is hopeless.
4. **`hl2` "BOSS OPEN" is ambiguous:** `entryGate N+1` + `godEntry N` appear when the boss is *open*, not defeated; `godGate` is the kill signal. Same as earlier reports; a `bossOpen` / `bossDefeated` field in `observation/expedition` would help.
5. **Real time:** directed `high` with TB 480 / pop 64 took 377 s for nothing at the D5 wall; `effort high` default `timeBudgetSeconds` 480 is too long for repeated re-plans. 100-200 s was enough every time it worked.
6. **`directedBuild` `success` at route 0 % is scored by `progress`** (as the GA): the verdict can be `veryGood` (progress 0.34 to 0.74) while the route stays 0 %. Reported again; `crossCheck` shows the other objective but the headline hides it.
7. Repeat: `verification.before` differs per call without a fixed `seed` / `verifySeed`.
