## What decided the run

1. **D6 boss: `a.illusion` vs `a.illusion-breaker`.** The boss race (Procyonian) voids the first ranged attack — one attacker's
   whole volley. Tier-6 arrow **#6309 鉄礫** has `a.illusion-breaker` (we owned 5). One copy on Sota: boss-only damage 0.50 → 0.61;
   one copy on each of the 3 ranged attackers: 0.73. A breaker only helps when its holder is the first ranged attacker, so put one on
   every ranged attacker. `tools/itemswap.mjs` tests an ability item in every slot.
2. **Farm the depth before the first elite you cannot beat.** D6 2F-3 (skip the 2F-4 elite): 654 tier-6/12 h at 1% Defeat vs 453 at
   2F-4 and 288 with gate attempts. D7 3F-3 + survival plan: 1,029 tier-7/12 h. When gear catches up, full depth gives about the same
   drops and also advances gates (D6 gates 3-5 fell in one 12 h farm at full depth).
3. **Check the boss-gate rate in the exact emulator, then use a route plan for the gate and a boss plan for the boss.** I spent
   21 game hours grinding the D6 boss gate on forecast numbers: 9 h with a GA route plan (forecast 18 %, live 1 success in ~30 runs)
   and 12 h with the boss plan (forecast 18 %, live 4/42, emulator 5 %). After more drops, a proxyopt attacker plan (7 calls) checked
   in the exact emulator (~25 %/run) opened the gate in 4.5 h, and the 4-call breaker plan killed the boss 36 min later.
4. **Lv30 unlocks an equipment slot.** At D7 a fresh plan right after Lv30 jumped gate 5 from 23 % to 73 %, the boss gate from 5 % to 54 %
   and the boss from 0 % to 81 % boss-only; D7 fell in the next 2 h.
5. **Cadence from the exact emulator (`client/cad.mjs`).** A sortie wipes the carried cycle progress, so a loop shorter than the natural
   cycle loses natural runs. D5: 2,520 s ≈ 1,080 s in runs at half the calls; D6/D7 farms: 1,080 s best; D8 (cycle ~18-25 min): 1,800 s
   (51 runs/12 h vs 42 at 1,080 s).
6. **Deities.** Fertility (move first +1) was the best route deity from D5 on, and also the best farming deity. Fortification helped D4
   gates only (it doubles rest time). Discord's extra rewards scale with deity rank (ours was 0), so it was not useful.

## Useful tips for another AI

1. Build the dist, twin (`twin/build.mjs`) and emulator (`tools/extract_autoequip.py` + `build2.mjs`) from HEAD before signup.
2. Loop = `client/run.py S 6 n obs [stopOpen]`: elapsed S, then sortie 6 (the server runs what the charge allows) only when
   `controls.sortie.available` **and** `currentHp ≥ HPF × maxHp` (`HPF` 0.95 during gate streaks, 0.5-0.6 while farming).
   The API reports `available: true` at 1 HP and the sortie fights from current HP.
3. At each stall: export → `plan.sh` / `p8.sh` (proxyopt + hpopt + GA) → apply only real gains. Re-plan every 12 game hours of a wall,
   and immediately after Lv20 / Lv30 slot unlocks (`read/observation/party` gives level and EXP %).
4. Before a long grind, confirm the success rate with `client/gatecheck.mjs` (exact API emulator, 2+ seeds). The forecast `lever`
   numbers on one fixed seed were off by up to ~3x (both directions) at the D6 boss gate.
5. Gate streak math: a k-streak at p needs about (1-p^k)/((1-p)p^k) runs. Below that, farm at the best depth and re-plan.
6. Deity moves: a party unlocked later can hold the deity you want (PT3 Fertility, PT4 Fortification). Set that party's deity to
   `none` first (1 call); `read/build/party/{p}` lists the valid deity IDs.
7. Boss-specific counters worth checking in `Specification_3.2` and `1.1`: `a.illusion-breaker` (#6309) vs Procyonian `a.illusion`.
   No `a.magic-seal` counter exists for D8; the D8 boss was 96 % boss-only anyway.

## Bugs and improvement requests

No game bug blocked the run. All 2,289 requests returned 200.

1. **`controls.sortie.available` is `true` at very low HP.** At 1 HP the sortie ran and was an instant Defeat (call 125: "hp 1, sortie 1/6 X").
   - Suggestion: add `maximumHp` next to `currentHp` in the elapsed/sortie `parties[]` facts so a client can apply an HP threshold
     without an extra read (I read it from `read/observation/expedition` once per loop script).
2. **`currentHp` stays at the last finished run's value during the rest phase** (e.g. 0 for 18 game minutes after a Defeat, then full).
   This matches the sortie rule ("no live cycle"), but the spec could say so; a `restingUntil`/`nextRunAt` hint would help.
3. **Request: per-party cycle facts in the elapsed response** (`cycleDurationMs`, carried ms). A sortie wipes the carry, and the natural
   cycle grew from ~11 min (D4) to ~25 min (D8 after a Defeat). Knowing it lets a client pick a loop length that does not throw runs away.
4. **Question: illusion-breaker scope.** With the breaker on Sota, the boss's illusion still voided Lop when Lop shot first
   (battle log: `illusion` event on Lop, 0/25 hits, then Sota's attack landed). If a breaker is meant to cancel the illusion for the
   whole party, this is a bug; if it is per-holder, the glossary text could say so.
5. **Repeat requests:** level/EXP in `read/observation/expedition`; a multi-character equip commit (408 build calls).
6. **Tooling (not game):**
   - `wallopt3` GA (`ALGO=ga`) found no improvement over its seed in all 6 late-game runs (D6-D8); proxyopt/hpopt/mdopt plans did the work.
   - `farm.mjs`/`farm2.mjs` can open a gate inside the simulated chunk (boss Clears appear at a locked gate). That is correct behaviour
     but makes "farm" numbers at `all` optimistic; use `cad.mjs` and read the gate state.
