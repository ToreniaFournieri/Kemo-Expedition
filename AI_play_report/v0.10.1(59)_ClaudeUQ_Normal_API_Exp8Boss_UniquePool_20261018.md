Run folder: `play_runs/normal_api_exp8_ClaudeUQ_20261018/` (STRATEGY.md, notes.md, replan.jsonl, logs/, client/). Electron user data `/tmp/bk-uq`, account `ClaudeUQ`.

## Concept
Free the side parties' unique characters as soon as each party unlocks, so PT1's GA can use them:
- After the D3-D7 boss kills, `client/release.py` sets `uniqueSelection: none` on both uniques of the new party (1 read + 2 calls).
- Every PT1 `gaSearch` sends `uniqueCharacter: true` for all six members (new in build 59), plus all other components, global items, all deities, and order.
- The first GAs after a release get one-change seeds: each freed unique on each non-support member (8-10 seeds).
- The rest is the ClaudeSS loop (GA only, E-based re-plans, slot-unlock re-plans, boss recipe on `gateDamage` `gateRoom: boss`). For elite walls I added the build-57 recipe (`gateDamage` on the X-4 elite, then a guarded route GA).

## Unique-character findings
1. **The GA uses the uniques actively.** 12 GA results changed a unique selection, and most were applied. The final PT1 had three uniques (Hagakure guardian/guardian, Mishka lord/lord, Orca alchemist/ranger). Kemo and Laika were both dropped.
2. **Big unique gains:**
   - D4 2f wall: Kemo -> Leonard on member 1, route 0.47 -> 0.88.
   - D5 4f-4 elite GA: Nox on member 5, elite share 0.18 -> 0.999.
   - D8 1f: Nox on member 6, route 0.32 -> 0.83.
   - D7 4f-4 elite: Hagakure on member 6.
3. **Unique seeds work (the first useful seeds so far).** `orca@4` (D4), `mishka@5` (D6), and `sougaha@5` (D7) ended in the best build. That is 3 of 42 unique seeds, but 3 of the 5 releases led to an applied swap through a seed. A seed changes one value and keeps the current gear (build-57 fill), so it starts close to the incumbent. Seeds copied from the record runs' D8 builds still lost (0 of 15; quick score 0.17-0.55 vs 0.61).
4. **The uniques mostly went to the front and support rows** (Leonard, Hagakure, and Mishka as lords/guardians), not to casters as in ClaudeU1. Unique lineages such as `meddlesome_fox` (defender, shield x1.3) and `hidden_grail` (robe x1.3, evasion) suit the front row.
5. **The uniques did not break the level walls.** D5 (Lv24) and D8 (Lv30) fell only at the slot-unlock levels, as in ClaudeSS. Pace before the walls was similar to ClaudeSS, so I cannot separate the uniques' effect from GA noise in one run.

## Other findings
- **Elite recipe (build 57) gave mixed results.** I ran `gateDamage` on X-4 at the entry HP (`startHp`), then a progress GA guarded by >= 0.85 x share, then success GAs guarded by `gateDamage >= 0.95`.
  - D7 4f-4: route 0.21 -> 0.39. Worked.
  - D5 4f-4: route 0.03 -> 0.15. Not enough.
  - D6 5f-4: route 0.05 -> 0.08. The elite GA even released Mishka and dropped the route to 0.4 % before the guarded pass.
- **`gateDamage` share overstates the win rate.** A 4f-4 share of 0.956 was only 61 % wins (26 % Defeat). Guards near 0.95 share are close to saturated.
- **Retreat (HP <= 30 % after a win) was the main loss at D6 and D8** (30-45 % of runs). `directedBuild` free, turtle, and balance (with `uniqueCharacter`) all returned noChange.
- **The route meter swings again (ClaudeSS Finding 6).** At the D8 5f gate, one build metered 18 % (random seed, 300 runs) and 54.7 % (seed 7, 300 runs) a few minutes apart, with no build change in between. The SE is about 2.5 %.
- 90 GA/directed searches used 5.1 h of the 5.3 h real time (GA time is still the limit).

## Tips for another AI
1. Release side-party uniques right after each unlock (2 calls). Send `uniqueCharacter: true` for every PT1 member, and send one-change seeds (`{characterId, uniqueSelection}`) at the next GA.
2. After build 58, use `gateDamage` + `gateRoom: boss` everywhere (meters too). A missed `bossDamage` returns 400, and a script that ignores it skips the boss GA.
3. Expect the D5 Lv24 and D8 Lv30 walls. Re-plan right after the unlock. Do not spend long GAs before it (D5: 6 searches, D8: 4 searches, all noChange or small).
4. To beat the D8 Lv30 wall sooner, reach Lv30 before D8, e.g. farm D7 5f-3 with a surviving build (ClaudeTune3).
5. Check elite rooms with `simulationRun` `gateDamage` + `startHp` = the `hpAtEntry` from the route detail. Read the Success % there, not only the share.
6. Meter a gate twice with different seeds before a big decision.

## Bugs / suggestions
- No game-side errors. The 6 non-200 calls were mine: 5 `bossDamage` after build 58, and 1 unknown member `bottleneckEnemies` (400).
- **Duplicate names after release (still present, ClaudeU1 #1).** After release, each converted character keeps the unique's name: PT2 101 "Leonard", PT5 402 "Sougaha", and so on. When the GA released Sougaha from PT1 member 5, it became a non-unique Procyonian still named "Sougaha", so two non-unique "Sougaha"s exist. Suggestion: give a converted character a default name (Spec 8.2.3).
- **Suggestion: report the gate win rate.** `crossCheck`/`gateDamage` results show only the share. Also returning the room's win rate (Success %) would stop a 0.95 share from looking like a solved elite.
- **Suggestion: unique seeding inside `gaSearch`.** Unique options appear only through mutation, and my one-change seeds found the useful swaps. The search could add one-change unique candidates for newly available uniques automatically.
- **Question: route meter swings** (18 % vs 54.7 % on one build, see above). Is some state (HP carry, condition) read differently between calls?
