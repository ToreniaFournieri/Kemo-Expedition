## Notices (bugs, inconvenient behavior, and suggested improvements)

### Bugs

* **B3 (High, still reproduces): FULL auto-equipment can leave casters with no attacks.** I switched everyone to FULL at Lv13. After the next 12h block, Selfin and ライカ both had magical NoA −1 (`mag222x-1`, `mag220x-1`), so they had no attacks for part of that block. At the following level-up (Lv14) FULL re-ran and restored NoA 5. The simulation still showed 100%, so the loss was hidden by the other characters. FULL still needs to keep at least one usable attack.
* **B4 (Low): `attacks[].available` is still `true` when NoA ≤ 0 (A6 is not fixed).**
  * Kemo reports `ranged 45 ×0` as available.
  * Kemo reported `melee 280 ×0` as available after I replaced his last gauntlet.
  * Selfin and ライカ reported NoA −1 as available during the B3 period.

### API issues and suggested improvements

* A16: App-side `invalid_request` still gives a stringified Error in `details.reason`. Example: `equipmentEvaluation` without parameters returned `"reason": "Error: invalid_request:targetItems"`. The field is named, but the reason is not readable.
* A17: `equip` with a wrong item string returned "`targetEquipment` is invalid (type)", but the underlying issue was `pattern` on element 0. The message should report the most specific rule, and ideally the expected pattern.
* A18: The control lease expires after 5 idle minutes. After that, every request, including `logOut`, returns 401 `login_required`. An agent that pauses to read documentation loses its session. Options: a longer TTL, a lease-renew endpoint, or `leaseExpiresAt` in every response.
* A19 (A13 is not fixed): `unreadDiaryTitle` text is still percent-encoded, and its timestamps are JST with no offset marker.
* A20: `equip` takes one item per call when `targetSlot` is used. A batch form such as `[{slot, item}]` would cut round trips. A full re-gear here took about 10 commits per character.

### Game and guide observations

* G5: Items with multiplicative defense (1313, `x2/3`) stack and dominate early defense. With two 1313 on the front line, the floor-3/4 danger was mostly solved. This is worth a line in the guide.
* G6: Magic defense matters much more against high-multiplier casters (Falco ×4.53, Kingfisher ×4.31), because it is subtracted before the multiplier. One `M.DEF +12/13` robe per character was the best single change for 4f-4.
* G7 (G2 persists): Gold stops growing when the party enters a new dungeon. For 24h in dungeon 2, Gold stayed at 254,718, because none of the new tier-2 variants had been sold, so none were auto-sold. The inventory reached 197 variants.
* G8: The over-level EXP penalty is steep. In dungeon 1, 4f-4 fell from 12.3 to 6.5 EXP per run at Lv10 → 11. In dungeon 2, 2f-4 fell from 170 to 43 per run at Lv15 → 16. `expectedPerRun` makes this visible, and the practical rule is to keep pushing to the deepest depth that stays above about 80%.
* G9: Clearing the dungeon-1 boss cost a large amount of condition in one block (great/251 → poor/−269), from the Retreat and Defeat results on floor 5. Condition then recovered to "great" within about 2 days of safe dungeon-2 runs.
* G10: FULL auto did not use tier-2 items when first switched on at Lv13; it kept tier-1 +5/+6 gear. It began mixing in tier-2 items (2101, 2102, 2111, 2304, 2108) only after later level-ups and drops. This is reasonable, since the power values are similar.
