## Tips for another AI
1. Never attack at route 0 %. Check the `success` meter first (cost me 6 sorties at D1).
2. When boss prep says NOTREADY, repeat it with more passes (`PASSES=5`, `ABTB=240`, `G=0.6`): at D5 this lifted route 2.3 % to 13.7 % and the boss died in 6 sorties. At D6 the same trick did not work at Lv27.
3. Marginal route (>= 0.5 %) is worth attacking anyway (D4 killed in 11 sorties).
4. Do not wait: at the first wall (D5 elite gate, Lv24) go farm with a surviving build (Tune3 recipe, D5 5f-4 to Lv27, D6 to Lv30). My six wall re-plans at Lv24 returned noChange/noisy and cost ~3 d; the D6 boss likely needs Lv30 (earlier runs needed it).
5. Run long waits as background `until` loops on log files; leading `sleep` and `timeout` are unavailable on this macOS setup.

## Bugs / concerns
- `gaSearch` verdict `noChange` and `directedBuild` `noisy` are indistinguishable for deciding whether to keep searching; a "search exhausted" / confidence hint would help.
- A `bossDamage` build can reduce the route to exactly 0 %, and the success meter then gives no gradient; `objectiveGuard` helps but only after the route is already positive.
- directedBuild has no equipment search of its own by default (autoEquipmentRun FULL in build 49 helps but its gain was still lower than GA on equipment-heavy objectives).
- Tool friction: no API bug caused data loss; no 5xx seen. Stale-log matching in my own `wait.sh` caused two misread waits (client-side issue, not game).

## Verdict
Hybrid GA-first with directedBuild for arrival/class changes is best, but the dominant cost was not choosing the search tool: it was grinding walls at too low a level. Next attempt: Tune3 recipe plus early farming, A/B only at arrivals.
