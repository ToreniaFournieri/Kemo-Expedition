## Balance reading
1. **Three tiers are clear.** Free (100%): squander, exercise, AFK, savings. Middle (47–83%): sleeping, embezzlement, donation, healing, boss-rare, losers. Nearly impossible: **treasure-super-rare 3% (1/35)**, **consecutive-wins 12% (4/34)**, poor-kid 36%.
2. **Build 25 vs the 300-day run (build 22)**: sleeping 43% → 69%, healing 38% → 48%, poor-kid 45% → 36%, embezzlement 55% → 47%, boss-rare 38% → 76% (20 h limit), consecutive-wins 9% → 12%, super-rare 2% → 3%. Boss-rare and sleeping clearly improved from the new limits; the two hardest did not move.
3. **Gold quests are void late-game.** squander/savings/AFK/exercise never fail (35/35, 35/35, 35/35, 33/33). Targets scale with dungeon level but not with wealth (parties hold 100k–400k gold). Scale them by banked gold or raise lv4 multipliers.
4. **Fix candidates**: super-rare: accept a rare drop or give 96 h; consecutive-wins: count Return as a win or lower the 5–20 base; losers 54% depends on whether the party loses at all (PT5/PT6 at easy depth win nearly always) — `depthLimit all` at an unbeatable depth makes it trivial.
5. **PT1, the played party, completes least among active parties** (55% vs 65–82%) because it fights at D8 where wins/drops are harder; this is the intended level scaling.

## Bugs / concerns
1. **PT4 received zero quests in 397 days.** It stays on "4th Elite gate 0/4" (Lv25, auto party) the whole time: an auto party that cannot clear its gate never rolls (spec 5.1.2), and no player action makes it clear. PT5 only got 28 quests (vs 137 for PT6), probably gated for most of the run (not checked per day). Either let side parties re-roll gears/destination, or roll while gated.
2. `compact.sideQuest` still does not say why a party has no quest (gated vs unlucky).
3. `cancelled` stayed 0 across 365 days because God Battles only occur at gate openings; the stat cannot show cancellations unless parties open gates while on a quest. The expiry/cancel split works.
4. 1,460 steps and 4,000 reads took 4 real minutes without error; no performance issue seen.
