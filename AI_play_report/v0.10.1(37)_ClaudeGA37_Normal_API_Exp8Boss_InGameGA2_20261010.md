**Result: I defeated the Expedition 8 boss at 17.75 game days, using 3,448 calls (3,455 in total).**

- This is 10.6 days faster than the first in-game GA run (ClaudeGA, build 35: 28.37 d, 3,614 calls). It is still 5.1 days slower than the record (ClaudeItem, build 33: 12.67 d, 1,786 calls), which used offline twin tools and healing sorties.
- First Clear: 2026-10-28T08:20:12Z (Global Diary `bossFirstClear`, PT1, dungeon 8). The game started at 2026-10-10T14:20:08Z.
- Account: fresh `prod`/`normal` account `ClaudeGA37`. I used the official API only, on an Electron session built from HEAD (build 37).
- Real time: about 4.5 h, within the 8 h limit. `gaSearch` took 207 min of it (146 searches).
- 3,453 of 3,455 requests returned 200. The other two were 400s from my own bad parameters (depth `6f-3`, category `i.arrow`). There was no 503: I kept every search at or under 90 s.
- Evidence is in the [run folder](../play_runs/normal_api_exp8_ClaudeGA37_20261010/):
  - `STRATEGY.md` (written before signup) and `notes.md` (findings N1 to N20, written during the run).

  - `logs/`, `final.kemoz.gz` and `client/`.

## What worked

1. **`considerItemsScope: global` fixes ClaudeGA's biggest problem.**
   - 34 of 146 results took items from PT2 to PT5 (25 different donor characters). `applyGaResult` applied all of them without an error or a stale result.
   - I never had to strip PT2 or PT3 by hand.
2. **Search all components together.** Use equipment, jewels, main and sub class, lineage, predisposition and race in one search.
   - At the D4 boss, the split passes (build only, then gear only) returned `noChange` and 1.2 %.
   - Three full passes went 0 → 5.8 → 7.6 → 34.6 %, and the boss died in the next loop.
3. **Restarting from a manual rebuild escapes the GA's local optimum.**
   - At D7 and D8 the GA had turned a ninja/ranger into an armor tank, so only 2 characters dealt damage. Many rooms ended in a Draw (timeout).
   - I re-geared that one character by hand as an attacker of the right element (3 calls). Then:
     - D7: the route to the boss went 11 → 24 % after one search;
     - D8 gate 3: 19.8 → 37 % with the rebuild alone, then 51 % after one search.
4. **Pinning an item by freezing its owner** (`equipment: false` on that character) works as a lock.
   - At D6 I put two 6309 illusion-breaker arrows on the two rangers and froze them. The forecasts went:
     - boss-damage share 42 → 67 %;
     - success 0 → 9.8 %.
   - The boss died on the 18th attempt.
5. **The `simulationRun` per-room table finds the wall room**, for example the D6 5f-4 elite or the D8 2f-1/2f-2 normal rooms. Together with `character/status` (attacks, `e.*`) it shows why the GA is stuck.

## Useful tips for another AI

1. **Build-36 loop (`client/hl.py`).**
   - API `elapsed` only processes whole cycles, and each cycle ends with a full heal. After a sortie that lost HP, the party stays at that HP until one full cycle has passed (about 17-30 min).
   - So:
     - elapse about one cycle;
     - top up 90 s at a time until HP is full;
     - sortie at full HP at gates;
     - add extra sorties only at 85 % HP or more.
   - When a sortie lost no HP, just wait 6 min for the next charge.
   - Fortification doubles the rest time, so it makes the loop longer.
2. **Search defaults.** Use `considerItemsScope: global`, all components, `considerOrderChange` and `considerDeityChange`, `effort: high` and `timeBudgetSeconds: 90`.
   - Re-search when the gate floor changes, and every 4-6 observations on the same gate.
   - Do not use "stall = identical observation" as the trigger: a streak that keeps moving never looks identical.
3. **Free the deities that new parties take automatically.** I set PT3 and PT4 to `none`, because `considerDeityChange` only offers deities that no other party holds.
4. **When the search says `noChange` and success is 0 %:**
   - Read the per-room table and every attacker's element. Element is the argmax of the summed `e.*` over all worn items.
   - Rebuild one character by hand: `removeAllEquipment` plus an `equip` array, plus `changeBuild` if needed.
   - Then search again.
5. **Element facts:**
   - D7 Origami have `a.thunder-null` and `r.fire x1.5`, and Undead have `r.fire x1.5`.
   - D8 Voidspawn have `r.thunder x1.3` and fire/ice x2/3, and Ghosts have `r.ice x1.5`.
6. **D6 boss (#315).**
   - It is Procyonian, so `a.illusion` cancels our first ranged attack.
   - Put 6309 on the rangers and freeze them, and reach Lv30 (one more equipment slot).
7. **Farm depths:** use a `minDefeat` search at each depth first.
   - D4: 4f-4 (92 %, 1 % Defeat).
   - D6: 3f-4, then 5f-3 for EXP.
   - D7: 4f-3 (88 %).
   - D8: 3f-3 (85 %, 3 % Defeat).

## Bugs and concerns

1. **Bug: `gaSearch` never fills a slot past the character's stored equipment array.**
   - **Cause.** The current genome is built from `character.equipment.map(...)` (`src/api/v1/gaSearch.ts`, around line 333). The stored array can be shorter than `maxEquipSlots`; `readModels.ts`, around line 1001, says so. Mutation `replace` picks `slot < slots.length`, and crossover maps over the child's array. Only the two heuristic seeds use `maxSlots`, and their extra gene is lost in crossover.
   - **Effect.** Character 2's new Lv30 slot stayed empty through 8 or more full searches. Filling it by hand raised the D6 boss-damage share from 42 to 57 %. ClaudeGA's "guardian kept one empty slot" is probably the same bug.
   - **Fix.** Pad `current.equipment` to `maxEquipSlots`, and pad again when a build gene changes the slot count.
2. **Concern (build 36): the API and the UI heal differently after a sortie.**
   - In the UI, `state.rest` heals max(200, 2 %) per 15 s Step, so a UI player can sortie again within minutes.
   - In the API, `elapsed` carries sub-cycle time and heals only when a whole cycle completes, and a sortie wipes the carry. An API party therefore waits a full cycle (which includes one natural run) for every heal.
   - The `elapsed` response does not expose the carried time or the cycle length. That cost about 350 top-up calls.
   - Suggestion: apply partial rest healing to the carried time, or return `carriedSeconds` and `secondsToNextCycle` per party.
3. **Concern: the GA cannot reach some good builds.**
   - It does not change team roles or elements. One-slot mutations cannot flip a character's element (several coordinated swaps are needed), and they cannot turn a tank back into an attacker.
   - It does not pick situational ability items. 6309 has low base power and pays off only in one boss room.
   - Suggestions:
     - multi-start, or "re-gear character X as role/element Y" macro mutations;
     - an element-aware heuristic seed built from the destination's race weaknesses;
     - a `fixedItems` option (keep item K on character C). `lockEquipment` requires FULL mode, so it cannot be used for this today.
4. **Concern: `bossDamage` is still not boss-only.**
   - The code opens the gates and runs the full route (`prepareObjectiveState`), so the route decides the result. At the D6 boss it reported a 2-6 % share while about 80 % of runs died before the boss.
   - Spec 9.1.3 says it "simulates the boss room". Please align the text, or add a boss-room objective from full HP or from the expected arrival HP.
5. **Concern: `considerDeityChange` silently excludes deities that other parties hold.**
   - New parties take deities automatically (PT2 Cunning, PT3 Fertility, PT4 Fortification).
   - Suggestion: offer them with a "release from PT n" step in `changeSummary`, or report the excluded deities in the result.
6. **Not on main: the 120 s bridge timeout for `gaSearch`.**
   - The 360 s timeout from ClaudeGA's branch commit `4f9e2391` (`desktop/main.cjs`) is not on main. `effort: high` with its default budget of 180 s still returns 503.
   - Budgets of 90 s or less are safe; a total of 104 s was observed with a 100 s budget.
7. **Minor:**
   - `effort: medium` ends after its 40 generations in about 15 s and leaves most of its 60 s budget unused.
   - A same-seed A/B of the scopes on one state (medium) gave: `normal` +6.8 pt, `withinTargets` noChange, `global` noChange. One sample proves nothing, but a bigger pool does not by itself give a better result within one short search.
