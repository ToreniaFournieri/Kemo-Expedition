## Concept
"GA test, serious run". gaSearch is the only search tool, with no tuning and no directedBuild. The run tests **when and how big** to run a GA. Each re-plan measures the route (`simulationRun` 300 runs) and computes the expected runs to finish the gate streak, `E = (1-p^k)/((1-p)p^k)`:
- `E - k <= 3`: no search.
- Otherwise a `high` GA. If `E - k` is still above 12, a big GA (64 x 200, 300 s), at most 2 per re-plan. From D4 on, `E - k > 45` goes straight to the big GA.
- Re-plans happen on arrival, on a new gate, on a slot-unlock level (14/19/24/30; added during the run), and after a stall budget of 12-24 loops.

The loop is GP's (one Cycle elapsed + one sortie). Client: `client/replan.py`, `hl4.py`, `boss4.py`, `chain.sh`.

## GA usefulness (106 searches, 5.9 h of the 6.2 h real time)
- `high`: 18 searches; 11 veryGood, 4 good, 2 noisy, 1 noChange. Mean applied gain +36 pt. 82 s average.
- Big (64 x 200): 88 searches; 35 veryGood, 14 good, 24 noisy, 15 noChange. Mean applied gain +26 pt. 224 s average.
- **Big GA is not a silver bullet.** It breaks some walls (D4 2f 0 -> 53 %, D6 3f 14 -> 66 %), but 44 % of big searches gave nothing. The real time goes almost entirely to search, and it was the reason this run took 6 h.
- **Low-p selection noise is real.** Below about 10 % route success, success-objective searches were mostly noisy or noChange (D5 boss gate 7.4 -> 7.4 %, D7 boss gate noChange twice). `quickRuns 300 / confirmRuns 1000` helped once (D5 boss gate 3.8 -> 22 %).
- **Finding 1, progress as the gate objective at low p.** On a gate route with success < 15 %, the first big GA used `objective: progress` (no guard), and I re-metered success afterwards. Results:

  | Gate | Success before | Success after |
  |---|---|---|
  | D7 boss gate | 6.7 % | 14 %, then 18 % |
  | D8 1f | 14 % | 66 % |
  | D8 2f | 0.7 % | 11 % |
  | D8 4f | 0 % | 32 % |

  In all, 6 of 8 were good or veryGood. The gate opened soon after in each of those cases, except D8 2f (a level wall). Progress is the smooth signal the success objective lacks.
- **Finding 2, boss prep.** The D6 recipe of unguarded bossDamage -> success guarded by bossDamage failed.
  - The bossDamage build killed the boss 63 % of the time in boss-only runs. But it died on the route: 9 % defeat in 1f-1, ~37 % lost in 2f, 7 % reaching the boss. The guarded success searches could not climb from 0.3-1.8 %.
  - The recipe that worked at D7: **unguarded bossDamage GA** (boss-only kills 94 %, progress 0.26), then **progress GA with objectiveGuard bossDamage >= 0.8** (progress 0.25 -> 0.90, route 0.4 -> 11 %). The boss died in 5 loops.

## Concerns
1. **Guarded searches often return noChange.** It happened 4 times:
   - progress / guard bossDamage 0.85 and 0.6 (D6);
   - bossDamage / guard progress 0.5 (D6);
   - bossDamage / guard progress 0.69 (D7).

   Each had about 5,000 evaluations. In the 0.6 case, the very next unguarded progress search found a build that met that guard (bossDamage 0.62, progress 0.26 -> 0.70). The guard seems to filter only at confirmation, so the search spends its budget on the unguarded objective and then discards everything. Suggestion: rank by the guard during the search too (a penalty or a constrained tournament), or say in the response how many confirmed candidates failed the guard.
2. Big GAs often return well before `timeBudgetSeconds` (bossDamage 75-150 s, success 140-300 s) and without 64 x 200 = 12,800 evaluations. There is no `stopReason`, so you cannot tell convergence, time, or a hidden limit apart (GP asked for this too).
3. levelProbe: 86 probes, `levelNeeded` null in 73. It was right that levels do not help most walls (D5 5f: Lv24 18.7 % vs 20.5 % now). But it read the D8 2f wall as hopeless, and Lv30 (8th slot) then opened 2f and 3f within about 1 d. It is still "does a level help?", not "should I farm?".

## Tips for another AI
1. D1-D5: skip a search when `E - k <= 3` and re-plan on every new gate; about 820 calls to the D5 boss.
2. Re-plan when a slot opens (Lv14/19/24/30). D4 5f: two big GAs at Lv18 reached 15 %, then one `high` GA at Lv20 reached 76 %.
3. On a gate with success < 15 %, run one big GA with `objective: progress` first, then a success GA.
4. Boss: unguarded bossDamage GA, then progress GA guarded by bossDamage >= 0.85 x the new share. Attack at a route of 1 % or more; natural Cycle runs kill bosses too. Never keep a pure bossDamage build for the attack.
5. Budget real time: a big GA costs 4-5 min. This run spent 5.9 h in search.

## Opportunities
- With the D7 boss recipe at D6 (2.4 d lost) and progress-first at the D7 boss gate (about 1 d), this run would have been at about 10.5 d. Use both from the start.
- Low-p gates and bosses need an objective with signal. A built-in `gate` objective scored by `progress` when `p` is tiny, or a `progress -> success` two-stage mode in gaSearch, would save the client logic.
- Use `high` where `E` is moderate. Big GA should be kept for real walls, because real time, not calls, was the limit.
