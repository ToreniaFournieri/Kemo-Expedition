## Summary

The opening guide was written for the retired experimental API, so its steps were mapped onto `/api/v1`: `changeBuild` (simulate, then commit with `confirmation: "yes"`), `commit/build/party/1`, `changeExpedition`, `purchaseShopItems`, per-character `autoEquipment`/`removeAllEquipment`/`equip`.

* Opening: the builds and equipment from the guide applied as written. The shop had a sword, wand and grimoire (60G each), which rolled +3, +1 and +2. The first 1,000-run simulation at 1f-3: room 1 100%, room 2 82%, room 3 37%, no defeats. The diagnostic sortie drew at room 3.
* An API account has no live cycle, so it progresses with `elapsed` blocks. One 1h block gave +545 XP. After that, 3h, 6h and 12h blocks were used.
* **Dungeon 1** was cleared at party level 10 (depth `all`, sim 10.6% clear per run).
* **Dungeon 2** was cleared at level 20–21, and **dungeon 3** at level 25 (the first `all` block with a 1.8% sim clear rate).
* Dungeon 4: all elite gates are open. The next gate is `bossGate` 0/2. The current wall is the 5f-4 elite (sim: 16% defeat).

## Key findings

* **FULL Auto Equipment beats the guide's manual loadout after about level 10.** At level 14 in dungeon 2, switching all six characters from the guide's SEMI and manual gear to FULL raised the 2f-3 simulation from 8.6% to 80.0% success. SEMI never fills slots gained from levels. From that point FULL was kept on everyone.
* Selling a variant marks it `s.sold`, and later drops of it are auto-sold in `state.sell`. Selling the low-enhancement common and uncommon stacks once is what turned the gold income on. Before that, gold sat at 20G while stacks capped at 99.
* The shop only stocks the five tier-1 commons. Three paid refreshes (200, 400 and 800G) gave the same lineup each time.
* Two Gods Battles in dungeon 1 (at levels 18 and 21) both ended in a Draw.
* XP per 12h dropped sharply at shallow depth. Moving to the deepest depth that still simulates about 60% or better per room was the most productive.

## Issues found

1. **`GET read/expedition/{p}/latestBattleLog` returns 500 `internal_error`.** It fails with and without `logId=diary:<id>`. The desktop log shows the response fails its own catalog schema: `/battleLog/rooms/N/endEvents/0` (`const`, `minItems`, `maxItems`, `additionalItems`, `anyOf`), `/battleLog/rooms/N/eventFormat` (`const`) and `/battleLog/rooms/N/events/*` (`type`). It happened from the first sortie on, so bottleneck analysis and Gods Battle review were not possible.
2. `read/build/character/{id}/equipmentEvaluation`: a `targetItems` value without a Jewel (`0/1302/1/0`) or with `0:0` is `invalid_request`. Only a real Jewel (`/might:1`) works, which also adds that Jewel's effect. Items that cannot take the chosen Jewel type come back as `"The requested projection is unavailable."` (`invalid_request`). Section 9.1.4.9 documents `0:0` only for `equipmentChanges`, but a no-Jewel form for `targetItems` would make item comparison possible.
3. The bestiary (`resources/bestiary`) lists no god entries, and its stats are unscaled base values. Without the battle log (issue 1), the Gods Battle cannot be diagnosed through the API.
