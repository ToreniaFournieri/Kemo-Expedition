## Tips for another AI run

1. **Clock and runs.** `commit/progress/elapsed` (max 43,200 s, 1 call) is the only thing that moves the clock. At Lv39 on D8 one step ran about 21–26 auto-expeditions and gave about 77k EXP. Sorties cost 1 call per run and give no clock time, so they are poor value once auto runs are plentiful.
2. **Do not over-use `simulationRun`.** It is a READ with body `{}` (use a raw POST; the commit envelope returns 400), it counts as a call, and 100 runs cannot measure a 1–7% event such as a boss clear. Use it to compare big changes (gear, deity), not to tune small ones.
3. **Skip routine observations.** Batch several steps, then read `observation/expedition` once; `clearGates` (kind, floor, current/required) shows gate progress. Avoid reading the full party after every step.
4. **Gates.** Elite gates for 1F–5F need 7/6/5/4/3 consecutive Clear/Return runs through X-3; the boss gate needs 2 through 6-3. Draw/Retreat/Defeat reset the streak and gates never relock. Farm with a `depthLimit` that matches the next gate (a deeper limit that you cannot survive resets the streak).
5. **Gear.**
   - Match element to the enemy: D8 Voidspawn resist is fire/ice 0.67, thunder 1.3. A character's attack element is the argmax of its item element bonuses, and thunder wins ties.
   - FULL auto-equip ignores element; fix with manual `equip` (one item per call, `targetSlot` required; an array without it only fills free slots).
   - Only tier 6+ thunder items helped; tier 3 thunder catalysts, bolts and arrows collapsed NoA (catalyst NoA is additive per tier).
   - Stripping PT2–PT6 and consolidating gear into PT1 gave +10–25% offense.
   - `equip` and `removeEquipment` switch FULL to SEMI.
6. **Deity.** Restoration (heals 20% of missing HP every 4th room) is best for D8 floors 5F–6F; fortification (physical x2/3) was better for passing floors 1–4 gates; attrition and mirage raise damage but cost sustain. Deities are unique per party.
7. **Boss tuning (hypotheses, only partly tested).**
   - Capture a real boss log early with one sortie plus `latestBattleLog`; it exposed the shock/seal pattern for 2 calls.
   - Make the shock victim cheap: equip an `a.boost` sword (item 4406, boss rare, tier 4) on the weaker melee character so it moves first about 89% of the time. This is what I did on Kemo; `a.null-shock` exists only on item 6402 and the machina race; I found no way to obtain either, so I did not use it.
   - Keep the best melee/ranged characters in rows 1–3, magic casters behind, but physical threat weight is 16/8/4/2/1/1 by row, so moving a fragile character to row 1 costs survival on every run.
   - Level 40 needs about 316k more EXP at 77k/step, roughly 4 steps.
8. **Where time was lost (untested ideas).** Lv28–39 took 4.5 months. Possible savings: reach the thunder/consolidated-gear setup earlier, jump to the highest sustainable destination sooner, and avoid long `all`-depth steps near gate unlocks (defeats reset streaks and waste steps).
9. **API gotchas.**
   - Control lease lasts about 15 minutes: on 401 `control_lease_expired`, call `fundamental/logIn` again to get a fresh session and lease.
   - Commits need `expectedRevision` and a fresh `idempotencyKey`; on `stale_revision`, retry with `currentRevision` (1 extra call).
   - `changeBuild` requires `simulation: false, confirmation: 'yes'`.
   - A deity already used by another party returns `deity_in_use`; free it first.
   - Item strings are `lock/itemId/plus/superRare[...]`; equipped items add a slot prefix and a jewel suffix; in `searchItems` the 5th field is quantity (free) or owner character (equipped).
   - Do not logOut mid-run if the Electron profile will be reused.
