## Concept result: directedBuild

56 `directedBuild` calls (all 200), 32 applied. Total real time 1,055 s. Per setting:

| Direction / objective / effort | calls | average gain (pt) | verdicts |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| `rapidRanged` / `success` / medium | 8 | +10.9 | 4 veryGood, 2 noisy, 2 worse |
| `rapidRanged` / `bossDamage` / medium | 9 | +9.1 | 4 veryGood, 3 good, 2 worse |
| `magicBurst` / `success` / medium | 7 | -5.0 | 3 veryGood, 4 worse |
| `magicBurst` / `bossDamage` / medium | 2 | +1.5 | 1 good, 1 worse |
| **`free` / `success` / high (unique, lineage, predisposition changeable, all deities)** | 29 | **+10.7** | 14 veryGood, 7 good, 8 noisy |

1. **Opening.** One call (`directedBuild rapidRanged`, 2 s, 22 changes) lifted the D1 route forecast from 84.4 % to 99.4 %, and one `applyBuildChanges` call applied it (10 calls instead of the 9-call hand opening plus a simulation). It was still **worse in play**: the D1 boss opened at call 112 (hand opening 63) and fell at 0.47 d / 123 calls (0.30 d / 69). The route forecast covers only the first gate floor, so the 99 % did not show the elite-gate deaths at 1f-4 and 1f-5 (9 Defeats, 14 Retreats in 48 sorties).
2. **`free` is the useful direction.** It found large class-layer gains at walls, where the GA and the tuning had stalled:
   - D5 gate 3: 41.8 to 55.2 % (and an earlier result 41 to 69 %);
   - D5 gate 5 (progress): 65 to 76 %;
   - D7 boss gate: 9 to 55 %;
   - D6 farm depth 5f-3: 4 to 20 % (then GA 23 to 70 %);
   - D6 and D7 route recovery at Lv30: progress 0.66 to 0.81 and success 0.2 to 3 %.
   The `high` effort run took 20-52 s (not budget-bound: 0 of 56 calls hit `timeBudgetSeconds`). Its steps used all three parts: class, race and order in Step 1, deity in Step 2 (only when `considerDeityChange` was `allDeities`/`unoccupiedDeities`), lineage and predisposition in Step 3 (e.g. `4: lineage oath`, `6: predisposition nimble`). It also swapped unique characters (`kemo`, `laika`) in and out.
3. **The fixed directions rarely beat the current build.** At gates, `rapidRanged`/`magicBurst` returned `noisy` or `worse` (-4 to -82 pt) against a GA `veryGood` of +6 to +18, because they hold the composition and the GA had already tuned the equipment. Only `rapidRanged` `bossDamage` at a boss was reliably good (+14, +14, +28 pt) before the big `bossDamage` GA. I dropped them from the gate loop from D4 on (saves 2 calls and about 10 s per re-plan).
4. **Class layer first, then equipment.** The best order at every wall was `directedBuild free` (class, order, deity, lineage), then tuning, then the big GA. The GA after a `free` apply still gained +17 to +47 pt each time.
5. **`applyBuildChanges` works as specified.** `changes` + `basis` + `verdict` in one call; 105 applies, 1 rejected. The rejection was `409 illegal_action` with `reason: build_basis_stale`, the correct result: my own loop re-plan had changed the build after the `free` search (D5, call 780). The stale search was safe to discard; I paused the loop, searched again and applied.
6. **Objective trap, again.** `directedBuild` with `success` at a route of exactly 0 % is scored by `progress`, like `gaSearch`. It was good for the route at Lv30 (D6, D7), but only after the `bossDamage` GA's boss build had been checked: see the Tips.

## Useful tips for another AI

1. **Class-layer re-plan at every wall.** Run `directedBuild` direction `free`, `effort high`, `considerDeityChange allDeities` (it is cheaper to leave `none` when no other party exists, but it never failed), `considerItemsScope global`, `objective success`, with `uniqueCharacter`, `raceGender`, `mainClass`, `subClass`, `lineage`, `predisposition` all `true`. Apply `veryGood`/`good` with `applyBuildChanges`; then tuning (4,2,3,5), then the big GA. Skip `magicBurst`.
2. **Do not use `directedBuild` for the opening.** The hand opening (lord/lord, guardian/guardian, 3 x ninja/ranger, alchemist/wizard, Mirage) still gives the faster D1 (0.30 d / 69 calls). A fair next test: start with the hand opening and run `free` only from D2.
3. **Never attack at a route of exactly 0 %.** Both D6 and D7 wasted 30 sorties (about 0.9 d and 120 calls) because `boss.sh` raised the boss-only kill to 86-98 % with the `bossDamage` GA, which left the route at 0 % (1f-2f deaths, 81 % Defeat). Check `simulationRun` `success` after the boss GA and attack only when the route is at least 5-10 % and boss-only at least 45 %. `full2.sh` now has the guard.
4. **Get the route above 0 % before the boss prep, and recover with a route-first re-plan.** At D6 Lv30 the route-first `free` + GA took route 0 to 38 % with the boss-only kill 49 % (no `bossDamage` GA was needed). At D7: `free` (success 0.2 to 3 %) then big GA 1 to 18 % kept boss-only at 77 %. Both bosses fell on the first or second sortie.
5. **D6: farm to Lv30 first.** Boss open at Lv25 and Lv28 both gave route 0 %. Lv30 (8th slot) was the breakpoint again: route 0 to 38 % with boss-only 49 %. The 5f-3 farm took Lv26 to Lv30 in 276 runs / 3.7 d. A `success` GA at 5f-3 (plus `free`) gives 70 % at Lv26-28.
6. **D8: re-plan every 6 observations (NST=6, one `free` + tuning + big GA per re-plan).** Gates 1-5 and the boss gate took 4.5 days with Lv30 to 32 and no farm. The GAs were `veryGood` (+13 to +18) at gates 2, 3, 4 and noChange once at gate 5.
7. **Heal exactly** (`hl2.py`: one `elapsed` of `max(60, secondsToNextCycle)` per loop, then a sortie). Heal misses: about 12 in 1,121 `elapsed` calls, mostly right after a reprice.
8. **Valid `tuneCids`:** tune only characters 1-6 of PT1 (my `tune.py 7,8` returned 404 twice).
9. **Tuning:** 213 tunings, 34 accepted (16 %), 1-10 s per call, so it is cheap next to the GA (54 GAs, 187 min real, 95 % of the real time).

## Bugs, concerns, suggestions

No game bug blocked the run. Of 2,950 requests, 4 were 404 (my own: `status` on the party, an invalid diary path, `stagedEquipmentTuning` for characters 7 and 8) and 1 was the correct `build_basis_stale` 409.

1. **`directedBuild` forecast at the opening is misleading.** `verdict veryGood`, 84.4 to 99.4 %, but the D1 clear was slower than the hand opening. `forecast` covers the route to the first gate only. Suggestion: document it, or return the `progress` score next to `success`, so a client can see that 99 % at the first gate says nothing about the elites.
2. **Fixed directions are only useful for `bossDamage`.** 9 of 16 fixed-direction `success` searches were `noisy`/`worse`. Suggestion: say in 9.1.3 that `free` is the general search and the other directions only constrain it; or report (in `composition`) the best distance-0 candidate that would otherwise win, so the client can see what the constraint cost.
3. **The route-vs-boss conflict is still not visible** (repeat of Tune2-5): a `bossDamage` search leaves the route at 0 % and a `progress`-scored `success` search then reverts the boss build. `directedBuild` and `gaSearch` verdicts judge one objective only. Suggestion: `verification` could return the other objective's before/after (boss-only share when objective is `success`, route when `bossDamage`) for information.
4. **`verification.before` changes between calls on the same save** (e.g. 0.41 / 0.392 / 0.418 at D5 gate 3) because every call uses a new verification seed; a single `veryGood` is then +13 to +28 pt over a `before` that is +/- 2 pt. Fine, but a client comparing two searches should pass the same `seed`.
5. **Deity Step 2 is cheap and sometimes decisive:** 13 candidates, about a second; it chose `fertility`, `attrition` or `fortification` in 8 of the high-effort runs. Not a bug; a note for tips only.
6. **Tuning and directed share the 404 `not_found` for characters of unknown ids** with the message "The requested projection is unavailable." Suggestion: say which parameter (`characterId`) is invalid, as `directedBuild` does for `targets.characterId`.
7. **Repeat: `secondsToNextCycle` after a reprice** is stale for one `elapsed` (about 12 heal misses in 1,121 `elapsed` calls, mostly after `changeExpedition` or an apply). Returning the recalculated value in the commit response would remove them.
