## Mechanics worth knowing (from specs, source and logs)

1. **Damage is `(raw attack − defense x (1 − penetration)) x amplifier x element`.** Defense is subtracted from the raw item attack before the amplifier. A weak-hitting character against pdef 963 loses over half of its attack; adding raw attack or penetration helps far more than linearly.
2. **Enemy attack thresholds.** `max(1, attack − defense)` means a character with pdef above the enemy attack is nearly immune. Ninja R677 x46 (first strike) and sage M641 x37 are harmless against pdef/mdef above those numbers and lethal against naked characters. When I made every character glass, the ninja alone took 17k HP in one strike.
3. **Offense is defense.** The initiative dice decide who acts first (rolled across all attack types). Dealers acting before an enemy kill it before it hits; Fertility +1 and Command made this work. Glass cannons paid off only because Kuz (row 2, sturdy) and the Kemo front line absorbed the rest.
4. **Distinct `c.*` bonuses stack.** Each distinct value of `c.ranged-attack`, `c.magical-attack`, `c.physical-attack` (jewel), `c.penet`, `c.accuracy` counts once and they sum. Low-tier common arrows and wands carry +16% to +23% each, so a cheap tier-3 arrow can be worth more than a high-tier armor slot. FULL auto-equip ignores this and fills armor/robe slots by class order.
5. **Accuracy decay.** Hit chance per hit is `potency x decay^(n−1)`; a decay of 0.90 makes extra attempts beyond about 15 nearly worthless, except for alchemists (Arcane Stability sets a 55% floor, so NoA stays linear for them).
6. **HP is quadratic in vitality.** Base HP is `L_eff x vit x (vit + mind) / 20 x growth`, so concentrating vitality items on one low-value character beats spreading them. HP is a shared pool, so the battery does not need to be in the front row.
7. **Retreat** (party HP at or below 30% after a won room) ended 40–55% of all runs in the simulations, which is why HP matters more than the Defeat column suggests.
8. Gravity Well (catalyst 7320) did not help: 40% of remaining HP at the caster's turn equals her normal damage because the ranged characters act first.

## Tips for another AI run

1. **Look for unequipped Super Rare items early**: `read/base/searchItems state=owned superRare=true`. FULL auto equip never picks them. `equip` with `remainsMode: true` and a `targetSlot` keeps FULL and the item is protected from replacement.
2. **Use Fertility from the start of D8 gates**, not Restoration. Check Mirage again only when the elite is unkillable and survival is fine.
3. **Do not use one-by-one equips.** `equip` with an array and no `targetSlot` fills free slots, but one unowned item makes the whole call fail with 409 `illegal_action:item_not_owned`. `removeEquipment` with `targetEquipment: [slots]` then one array `equip` is 2 calls per character.
4. **Jewels**: `removeEquipment` and `removeAllEquipment` return jewels to inventory; `jewelAttach {targetEquipment: slot, jewelToSet: "might:2"}` is 1 call per jewel. Only distinct ranks stack on one character.
5. **Class change** (`changeBuild` with `mainClassId`, `subClassId`, `simulation:false`, `confirmation:"yes"`) works for non-unique characters. Predisposition and lineage changes on unique characters (Kemo, Laika) return 409 `unique_character_immutable`.
6. **Battle logs of defeats** come from `read/expedition/{p}/latestBattleLog?logId=diary:<id>` (the `diaryEntry` endpoint returns only metadata). They are the cheapest way to see which enemy kills you.
7. `simulationRun` takes at most 1,000 runs and is seeded by the revision, so any build change reshuffles the noise (about ±1.5 points for a 40% rate). Compare big changes only.
8. Gates: a `Return` counts only if room X-3 was cleared; streaks reset on Draw/Retreat/Defeat. After the boss gate opens the boss is just another room of the run; the boss (HP 166k) was far easier than the 3F elite (264k).
9. Where a faster run could save time: equip the Super Rare items and move to the glass-cannon/Command/vitality-battery layout before Lv36; the first 83 days (D1–D7) were not re-optimised in this run.
