## How the build-45 changes did

### `secondsToNextCycle` (exact heal): works, the biggest call saver

- The loop (`hl2.py`) sends one `elapsed` of `max(60, secondsToNextCycle)` per loop, then sorties. The party is at full HP after it, as specified.
- 8 heal misses in about 1,000 `elapsed` calls. Every miss came right after a reprice (`changeExpedition`, or an `applyBuildResult` that changed the build), where the client still held the old value.
- There were no 90 s top-up loops and no HP-learning code. ClaudeTune3 spent about 30 % of its `elapsed` calls on top-ups.

## Bugs, concerns, suggestions

No game bug blocked the run. Apart from the container restart (item 5), every request returned 200.

1. **`secondsToNextCycle` goes stale after a reprice, and no response returns the new value.**
   - `changeExpedition`, `applyBuildResult` (a build change) and deity changes reprice the Cycle, as 9.1.4 says.
   - None of those responses returns the new `secondsToNextCycle`, and `read/observation/expedition` does not carry it either.
   - Result: 8 heal misses, one extra `elapsed` each.
   - Suggestion: add `carriedSeconds` / `secondsToNextCycle` to the party entries of `read/observation/expedition` and to the `applyBuildResult` / `changeExpedition` / `commit/build/party` responses.
2. **Winner's curse in tuning steps 2-5 still decides most rejections.**
   - __S5REJ__ tunings kept a candidate in step 5 that step 6 then rejected.
   - Example (D6 gate 3, char 2): 6309 `a.illusion-breaker` scored +32 % on quick runs and +40 % in step 5, then +2 pt (under 2 SE) in step 6.
   - Example (char 3, same tuning): step 5 kept +22 %, step 6 measured -2.4 pt.
   - Suggestion: score step 5 with `baselineRuns` on a fresh seed, or qualify step 2-4 candidates by paired difference against SE (ClaudeTune3 concern 3).
3. **The progress verdict has a cliff at one success.**
   - At the D4 boss route, `before` 0 and `after` 0.002 (1 success in 500) switched `scoredBy` back to `success`, and the verdict was `noisy` (gain 0.2 ± 0.2 pt).
   - With both builds near 0, a single lucky run decides which score is used.
   - Suggestion: use `progress` while both success rates are under about 1 % (or within 2 SE of 0), not only at exactly 0.
4. **The route GA and the boss GA overwrite each other.**
   - At the D7 boss, the progress-scored `success` GA (0.23 → 0.76, `veryGood`) took the boss share from 0.62 back to 0.37 (100 % Draw).
   - At D2 the success passes also cut boss-only kill from 84 % to 32 %.
   - Suggestion (repeat of ClaudeTune2 item 6): a `minBossDamage` / `minSuccess` constraint, or a combined objective (route success × boss kill).
5. **Container restart and `logIn` returning 503.**
   - The cloud container restarted once (real 00:50 UTC) during a boss re-plan. Electron was restarted on the same `userData`, and the save had every commit up to the last one.
   - The first `logIn` right after `PLAY_SESSION_READY` returned 503 `runtime_unavailable` for about 10 s.
   - Suggestion: write the descriptor only after the renderer is ready, or return `Retry-After`.
   - Kept `buildResultId`s were gone after the restart (as specified).
6. **Valid `depthLimit` values are still undocumented.** `5f-1` returned 400 `invalid_request`, while `4f-3`, `5f-3` and `5f-4` were accepted (repeat of the ClaudeTune2 concern).
7. **New parties start in FULL mode with a deity and take drops.**
   - Party 2 (holding Cunning) and Party 3 appeared during D4-D5, and the GA kept taking items from them (donors 103, 105, 202, 204, 206, 302, 303).
   - Tuning cannot take those items, because it uses only the Inventory and the character's own items.
   - Suggestion: an API option to create new parties OFF and without a deity, or a `considerItemsScope` for tuning.
8. **My own tool bugs** (not the game):
   - `hl2.py` did not seed the gate key from the first observation, so the first gate change after a loop start waited for the stall trigger (D4 gate 2 waited 12 loops).
   - A `pkill -f` pattern killed my own shell.
