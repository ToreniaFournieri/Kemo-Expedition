## How the build-45 changes did

### `secondsToNextCycle` (exact heal): works, the biggest call saver

- The loop (`hl2.py`) sends one `elapsed` of `max(60, secondsToNextCycle)` per loop, then sorties. The party is at full HP after it, as specified.
- Heal misses were 8 to 15 in 1,362 `elapsed` calls (at most 1.1 %). The 8 I inspected all came right after a reprice (`changeExpedition`, or an `applyBuildResult` that changed the build), where the client still held the old value.
- Short `elapsed` calls (150 s or less) were 13 of 1,362 (1 %). ClaudeTune3 had 304 of 1,013 (30 %), its 90 s top-ups. No HP-learning code is needed any more.

### `stagedEquipmentTuning` diagnostics (`candidatesTried`, `bottleneckCounters`)

- **`candidatesTried` explains the empty D1 tunings.** At D1 it was `{0, 0, 0}` and `a.true-sight` was `noItem`: tier-1 items carry no element, resistance or ability. One diagnostic call was enough, so D1 tuning was skipped.

## Useful tips for another AI

1. **Heal exactly.** After each sortie, read `secondsToNextCycle` from the `sortie` (or `elapsed`) response and send one `elapsed` of `max(60, secondsToNextCycle)`. The party is at full HP after it.
   - Keep the value across loop restarts (`hl2.py` stores it in the token-state file).
   - After `changeExpedition` or `applyBuildResult` the value is stale, so expect one extra `elapsed`.
2. **Real time is decided by `gaSearch`, not by game days.** Loops cost about 1 real minute per game day; a GA costs 2.5-5 minutes.
   - When real time is short, grind a streak gate with no re-plan instead of re-running the GA.
   - The D7 boss gate opened after 1 game day of grinding (1 real minute), after four GAs had moved it nowhere (1 → 3 %, 4 → 4 %, 11 % `noChange`) in about 11 real minutes.
3. **Measure the route before re-planning.**
   - `arrive.sh` costs 2 calls: `changeExpedition`, then `simulationRun` 300. It re-plans only if the route is under 85 %. D5, D6, D7 and D8 all started above 85 % without a re-plan.
4. **Tuning first is cheap and still pays.** About 2-10 s and one call per attacker; about 1 in 5 tunings is accepted, often a resistance or element switch.
   - Stop after the first rejected tuning when the route is already at or above 95 %. The 7 % relative bar rarely passes there.
5. **Counters:** read `bottleneckCounters`.
   - `noItem` means farming the dungeon that drops the counter, not more tuning.
   - At the D6 boss the counter (6309 `a.illusion-breaker`) went into the build only once there was a free slot: right after Lv30 (8th slot) the tuning took 6309 x2 with a fire switch, +22.5 pt boss share.
6. **Farm on a route build, at the depth `probe.py` picks.** `probe.py` costs 2 calls per depth (success, EXP/run, drops/run). Retreats still pay EXP.
   - D3: 4f-3, 83 %, about 1,200 EXP.
   - D6: 5f-3, 25-79 % after re-plans, about 2,150 EXP. Lv27 → 30 in about 2.7 game days.
   - D7: 5f-3, 54 %, about 2,870 EXP and 17.6 drops. Three game days took 2 real minutes and raised the D7 boss kill from 0 to 76 % after one boss re-plan.
   - D5 is a poor farm: about 1,000 EXP/run.
7. **GA settings for gates:** `quickRuns` 100 instead of 300 gives about 2.5x the evaluations in the same budget (median 860 evaluations at 300 runs / 300 s; 2,248 at 100 runs / 300 s; 1,612 at 100 runs / 240 s).
   - Use `considerDeityChange: allDeities` once Party 2/3 exist, because they hold deities.
   - Free new parties early (`freept.py P`: 7 calls).
8. **Bosses:**
   - Run the `bossDamage` GA first, then one `success` GA.
   - If boss-only is at least 45 % and the route at least 10 %, attack without a further re-plan (D5: first sortie).
   - If boss-only kill is near 0 after the `bossDamage` GA, farm drops and EXP first: D3, D6 and D7 all needed a farm before the boss fell.


## Opportunities for future runs

- **Gear walls cost the most game time:**
  - D3 boss: route attrition, 0.37 d lost against ClaudeTune3.
  - D5 gate 3 and gate 5: 1.2 d.
  - D6 gate 3: about 1 d.
  - D6 boss at Lv27: needed Lv30, about 2.7 d.
  - D7 boss: about 3 d of farming.
  - ClaudeTune3 met fewer walls with similar tools, so item luck and an early farm with high EXP (D4 5f-3 and D6 5f-4) look decisive. A D4 farm before D5 (as ClaudeTune3 did) would likely have saved D5's 1.2 d.
- **Farm for Lv30 at D6 before the D6 boss.** The D6 boss became easy at Lv30 (8th slot), and the same EXP is needed for D7. Farming D6 5f-3 to Lv30 straight after the D6 gates, then D6 boss, then D7, avoids two separate farms.
- **Real time:** GA passes were about 87 % of API real time (304 of about 350 minutes).
  - Cut the GA budget to 150 s at gates.
  - Skip stall re-plans when two consecutive GAs return `noChange` or `noisy`.
- **Calls:** short `elapsed` top-ups fell from 30 % to 1 % of `elapsed` calls. The remaining overhead is tuning (about 4 calls per re-plan) and observation reads (every 6 loops).


## Bugs, concerns, suggestions

No game bug blocked the run. Apart from the container restart (item 5), every request returned 200.

1. **`secondsToNextCycle` goes stale after a reprice, and no response returns the new value.**
   - `changeExpedition`, `applyBuildResult` (a build change) and deity changes reprice the Cycle, as 9.1.4 says.
   - None of those responses returns the new `secondsToNextCycle`, and `read/observation/expedition` does not carry it either.
   - Result: 8 to 15 heal misses, one extra `elapsed` each.
   - Suggestion: add `carriedSeconds` / `secondsToNextCycle` to the party entries of `read/observation/expedition` and to the `applyBuildResult` / `changeExpedition` / `commit/build/party` responses.
2. **Winner's curse in tuning steps 2-5 still decides most rejections.**
   - 79 of 274 tunings kept a candidate in step 5 that step 6 then rejected.
   - Example (D6 gate 3, char 2): 6309 `a.illusion-breaker` scored +32 % on quick runs and +40 % in step 5, then +2 pt (under 2 SE) in step 6.
   - Example (char 3, same tuning): step 5 kept +22 %, step 6 measured -2.4 pt.
   - Suggestion: score step 5 with `baselineRuns` on a fresh seed, or qualify step 2-4 candidates by paired difference against SE (ClaudeTune3 concern 3).
3. **The progress verdict has a cliff at one success.**
   - At the D4 boss route, `before` 0 and `after` 0.002 (1 success in 500) switched `scoredBy` back to `success`, and the verdict was `noisy` (gain 0.2 ± 0.2 pt).
   - With both builds near 0, a single lucky run decides which score is used.
   - Suggestion: use `progress` while both success rates are under about 1 % (or within 2 SE of 0), not only at exactly 0.
4. **The route GA and the boss GA overwrite each other.**
   - At the D7 boss, the progress-scored `success` GA (0.23 → 0.76, `veryGood`) took the boss share from 0.62 back to 0.37 (100 % Draw).
   - At D2 the success passes also cut boss-only kill from 84 % to 32 %.
   - Suggestion (repeat of ClaudeTune2 item 6): a `minBossDamage` / `minSuccess` constraint, or a combined objective (route success × boss kill).
5. **Container restart and `logIn` returning 503.**
   - The cloud container restarted once (real 00:50 UTC) during a boss re-plan. Electron was restarted on the same `userData`, and the save had every commit up to the last one.
   - The first `logIn` right after `PLAY_SESSION_READY` returned 503 `runtime_unavailable` for about 10 s.
   - Suggestion: write the descriptor only after the renderer is ready, or return `Retry-After`.
   - Kept `buildResultId`s were gone after the restart (as specified).
6. **Valid `depthLimit` values are still undocumented.** `5f-1` returned 400 `invalid_request`, while `4f-3`, `5f-3` and `5f-4` were accepted (repeat of the ClaudeTune2 concern).
7. **New parties start in FULL mode with a deity and take drops.**
   - Parties 2-6 unlock at the D3-D7 boss first clears. Party 2 held Cunning, and the GA kept taking items from them as donors (103, 105, 202, 204, 206, 302, 303, 405).
   - Tuning cannot take those items, because it uses only the Inventory and the character's own items.
   - Suggestion: an API option to create new parties OFF and without a deity, or a `considerItemsScope` for tuning.
8. **My own tool bugs** (not the game):
   - `hl2.py` did not seed the gate key from the first observation, so the first gate change after a loop start waited for the stall trigger (D4 gate 2 waited 12 loops).
   - A `pkill -f` pattern killed my own shell.
