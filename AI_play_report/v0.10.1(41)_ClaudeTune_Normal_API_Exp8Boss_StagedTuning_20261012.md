# ClaudeTune: Normal API Expedition 8 boss clear, v0.10.1 (41), "In-game stagedEquipmentTuning"

**Result: I defeated the Expedition 8 boss at 15.00 game days, using 3,059 API requests.**

- That is 2.75 days faster than the previous in-game-only run (ClaudeGA37, build 37: 17.75 d, 3,448 calls). It is 2.3 days slower than the record (ClaudeItem, build 33: 12.67 d, 1,786 calls), which used offline twin tools.
- First Clear: Global Diary `bossFirstClear` PT1, dungeon 8, at 2026-10-26T21:30:40Z. The game started at 2026-10-11T21:29:45Z.
- Account: fresh `prod`/`normal` account `ClaudeTune`, official API only, Electron started from the repo root on a dist built from HEAD (build 41).
- Real time: about 4.1 h, within the 8 h limit (it includes one pause for a usage limit).
- 3,054 of 3,066 requests returned 200. The 12 others were my own mistakes or expected rejections (listed under "Bugs and concerns").
- Evidence is in the [run folder](../play_runs/normal_api_exp8_ClaudeTune_20261012/):
  - `STRATEGY.md` (written before signup) and `notes.md` (findings T1 to T32, written during the run);
  - `batch_calls.jsonl` (every request), `tune_log.jsonl` (every tuning), `ga_log.jsonl` (every search);
  - `logs/`, `client/`, `tools/` (two offline post-run checks) and `final.kemoz.gz`.

## Concept

- All planning used in-game tools only: the new `read/build/character/stagedEquipmentTuning`, plus `gaSearch`, `applyBuildResult`, `simulationRun` and status reads. No twin, emulator, proxyopt or hpopt was used for any play decision.
- Tuning searches elements, resistances and ability items, but never raw power. So I paired it with a power source:
  - planned: FULL auto-equip as the power refresh;
  - used in practice: `gaSearch` (all components, `global` scope) first, then tuning on each attacker (`client/wall.sh`).
- The loop was GA37's build-36 HP-aware `hl.py`, which re-plans automatically when the gate floor changes or progress stalls.

## How stagedEquipmentTuning did

- **Usage:** 403 tunings, 28 accepted (7 %). By objective, 26 of 327 `success` tunings and 2 of 76 `bossDamage` tunings were accepted.
- **What was kept:** the 28 accepted results kept 21 offense-element switches, 12 resistance changes and 1 ability item.
- **Size:** accepted gains were +7.6 % to +99 % relative (median about +20 %).
- **Speed:** median 1.7 s, at most 9.6 s, about 3,900 simulation runs per call. No timeout.
- **Reproducibility:** the same `seed` on the same revision gave an identical result.
- **Jewels and changeSummary:** a one-element swap applied as 2 to 4 entries (only the changed slots).
- **Where it beat the GA:**
  - D4 gate 2: the GA was stuck at 0 %. A tuning fire switch on char 2 (+99 %) gave 17.6 → 33.8 %.
  - D6 gate 3: the GA returned noChange at 21 %. Two tunings (char 2 thunder +75 %; char 3 `r.none` plus fire +14 %) gave about 42 %.
  - D6 boss: a fire switch on char 2 (+19 %) took the boss share 0.42 → 0.49 between GA passes. The boss then died on the first attempt.
- **Breakers:** with the boss room as the bottleneck, the ability step tried the 6309 illusion-breaker first at the D6 boss (+18 % / +64 % quick). Step 5 did not keep it, so I equipped it by hand.
- **Where it did nothing:**
  - bosses under the `success` objective (0 % gives no signal);
  - forecasts above about 90 % (the relative +7 % bar cannot be met);
  - every time the wanted element existed only on lower-tier items.

## Useful tips for another AI

1. **Re-plan as soon as a gate stalls.** After any `applyBuildResult` the party is SEMI, so new drops are never equipped until the next search or tuning. I lost about 8 game hours at D2 gate 3 this way (one GA took it from 22 % to 97 %).
2. **Re-plan order:** `gaSearch` (all components, `considerItemsScope: global`, effort high, 90 s budget), then `stagedEquipmentTuning` on each attacker. Skip the tuning when the GA forecast is 80 % or more.
3. **Bosses:**
   - Run `bossDamage` GA passes until success is above 0.
   - Then switch to `success` GA passes. At D7, one `success` pass took a 0.68-share / 1.9 % build to 28.8 %.
   - Tuning with `bossDamage` sometimes adds a few points on top.
4. **Gates are consecutive streaks.**
   - Below about 60 % per run, farm instead of grinding.
   - Sortie only at 95 % HP or more: forecasts assume full HP, and a low-HP Retreat resets the streak. My first loops used 60 % for extra sorties, which cost streaks.
5. **Farm depths that worked:**
   - D4: 4f-4.
   - D5: 4f-4.
   - D6: 4f-4, then 5f-3 (2,600 EXP and 20 drops per run). Use a GA `experience` build.
   - D7: 5f-3. From Lv29 9 % to Lv30 took 25 h. Lv30 took the boss share from 0.23 to 0.54 with one GA pass.
   - D8: 3f-3, 36 h in total.
   - Loops: K=6 batched sorties, 2,400 s loops, about 42 calls per 12 h.
6. **Free every new party at unlock.** Send `removeAllEquipment` with empty parameters for each of its 6 characters, then set the party's deity to `none`. Tuning only takes the Inventory and the character's own items.
7. **Exact meter:** a `gaSearch` with `targets:[{characterId:1, changeableComponents:{}}]`, a fixed seed and `verifyRuns:1000` returns the build's exact rate in about 1 s. Use it for deity sweeps and before/after checks.
   - D6 boss share by deity: fertility 0.24, restoration 0.19, mirage 0.16.
   - D8 gate 4 success by deity: mirage 0.37, fertility 0.33.
   - Note: the meter replaces the party's kept search result.
8. **D6 boss (#315):**
   - Arrival HP decides the fight. In the live log the party reached the boss at 36 % HP, and the boss's first magic thunder hit (13,482) is larger than full party HP (11,778).
   - What worked: farm to Lv28, keep 6309 on the rangers, run four bossDamage GA passes (two were `noisy` real gains, applied with confirmation), add a tuning fire switch.
9. **Avoid FULL auto-equip on the GA's hybrid classes.**
   - FULL follows the main class's category order, so an alchemist/ranger or sword-saint/ranger gets melee or robe items.
   - At D6 it took the boss share from 0.24 to 0 (runs died at 1f-4). With a 0 score, neither the GA nor tuning has any signal.
   - FULL is only safe when the main class matches the role (ninja/ranger: D6 gate 3 went 63 → 72 % after a rebuild plus FULL).
10. **Rebuilding a tank as an attacker is situational.** It helped at D6 gate 3. It hurt at the D7 boss (0.29 → 0.15) and at D8 gate 4 (0.37 → 0.26 → GA 0.34). The tank gear was holding the party HP pool. Measure it with the meter first.

## Opportunities for future runs

- **Time:**
  - Start farming the moment a streak gate is under 60 %. D6 and D8 each had about a day of re-plans and grinding before the farming that actually broke them.
  - Use full-HP-only sorties from the first gate.
- **Calls:**
  - 403 tunings for 28 accepts. Tuning only between 15 % and 80 %, and only attackers, would save about 250 calls.
  - Observing every 6 loops instead of every 3 would save about 120 calls.
  - The total to the kill was 968 elapsed, 910 sortie, 403 tuning, 253 observation, 177 `gaSearch` (148 searches and 29 meter calls), 108 apply, 78 manual builds, 31 `simulationRun` and 32 `changeExpedition`.
- **Tool combination:** if tuning had a power step (below), it could replace most GA passes at gates and cost far less real time (2 s against 60-90 s).

## Bugs and concerns

No game bug blocked the run. The rejected requests were:

- 6 × `removeAllEquipment` with a `simulation` field (my error; the operation takes no parameters);
- `category=i.arrow` in `searchItems` (my error; the value is `arrow`);
- `undoEquipment` 409 `equipment_unavailable` (see concern 8);
- an `applyBuildResult` without `confirmation` for a noisy result (my error);
- an `applyBuildResult` 409 after my meter replaced the result (as specified);
- one `stale_revision` that my client retried;
- one `control_lease_expired` after the usage-limit pause.

1. **Spec mismatch: `bossDamage` does not simulate the boss room.**
   - Spec 7.1.5 and 9.1.3 2-3-7 say it "simulates the boss room of the current destination". Tuning and the GA both call `prepareObjectiveState`, which opens every gate and runs the full route.
   - So tuning's bottleneck room, its element order and its breaker priority follow route enemies whenever runs die on the route. Offline on the GA37 D8 save, the bottleneck was 3f-4.
   - Suggestion: add a boss-room-only score (from full HP or from the expected arrival HP), or align the spec text.
2. **Winner's curse in steps 2-4.**
   - Each step records the best of up to about 50 candidates scored on 100 quick runs, so recorded gains are inflated. Examples:
     - D5 gate: +21-27 % quick, then +1-4 % in step 5, then -2 to -11 % in step 6;
     - D4 boss: +38.8 % in step 5, then +9.7 % in step 6.
   - With 100 runs, one success is about 3 % relative, so ties are common. Four different guardian candidates scored exactly +21.86 %. An offline identity check showed this is not a cache bug.
   - Suggestion: default `quickRuns` to 300, or re-score the recorded candidates on a fresh seed before step 5.
3. **Relative thresholds:**
   - `minImprovement` / `minGateImprovement` are relative, so tuning can never accept from about 93.5 % success upward;
   - at 0 % `success` every candidate has ratio 1 (flat).
   - Suggestion: also accept an absolute gain (for example +2 pt with the luck check), and use the boss damage share as a tie-break when `success` is 0.
4. **No raw-power step, and a narrow item pool.**
   - Tuning never upgrades an item to a stronger one of the same element or category.
   - The element switch only replaces items within the same category. At D8 the only thunder item for char 4's ice slot was tier 3 (improvement 0).
   - Tuning never takes items worn by other characters.
   - Suggestions: a power step (strongest item per slot within the current element), category-changing element switches, and a `considerItemsScope` option like `gaSearch` has.
5. **`steps.finalTuning[].improvement` after a swap is relative to the candidate it replaced, not to the current build.**
   - At D6 char 2, finalTuning showed +0.048 while step 6 showed +0.747. That reads like a contradiction.
   - Suggestion: document this, or report both numbers.
6. **`gaSearch` `success` stalls when every run reaches the boss and Draws.**
   - At the D1 boss, the rooms-reached tie-break is flat, so the search returned noChange at 0 %. The `bossDamage` objective gave 0 → 49 % on the same state.
   - The same boss-share tie-break would fix it.
7. **`bossDamage` GA verdicts are often `noisy`.**
   - The share's variance uses the Bernoulli bound p(1-p). At the D6 boss, gains of +4.3 pt (SE 2.6) were real; the boss fell after I applied them.
   - Suggestion: use the per-run share variance.
8. **FULL auto-equip and undo:**
   - `autoEquipment FULL immediate` on chars 2-5 in sequence let the later characters take the items the first one released.
   - The first character's undo then failed with 409 `equipment_unavailable` until the others were undone.
   - This is reasonable, but a party-level undo, or a hint in the error, would help.
9. **Measuring replaces the kept result.** Any `gaSearch`, including a zero-component measurement, invalidates the party's kept result. A read-only measure mode, or a `keepResult: false` flag, would help.
10. **Repeat of GA37 concern 2 (build 36).** The API heals only per whole cycle, and streak gates need full-HP sorties. The last D8 gate loop alone (gate 4 to the boss) spent 34 heal top-up `elapsed` calls. Returning `secondsToNextCycle` per party is still the cheapest fix.
