## Concept and method
Question: does the refined `directedBuild` (b50-52: equipment polish, final selection, margin rule) beat `gaSearch`? Method: paired A/B (same `seed`/`verifySeed`/time budget) at every re-plan (gate-floor change, every 6 observations, boss prep), apply the winner via `applyBuildChanges`. Hand opening (9 calls), tuning first on 6 chars, boss prep = tuning + A/B `bossDamage` then `success` with `objectiveGuard` (PASSES=5, G=0.6), attack only when route >= 0.5 %. Farm to Lv30 at D6 5f-3 before the D6 boss.

## Tips for another AI
1. Reuse the hl2/hl3 loop, `stage.sh`, `boss.sh` (PASSES=5 ABTB=200 G=0.6); D1-D5 took 978 calls / 3.2 d this way.
2. At the D6 wall (bossDamage 17 %, route 29 % at Lv26) go straight to farming: probe depths (`probe.py 6 5f-3 ...`), one success GA, then `hl2.py` K=2 with STOPLV=30. 230 loops = 480 calls, 7.8 d -> Lv30; then the boss fell in 2 prep rounds (gate streak 2).
3. The "bossGate 0/2" entry means two consecutive Clears of the boss route; `godGate` = boss dead (confirm with `read/observation/diary` global `bossFirstClear`).
4. `stage.sh` loop count 200 may end before the boss opens; check for "BOSS OPEN" before running boss prep (see bug below).
5. Never attack at route 0 %; marginal route (>=0.5 %) after PASSES=5 prep was enough at D3 (14 %) and D7 (5 %).

## Opportunities
- Skip the paired A/B at gate re-plans (GA only, tuning first): the A/B search time was ~19,000 s of search (~5.3 h of the 6 h real time, when both are run); GA alone is ~40 %. Real time was not binding, but call count is not the cost: re-plan frequency is.
- Re-plan gate loops cost calls (3 per loop). Use K=2 sorties per loop when HP allows, and start the D6 Lv30 farm at the first D6 boss-gate wall (I lost ~1.7 d and 470 calls in the D6 gate loop at Lv25-26 first).
- Merge `hl3.py` stage stop into `chain.sh` so it only starts boss prep on "BOSS OPEN".

## Bugs / concerns
- **My client bug**: `chain.sh` ran `boss.sh` 3 x 30 sorties after `stage.sh` ended with the D8 elite gate 4/5 still open (the 200-loop cap), wasting 90 sorties (~270 calls, ~1.5 d). Not a game bug.
- Game-side: no 4xx/5xx errors, no data loss. `directedBuild` real time exceeds `timeBudgetSeconds` slightly (e.g. TB 200 -> 198-199 s, fine); `noChange` from both searches is not a "search exhausted" signal (same concern as the AB report): an explicit field would help.
- `bossGate` appears in `clearGates` as a 0/2 streak whose meaning (two consecutive boss-route Clears, not two boss kills) is easy to misread; the observation could say so.
- A `bossDamage` build still drops the route to 0 % at times (D1-D3); `objectiveGuard` fixes it only after the route is positive (same as earlier reports).

## Verdict
Refined directedBuild is now a sound arrival/class-layer tool, honest about noChange, but gaSearch is still better per second and per evaluation for equipment-heavy re-plans. The run was ~6 d slower than the record, from the D6 Lv26 wall (needs earlier farming) and the D8 chain bug.
