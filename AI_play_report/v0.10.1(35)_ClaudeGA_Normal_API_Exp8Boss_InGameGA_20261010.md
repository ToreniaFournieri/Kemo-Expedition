# ClaudeGA: Normal API Expedition 8 boss clear, v0.10.1 (35), "In-game GA research"

**Result: I defeated the Expedition 8 boss at 28.37 game days, using 3,614 calls in total.** This is much slower than the record: ClaudeItem took 12.67 d and 1,786 calls with offline twin tools.

- First Clear: 2026-11-07T17:04:02Z (Global Diary `bossFirstClear`, PT1, dungeon 8). The game started at 2026-10-10T08:12:21Z.
- Fresh `prod`/`normal` account `ClaudeGA`. Official API only, on an Electron session built from HEAD (build 35).
- Real time: about 4.4 h, within the 8 h limit. `gaSearch` itself took 209 min of that (200 searches).
- 3,609 of 3,614 requests returned 200. The other five were 4 × 400 (my bad query formats) and 1 × 503 (bug 1 below).
- Evidence is in the [run folder](../play_runs/normal_api_exp8_ClaudeGA_20261010/):
  - `STRATEGY.md` (written before signup) and `batch_calls.jsonl` (every call).
  - `ga_log.jsonl`: every `gaSearch` with its body, verdict, verification, evaluations and real seconds.
  - `final.kemoz.gz` and `client/` (the scripts).

## Concept

All planning used the new in-game `read/build/party/{p}/gaSearch` and `commit/build/party/{p}/applyGaResult`. I used no twin, emulator, proxyopt or hpopt. The opening, the loop client and the route knowledge came from ClaudeItem.

## What the in-game GA does well

1. **One call per re-plan.** `applyGaResult` applied up to 36 changes in one call. There were 72 applies against 59 manual build calls in the whole run, compared with 391 build calls for ClaudeItem.
2. **Big early gains.**
   - D1: 26% → 99%.
   - D2 gates: 0% → 92%.
   - D3 boss gate: 10% → 86%, once order and deity were included (`considerOrderChange`, `considerDeityChange`).
3. **It fills new slots.** After Lv30, the GA put items into the new slot. SEMI auto-equip had left it empty.
4. **Verdicts are honest.**
   - `noisy` and `worse` verdicts correctly flagged final-selection winners that lost on the verification seed (one `worse`: −5.4 pt).
   - The verdict counts were 88 `noChange`, 41 `good`, 38 `noisy`, 32 `veryGood` and 1 `worse`.

## Useful tips for another AI

1. **Free the gear held by PT2 and PT3 first.** Use `removeAllEquipment` plus `autoEquipment mode:OFF` on 101-106 and 201-206 (24 calls).
   - Their FULL auto-equip takes the best tier-6/7 and epic drops, and `gaSearch` never uses another party's items.
   - At D7 this one change took the boss gate from 6% to 15%, and from 33% to 48% after more passes.
2. **Search settings.**
   - Always pass `considerOrderChange` and `considerDeityChange`.
   - Use `effort: high` with `timeBudgetSeconds` ≤ 110, because of bug 1.
   - `low` effort was too weak after D2.
3. **When success is 0%, get a signal first.**
   - Run `bossDamage` first; this took the D4 boss from 0% to 2% success and 33% damage. Then run `success`, which reached 16.6%.
   - When the full search returns `noChange`, run a build-only pass (`mainClass`/`subClass`/`lineage`/`predisposition`, no equipment). At D7 it found +8.7 pt (ranger and sword-saint) that the full search missed.
   - Then run 2-3 `success` passes. At the D7 boss they went 0.2% → 4% → 11% → 24%, and the boss fell in the next loop.
   - My `client/polish.sh [boss]` runs this sequence.
4. **Use 1-2 sorties per loop at gates, with `HPF` ≈ 0.9.**
   - In a sortie batch, the 2nd and later runs start from the HP the previous run left. The forecast assumes a full-HP start.
   - So with `numberOfSortie: 6`, the extra runs were mostly Retreat or Defeat and reset the streak.
5. **Do not grind a 0% boss.** I lost about 20 h at D5 and 20 h at D6 looping before re-planning.
6. **Re-run the GA after you change the depth limit for farming.** At D8 2f-4, two changes (order and deity) took Defeat from 53% to 8%.
7. **Using FULL auto-equip as a seed did not work.** It gave 0%, and the GA could not climb from it.
   - Reverting needed two Undos per character, because the mode change back to SEMI was its own Undo step.

## Opportunities for future runs

- **The best mix is probably twin-tool plans plus `applyGaResult`-style application.** The in-game GA was weaker than proxyopt plus a seeded GA for D5-D8 walls. It stalled at local optima many times: 88 `noChange` results, even with 4,370 tier-8 items owned.
- **Free the PT2/PT3 gear at signup.** This alone probably explains days of the D5-D7 delay.
- **Calls.** 1,578 elapsed, 1,210 sortie, 423 observations, 201 `gaSearch`, 72 applies, 59 builds, 37 `changeExpedition`, 34 other. Fewer searches (only at gate changes plus one polish) and 2,520 s farm loops would save about 300 calls.

## Bugs and concerns

1. **Bug: `effort: high` always times out.** The preset `timeBudgetSeconds` is 180, and the API allows up to 300. The desktop bridge rejects any renderer request after 120 s (`desktop/main.cjs` `apiV1RendererTimeoutMs`), so the call returns 503 `runtime_unavailable` ("The game authority is unavailable.").
   - Fix: give `gaSearch` a longer timeout (≥ 310 s), or cap the budget below 120 s.
   - Open question: does the renderer keep computing after the 503, and can a late result replace the stored `gaResultId`?
2. **Concern: no way to include other parties' items.** `gaSearch` ignores items equipped by other parties, while their FULL auto-equip takes the best drops. The GA then quietly returns `noChange`.
   - Suggest an option such as `includeOtherParties`, whose `changeSummary` would start with `removeEquipment` on the donors.
   - At least add a warning when better variants are worn by other parties.
3. **Concern: `bossDamage` is not boss-only.** It runs the full route with gates open and from the current state, so route Retreats (≤30% HP) dominate it. For example, D7 showed 0.40 average share with 0 kills.
   - Spec 9.1.3 says it "simulates the boss room", which reads as a boss-room-only fight; 9.1.4 says it runs the route. Align the two texts.
   - Also consider a true boss-room objective at full HP (the twin `bossonly` metric).
4. **Concern: forecast vs live sortie batches.** Forecasts assume a full-HP start, but live batch runs 2..k start from reduced HP. Gate grinds at a 45% forecast looked like 10% live.
   - Suggest documenting this, or giving `gaSearch`/`simulationRun` an option to start from the current HP.
5. **Concern: weak escape from local optima.** The search often returned `noChange` while a narrower search (build-only, or another objective) found improvements. Suggestions:
   - Run several restarts inside one call.
   - Add a heuristic seed that equips the highest-tier or highest-base-power variants.
   - Add per-character coordinate passes.
6. **Minor issues.**
   - `searchItems` defaults to `limit 10` with `truncated:true`, which looks like an empty inventory at first glance.
   - `character/equipment` rejects `characterId=1,2` with a helpful message (repeat the parameter). This is fine.
   - SEMI auto-equip leaves new level slots empty (seen at Lv30).
   - Guardian (char 6) kept one empty slot after every GA. Either no equippable variant existed, or this is a gap in the search.
