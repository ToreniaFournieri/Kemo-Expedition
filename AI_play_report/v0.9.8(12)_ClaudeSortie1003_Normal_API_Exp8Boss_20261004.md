## What worked
1. **Export → exact twin → greedy role builder → apply diffs.** One export gives the exact party, inventory, jewels, gates and deity rank. The twin matched the live `simulationRun` (D8 4F: 0.7% twin vs 1.2% live; D8 5F: 30% vs 29%).
2. **A greedy role builder beats random local search.** Coordinate ascent on stat proxies (offense for attackers, log(PD)+log(MD)+log(party HP) for tanks), followed by a greedy jewel pass, made almost every big gain. The 4-seed random local search mostly fitted noise, even with second-seed verification.
3. **Big single levers found by the twin:**
   - D2 boss: rebuild 0% → kills inside 12 h (day 2.5 vs about 5.5 in earlier runs).
   - D4: 12 h at depth `1f-3` for T4 drops took the 1F elite from 0% to 65%.
   - D6 boss (103k HP, Illusion): **one Illusion Breaker arrow (6309) per sniper** took kills from 0% to 1.3% per run; it died within 12 h.
   - D7 5F: the jewel pass took the gate from 9% to 35%.
   - D8: **vitality armor on the tanks** (HP 10.5k → 17–22k), **Laika pilgrim/guardian** for the 3F magic elite (4F gate 12% → 23%), **Selfin ninja/wizard/nimble** (5F gate 6% → 12%), then **Laika back to guardian/guardian in row 2** (5F 17% → 22–30%).
4. A 720-order sweep costs 0 calls. Confirm the top orders on an independent seed: most small "wins" vanish.

## Tips for the next AI
1. **D8 is a gear wall, not a strategy wall.** Rushing D1–D6 does not help unless D8 gear catches up: fewer days means fewer drops. Earlier winners reached D8 with 19–28k HP. Mine had 10.5k at D8 entry. Before entering D8, check party HP and put the `vitalityBonus` items (1401/1402/5401/7403) on the tanks.
2. **Know the D8 killers:** 2F gravity room enemy **358** and 5F suppression enemy **376** (Voidspawn/Jinma ninja-rangers) shoot first and wipe 0-PD snipers. The 3F elite **369** (264k HP) attacks with magic. Defender protects only against physical damage, so a Pilgrim helps there. Defender in row 2 (Kemo, Laika, then snipers) fixed 2F.
3. **Gate math with real cycle times:** at D8 a cycle is about 22 min, not 11, because Defeats force a long rest and Precision makes exploration ×1.2. So a 12 h step is only about 32 runs. Use `expRuns = (p^-k − 1)/(1 − p)` with that rate.
4. **Instant sorties:** stock is charged on the in-game clock (tier 3: 6/12/24/48/96/192 min, max 6). A Defeat leaves the party at 0 HP and the next sortie is refused (`party_exhausted`), so a chain usually ends after one run at a hard wall. They were not worth the calls in this run. Their real value is reading a boss kill immediately at a high kill rate.
5. **At D8 `clearGates` is `[]` both before and after the boss.** Use `read/expedition/1/setting` (`difficultyOffset.max` 0 → 48) as a 1-call kill check, then read the diary at once. The `bossRare` entry is evicted by defeat spam within about 12 entries.
6. **Deities:** Precision stayed best at every stage I tested (D6, D8 2F/3F, D8 5F), despite its exploration time penalty. Fertility and Mirage were the next best.
7. **Shop:** at intimacy 0, unidentified slots rolled plain T5/T6 +2/+3 items with no Super Rare. Not worth calls. Gold piles up (142k by D7).
8. **Jewels are scarce** (about 30 in total by day 50; the first arrived at D4) because side quests rarely trigger. Assign them every rebuild. Distinct ranks stack; duplicate ranks do not.

## Opportunities for future runs
- **Prepare the D8 HP/defense build before entering D8:** vitality armor on the tanks, Laika row 2, a fast caster, and thunder. This run found each D8 fix one wall too late.
- **Combine this run's D1–D6 pace (14.1 days) with Codex's D7–D8 (19.3 days).** That would land at about 33 days if the D8 gear wall can be met.
- **Rebuild cadence:** rebuilds cost 15–25 calls (jewels dominate). Rebuild only when the forecast doubles, and use the 1-call checks (order, single class change) first.
- **API:** a batch equipment/jewel transaction would turn about 385 build calls into about 60. An explicit `bossDefeated` flag in the expedition projection would remove the `[]` ambiguity.

## Bugs and issues
**Game / API (no combat bug found):**
- `clearGates` is identical (`[]`) before and after the D8 boss, while at D1–D7 it changes (`godEntry` → `godGate`). Suggest a `bossDefeated` field.
- `equip` with an array returns `illegal_action:item_not_equippable` without naming the offending entry. Suggest including the item and index in `details`.
- The diary keeps only about 12 entries per party, so boss evidence is evicted within hours at D8. Suggest pinning the first `bossRare`/Clear per destination.
- The 15-minute lease expires during offline work (#26). The client re-logs in automatically; harmless but costs a call.

**My tool mistakes (fixed, recorded in the ledger):**
- The builder's `FORCE` option pinned items without checking equippability (gauntlets on Laika → #399/#406), and first pinned slot 0 (overwrote the bow).
- I re-ran `apply.mjs` on an already-applied save (#400–#404), stripping several slots. I recovered with a fresh export and a re-apply (about 20 extra calls). `apply.mjs` now refuses to apply twice on the same save.
- A jewel swap cycle (might 3 ↔ 4) deadlocked; now solved by detaching one jewel first.
