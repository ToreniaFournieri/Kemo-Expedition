## Strategy (before signup)

**Concept: "Charge-Clock".** Same proven party as Fable/Slot2 (Laika guardian/guardian in front, Kemo lord/lord, three Ursan/Leporian ninja/rangers, Selfin alchemist/wizard). The new part was a set of mechanics read from the code, which earlier runs did not use:

1. **Sortie charges refill on a stock-dependent clock** (`instantExpedition.ts`).
   - Before the D1 boss: 1/2/4 min. After D1: 2/4/8/15. After D2: 4/8/15/30/60. After D3: 6/12/24/48/96/192 min.
   - Refilling from 0 is cheap. "Elapsed C(k) + sortie k" loops add k runs per loop, at 2 calls each.
2. **`depthLimit` does nothing for Clear-Gates.** A locked X-4 gate already ends the run as Return after X-3 (`expeditionService.ts`). I made no depth calls for gates; earlier runs spent about 40 calls on them.
3. **An offline emulator of the API.** It uses the game's own code:
   - `stageApiV1ElapsedProgression` for elapsed time;
   - `applyApiV1Commit` for sorties, builds and equipment;
   - the FULL/SEMI `planAutoEquipment`, copied out of the `HomeScreen` component.

   It reproduced Fable's opening (D1 boss in 1.0–1.5 d) and was used to choose cadences. All plans were scored first with the existing twin tools (`pipe.sh`, `lever`, `wallopt3` GA).

## What decided the run

1. **D1–D5 in 5.45 days.** Two things combined:
   - short loops on cheap charges: 3 h + 3 sorties at D1, 1 h + 4 at D2, 2 h + 5 at D3;
   - a small plan rated at the first stall, never after days.

   Examples:
   - D1 boss: plan A (8 calls) took it from 2.4% to 100%.
   - D2 gates 5/6: a GA plan (13 calls) reached 96%/85%. `pipe.sh` plan C needed 34 calls for only 55%/30%.
   - D4 gate 1: an attacker plan (13 calls) took it from 0% to 96%, using 916 unused tier-3 items.
   - D4 boss: a thunder-weighted plan C plus Fortification, then a GA seeded from it, reached 58%.
2. **From D6 on, the clock is item tier, not level.** Level only raises party HP; attack comes only from gear.
   - The D6 boss (Procyonian, resists thunder) did 0.47 boss damage at Lv26, with 0 kills. Two days later, with tier-6 drops, a fire-weighted plan did 0.80 damage and 16% full-route clears. It fell in the next loop.
   - **D7 boss gate:** I stayed at ~9% for 3 days. A fresh-export re-plan at day 12.35 (1,700 tier-7 items) still gave only 6%. My next fresh export was at day 14.93 (2,862 tier-7 items); its fire plan gave gate 64% and boss 44%, and the boss fell 2 h later.
   - **D8:** a re-plan with 580 tier-8 items took gates 2/3/4 from 54/50/1% to 78/76/40%. They fell within 12 h.
3. **Element weights changed at every wall.** D4 boss thunder (+Fortification), D5 ice, D6 boss fire 1.5, D7 fire 2, D8 thunder 2. Equal weights gave 0% at D5 and D8.
4. **Fertility was the best deity from D5 on**, except D6 gates 3–4 (Precision). Mirage was best for D4 gates.
5. **D8's last stretch was route survival, not damage.** Boss-only kills were already 97%, but only 5–12% of runs reached the boss. Gates 5–6 needed the extra HP of Lv31–32 and about 2 days.

## Useful tips for another AI

1. **Build the dist yourself:**
   ```bash
   npx vite build --outDir <ws>/app/dist --emptyOutDir
   ```
   Copy `desktop/`, `scripts/run-api-play-session.cjs` and `package.json` next to it, and symlink `node_modules`. ClaudeRec's sortie 500 came from a stale dist. Here, 177 sortie calls returned no 500.
2. **Cadence.** Each loop is "elapsed S; sortie k". A sortie wipes the carried sub-cycle remainder (`authority.ts`), so a loop gives floor(S/cycle) natural runs plus k sortie runs.
   - What I used: k=5, S=11160 s, for grinds. k=3, S=2520 s, once the boss was open with ≥10% odds.
   - A sortie batch stops at the first Defeat, because HP is 0. A Retreat does not stop it.
3. **Never send `depthLimit` for gates.**
   - Use `beforeBoss` only while the boss is open but unbeatable. You lose no EXP, because boss EXP only comes with a kill, and you get fewer Defeats.
   - Use `4f-4` when you need sorties that rarely Defeat.
   - Defeat keeps the route EXP.
4. **Re-plan from a fresh export every ~12 game hours of a wall, or when a new item tier piles up.** Count the inventory by tier in the export. At D7 I went 2.6 days between fresh exports, and the plan jumped from 6% to 64% somewhere in that window. A GA run on an old export cannot find gear that dropped after it.
5. **Watch for slot unlocks.** Level-ups at Lv10, 14 and 20 gave every character an empty slot. SEMI mode, which any manual equip switches on, never fills it. Fill it with a plan.
6. **Prefer a GA seeded from a `proxyopt` plan.**
   - A cold GA on the boss objective only learns to survive (reach 95%, 0 kills).
   - Use `LAMBDA` (~0.003) to keep plans small.
   - Score boss damage with `bossfight.mjs`/`dmgplan` before buying calls.
7. **Apply a plan right after its export.** SEMI upgrades change item enhancement, and old plan items then fail with `item_not_owned` and leave empty slots.
8. **Use `read/observation/expedition` for gates.** `read/observation/compact` returns 500 as soon as any party has a timed side quest (bug 1).
9. **Kill check:** a D8 gate list of `[]` after `godEntry` means a kill. Confirm with the Global Diary `bossFirstClear` (it has entries for D1, D2 and D8 only).
10. **Pick EXP farms with `tools/farm.mjs`.** At my levels, a cleared dungeon at a difficulty offset never beat the frontier (≤ 70–100k EXP/12 h everywhere).

## Opportunities for future runs

- **A fixed 12 h re-plan cadence at walls.**
  - The wall breaks came 1–3 days into each wall, when enough higher-tier drops had piled up: D6 boss 0.5% at day 9.22 → 16% at 10.25; D7 gate 6 6% at 12.35 → 64% at 14.93.
  - Exporting every 12 h would catch the crossing point sooner, probably by 0.5–1.5 d in total. Each re-plan costs one export plus the plan.
  - D1: rate the boss when its gate opens (about 0.3 d lost).
  - With these, about 18 d looks reachable with the same play.
- **Calls.** 43% of requests were builds (348) and 177 were sorties.
  - A GA with `LAMBDA` and skipping gains under ~10 points keep plans at 8–20 calls.
  - k=6 loops in long grinds would save about 8 calls per day for about 0.1 d per day.
  - A run aiming for under 500 calls should use 12 h steps + sortie 6 during grinds, and plans only at walls.
- **Survival gear in grinds.** Retreats keep sortie batches going and Defeats end them. Gear that turns Defeats into Retreats raises the sortie yield (average yield was ~2.6 of 5 at D6–D8).
- **A route objective that accounts for HP attrition across rooms.** D8 gate 5 losses were spread over 2F, 3F-4, 4F-4 and 5F-1/2. Single-gate GAs stall near 15%.

## Bugs and improvement requests

1. **`read/observation/compact` returns 500 `internal_error` when a party has a timed side quest (build-27 regression).**
   - Electron logs `produced a response that does not match its catalog schema [{path:'/partyInfo/1/sideQuest', keyword:'pattern'}]`.
   - `formatCompactSideQuest` now appends `-YYYYMMDD HH:MM`. The catalog pattern is still `^q\.[A-Za-z_-]+-[1-4]: [0-9]+/[0-9]+(?:-[0-9]+h/[0-9]+h)?$`.
   - The timestamp also uses local-time getters (`getHours()`), so it follows the host time zone rather than UTC.
   - Expected: update the contract pattern and use UTC.
2. **Global Diary `bossFirstClear` is still missing for D3–D7.**
   - Only D1, D2 and D8 have entries, while `defeatedBossExpeditions` shows all 8.
   - Fable reported the same in build 17.
3. **Global Diary achievement entries carry a stale timestamp.**
   - `clear:100`, `clear:1000`, `jewel:1`, `jewel:10` and `superRare:1` are all stamped 2026-10-10T12:05:18.725Z. That is the same time and ID prefix as the D2 first-clear entry.
   - The export at day 1.79 still had `achievementClearTotal` 3 and no milestones. The final total is 1,739, mostly from side parties, so these entries were created later with an old time.
4. **"Boss Rare acquired" diary titles on runs that never reached the boss.**
   - D1 had two such entries (Return 16/24 and Retreat 17/24). Boss-rare items 1402 and 1406 dropped from normal or elite rooms.
   - They raised `godBattle:1:bossRare` to 2 before the boss was beaten.
   - If drops outside the boss room are intended, the title is misleading.
5. **Not a game bug:** ClaudeRec's sortie 500 in build 27 came from a stale dist. A fresh build returns `summary` correctly.

Improvement requests:
- add PT level/EXP to `read/observation/expedition` (compact is the only read with the level, and it breaks);
- a per-party `bossDefeated` flag (the `[]`/`godEntry` ambiguity remains);
- `chargeStock` and `currentHp` in the sortie response;
- SEMI should fill newly unlocked empty slots, or report them in the elapsed response;
- `equip` could accept "same item id, best owned enhancement", which removes most `item_not_owned` plan failures;
- an "apply equipment for several characters" commit would cut about 100 build calls per run.
