## How to read

* **Accepted** (step 6, beat the current build by 7 % and 2 SE) is the strength signal. **Passed** (the 7 % quick check) is not: quick runs are noisy, and many candidates that pass are rejected later.
* **Tried** is known only for full records (build 47+, last column). An ability that is never tried was never owned (or never fit); that is availability, by design, not weakness. Only "tried often, rarely accepted" points to a weak ability.
* A gain is the whole item swap: a strong item can carry a weak ability. **Alone** counts acceptances where the ability item was the only change; **Items** shows which item carried it. **freeGain** (build 47+) measures the ability itself, given for free with no item change: near or below 0 means the counter does little or harms in that room.
* Gains are in the tuning's score: route success for `success` tunings, boss damage share for `bossDamage` ones.
* The AI runs use similar parties and strategies, and the tuning rules changed between builds 41 and 47: compare like with like.
