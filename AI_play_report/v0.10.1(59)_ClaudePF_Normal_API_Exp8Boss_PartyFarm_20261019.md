## Concept
PT1 = the ClaudeUQ recipe (GA-only loop, boss7x, unique release + `uniqueCharacter`). New part: side parties farm items for PT1.
- PT2 gets **Goddess of Discord** (+1 reward ticket at rank 1, so 3 draws instead of 2) and a **Difficulty Offset** when it can survive one (+2 = +1 item ticket, +4 = +1 Super Rare ticket).
- Side parties stay in FULL auto-equip; PT1's GA (`considerItemsScope: global`) takes whatever it wants from them.
- Side parties advance through their natural Cycles inside PT1's elapsed calls (no extra calls), plus a short GA for PT2 when it is stuck.

## Side-party farm: what happened
1. **Destinations and offsets are per party.** PT2 started at Lv1 with only D1 selectable and offset max 0. It had to beat every boss itself, so it could never farm the frontier tier.
2. PT2 climbed through natural Cycles: Lv1 -> 7 and the D1 boss by 1.8 d; D2 boss by ~3.5 d; D3 5f gate at Lv19-20 by ~4.6 d, then stalled (Defeats). Three short PT2 GAs (`withinTargets`, deity locked) fixed each stall: 0.55 -> 1.0, 0 -> 1.0, and 0.41 -> 1.0.
3. Offset: D2 +6 gave 58 % Defeat (+6 at 5f-3: 61 %), and +2 gave 45 % (100 % after a GA). From 6.5 d PT2 farmed D2 +2 (4 tickets).
4. **PT2 lost Discord at 6.4 d.** My manual D6 boss GA (`considerDeityChange: allDeities`) moved Discord to PT1 (call 1,314 sets `party/2 deityId none`). Four calls later another GA moved PT1 back to Fertility, but PT2 stayed at `none` for the rest of the run. PT3 (call 527), PT4 (1,389), and PT6 (2,269) also lost their deities this way. PT1's GA changed its own deity 43 times in this run.
5. **Effect on PT1: not measurable.** PT1's final 65 items include 21 at +4..+6 (x2.75-x5.0), many from older tiers (4109+6, 3303+3, 4103+4, 5107+5 x3, 5102+4). Enhancement bags are per party, so side-party draws do add high-enhancement items. But the API does not say which party dropped an item, so I cannot separate them from PT1's own drops. The walls that cost time (D6 route, D7 Lv30, D8 gates) were route and level walls, and side-party items did not open them.
6. Cost: 36 side-party calls (1.5 %) and ~9 min of side-GA time. One PT1 GA result was lost: my PT2 apply changed the inventory while a PT1 GA was running, and the PT1 apply got 409.

**Verdict:** under build 59, the side-party farm is cheap but did not make the D8 run faster. A new party needs about 8+ in-game days to reach a tier that matters for PT1's D6-D8, which is about the length of the whole run.

## Tips for another AI
1. **Protect side-party deities.** With `allDeities`, PT1's GA takes another party's deity, and that party keeps `none` even after PT1 moves on. Use `unoccupiedDeities` for PT1, or re-set the side deity after every apply.
2. **D7 boss at Lv28 with share < 0.9: farm to Lv30 right away** (D7 4f-3, `K=6 HPF2=0` batch sorties, `STOPLV=30`). Lv30 took share 0.69 -> 0.99. My two attack tries before the farm wasted ~1.3 d.
3. Run side-party GAs only while PT1 is not searching, with `considerItemsScope: withinTargets` and `considerDeityChange: none`. While PT1's loop runs, apply often fails `build_basis_stale` or `item_not_owned`. Retrying without `basis` sometimes works.
4. `godGate <dungeon>` in the gate list = that boss is dead. Check it before any re-plan (my `hl4.py` started a GA first).
5. D6 boss: if the route stays at ~0.5 %, plain boss7x passes are still the best of what I tried (a route-first GA, an elite GA, a long guarded GA, and Restoration all failed).

## Bugs / suggestions
- **Deity taken from a side party is never returned (question/suggestion).** `applyBuildChanges` from a PT1 GA sets `party/2 deityId: none`, and the next PT1 GA picks another deity, so PT2 stays without one. Suggestions: when a later change frees the deity, give it back to the party that lost it, or at least list "deity taken from PTn" in the GA result/`changeSummary` warnings. A search could also prefer unoccupied deities on ties.
- **Side-party GA vs. running progression.** `build_basis_stale` / `item_not_owned` when the inventory changes during a 1-2 min search (PT1 drops, side-party FULL auto-equip, auto-sell). Suggestion: base `basis` on the items the changes actually use, not on the whole inventory.
- **No item provenance.** Suggest a per-party drop counter (items, enhancement, SR) in observation/Statistics, so a farm party can be evaluated.
- **`simulationRun` only simulates the current setting**, so testing a side-party offset/depth costs one commit per option. Suggest optional `destination`/`depthLimit`/`difficultyOffset` overrides.
- **levelProbe at the D7 boss** said Lv30 rate 0 (route, greedy fill), but the real Lv30 re-plan gave 85 % boss wins. For a boss goal, also probe the boss room alone.
- My own client errors: `hl4.py` re-plans on `godGate` before its kill check (1 wasted GA); `b6.sh` ran `release.py` without `env.sh` (PT5 release ~20 calls late); `read/progress/donationBox` is a wrong path (404). The 9 `stale_revision` 409s were concurrency, all retried.
