## Mechanics worth knowing (from specs, source and logs)

1. **Damage is `(raw attack - defense x (1 - penetration)) x amplifier x element`.** Boss and elite defense is 500–750, so raw item attack is leveraged: +10% raw is about +20% damage, whereas a +10% amplifier is only +10%. A strong build is one with high raw attack on every attacker.
2. **Distinct `c.*` bonuses sum, class/lineage/race multipliers multiply.** Only one bonus of the exact same name applies (so a sage/sage "master" class does not double `c.grimoire_x1.4`). Arrow raw attack for a ranger sub with the abyssal_sea lineage is x1.4 x 1.2 = x1.68, bolts stay x1.0, so arrows beat bolts per slot.
3. **Jewels are not small.** `might` (arrows, bolts) and `arcana` (wands, grimoires) add `c.physical-attack` / `c.magical-attack` +0.22, +0.21, +0.19, +0.18 ... by rank 1, 2, 3, 4 ..., and every distinct rank stacks. Three `arcana` jewels took Selfin's amplifier from 3.17 to 3.99 and her raw from 1,707 to 1,998. `fort` / `ward` (+0.13 ... +0.05) lower damage taken the same way, and `focus` / `shade` are only +0.008 ... +0.001. I misjudged them early because the first jewels I looked at were `focus` and `shade`.
4. **Row matters for ranged and melee, not for magic.** Hit potency is 1.00, 0.85, 0.72, 0.61, 0.52, 0.44 by row for ranged and melee; magical attacks ignore it, so casters belong in the back rows. In the three or four logs where I counted, a 36-attempt enemy volley put about 17 attempts on row 1, 10 on row 2 and 7–8 on row 3, with rows 4–6 getting only a few.
5. **Element: one element item makes the attacker that element, and its multiplier is the sum of the distinct `e.*` values.** The first item is worth most (x1.11 and the enemy weakness), later ones add only +0.09–0.10. For the snipers an extra elemental bolt costs raw attack (their arrows are x1.68), so one is enough; the casters can carry several elemental catalysts for free because catalysts only add attack count. Check the enemy-type table in Spec 4.1 per destination: D6 Procyonian boss thunder x0.67, D7 Origami `thunder-null` and fire x1.5 (Undead also), D8 Voidspawn fire x0.67 and thunder x1.3.
6. **Voiders**: `a.ranged-null` and `a.illusion` void whole ranged attacks, `a.thunder-null` voids thunder attackers, `a.magic-seal` voids the first spell. Plan for at least one caster alongside the snipers.
7. **HP is quadratic in vitality.** Base HP is about `L x vit x (vit + mind) / 20`, so put every `b.vitality+1` item on one or two characters (Kemo and Laika). Ten 7402 armors on Kemo were worth +37% party HP for two calls.
8. **Enemy alphas decide most failures.** First-strike ranged enemies with 36–47 attempts (D8 Vespa, Myura, Zeke) can take 20k HP before our casters act. Magic acts after them, so the snipers' initiative and damage are what matter. Fertility (+1 to every action) beat every other deity in forecasts at D6–D8.
9. **Things that were worse and reverted**: `nimble` (a.boost) on both snipers (accuracy -0.02 cost more than +1 initiative), Kuz as sage/sage (lost two slots and NoA), restoring armor slots on the snipers, Restoration, Fortification, Cunning, Mirage, Attrition, Resonance and Precision as the deity.

## Tips for another AI run

1. **Do the raw-attack work first, at D5–D6.** Convert the two ranged characters' armor/robe slots to arrows (distinct `c.ranged-attack` values, T-highest), put `might` / `arcana` jewels of ranks 1–3 on them, and move all `b.vitality+1` items to Kemo and Laika. These three changes together were worth more than every deity, row-order and class change I tried, and they would have saved most of the 13 days spent on the D6 boss.
2. **Pick the element per destination from the enemy-type table** (one element item per sniper is enough). Re-check it whenever the destination changes: my fire loadout was right at D7 and wrong at D8.
3. **Use `simulationRun` with `numberOfRun: 1000`** (default is 100; any other member is rejected). It is a READ POST with a raw body. Compare big changes only; boss-room win rates below 1% need the 1,000-run size.
4. **Cheap diagnostics**: `read/observation/diary?partyNumber=1` gives entry ids, and `read/expedition/1/latestBattleLog?logId=diary:<id>` returns the full log, which shows the enemy, who acted first and who took the damage. `changeBuild` with `simulation: true` previews stats but trims slots; `equipmentEvaluation` only returns defense deltas, not attack.
5. **Equip tips**: `removeEquipment` with a list of slots followed by one `equip` array fills slots in two calls; `equip` with `targetSlot` replaces one slot; a replaced item drops its jewel back to the inventory, so reattach it. Both switch FULL to SEMI, which is fine here.
6. **Step in batches** (six to eight 12 h steps per decision) and read the expedition once at the end. Re-scan the best items by category about every ten steps once a new tier drops (T7 and T8 arrive as low enhancement, and a `+5` or `+6` drop is worth a re-slot).
