## Useful tips for another AI
1. **Fights are one volley** (a single COMBAT phase). Draw means neither side died. For bosses and elites, compute "damage in one volley ≥ enemy HP" and "enemy volley < party HP". Check initiative order in the battle log (`bosslog.mjs`).
2. **Damage amplifier = 1 + sum of DISTINCT `ranged_attack` values.** Low-tier arrows carry the larger values (T1 +0.23 … T6 +0.17), and each distinct value stacks. The best sniper mixes one arrow per tier with a few high-attack items. Per-hit accuracy decays geometrically (`0.90 + c.accuracy`, capped at 0.98), so accuracy items compound with many attacks. Magic ignores row potency.
3. **Element matters per dungeon**: D5 Dragons fire ×1/2 (use ice or none). D6 Mech/Chiropteran weak to thunder, but the D6 6F Procyonians resist it and have Illusion. D7 Origami are **thunder-null** and weak to fire. D8 Voidspawn are weak to thunder. Removing wrong-element gear alone moved D5 gate 5 from 8% to 40% and D7 from 3% to 23%.
4. **Use the 1-call levers at every wall**: deity (Fortification vs physical bosses, Mirage on D5, Precision for D6–D8), order (snipers in rows 2–4 for magic-attack bosses where Defender does nothing; Laika in front for physical routes), race/class (Felidian or Ninja/Wizard caster for initiative walls).
5. **Side parties on FULL auto equipment hoard the best gear.** PT2–PT4 held many T6 +3…+6 items. My `SIDE=1` optimizer option pools their equipment and adds `removeEquipment` calls only for the chosen slots.
6. **Supports must carry the high-tier shields**: Kemo (lord ×1.68 shields, 13 slots) and Laika. HP went from 12.0k to 20.9k in 1 second of greedy search (`hpopt.mjs`). D8 defeats fell from 54% to 11% with Laika back in front.
7. **Search tools that worked**: `proxyopt.mjs`, a deterministic attacker proxy (attack × distinct-bonus amplifier × element weight × expected hits plus a greedy jewel pass; about 1 s). Then simulator hill-climbs (`ALGO=climb`, `WPROG=3` for gates, `OBJ=bossonly` for bosses), 4 seeds in parallel, each about 3–5 min. The GA was weaker than the climb at every wall.

## Bugs and issues found
1. **Twin HP cache trap (tooling, high impact):** `computePartyMaxHp` caches by `party.characters` array identity (WeakMap). The twin optimizers in `play_runs/ai_play_tools` edit equipment in place, so **every search before the fix used a stale party HP**. HP items looked worthless to all earlier Claude runs' GA. Fixed by refreshing the array in `lib.sim()`, `bossonly.mjs`, `wall.mjs` and `hpopt.mjs`. Suggestion: key the cache on equipment content too, or document it next to the function.
2. **Electron play session died during a long idle** (about 1 h 40 min). The result was connection refused, then 503 `runtime_unavailable` for about 20 s after a restart. Restarting with the same `--user-data` recovered the exact state (`start.sh`).
3. **Diary eviction again lost the boss-kill log.** PT1 writes a defeat entry about every 30–60 min and only 12 are kept. Suggestion (repeated from other reports): pin boss-clear entries or expose a boss-defeat timestamp.
4. **Gate list semantics**: during D2 the list showed only the next elite gate, not the entry gate of D3. My first auto-advance guessed wrong (call 22, `illegal_action`). Rule that worked: advance when every remaining gate is `godGate`.
5. My loop did not stop after the D8 kill (destination 8 has no next dungeon). That cost 2 extra steps, 1 game day and 4 calls. Stop on `godGate`/empty gates at D8.
6. Design observation: FULL auto on side parties silently competes with PT1 for top-tier inventory. A "reserve best items for PT1" or priority option would help API players.

## Opportunities for future runs
- Start the proxy optimizer, the HP optimizer and the side-party pool **from D4**. I only discovered the HP cache bug and the side-party hoarding at D8. Earlier use would probably have saved D4–D6 time (D6 alone took 10.5 days).
- Keep two loadouts per wall: a route build (Laika front, HP) and a boss build (snipers rows 2–4). Switch with one order call plus a small plan when the boss gate opens.
- At D8 gate 5, go straight to the Ninja/Wizard (or Felidian) caster. Both CodexBurst and this run found it there.
- Reduce calls: plans averaged 15–25 calls. A batch equip endpoint would cut about 250 calls per run.
