## Tips for another AI run

1. **Look at jewels from the first day.** `read/base/searchItems?state=owned&details=all` does not list them; query `category=jewel` separately. All six parties (PT1–PT6) roll side quests whenever their current dungeon has no open gate, and each completed quest drops one jewel into the shared inventory (about one per in-game day here, ranks 1–4 here). I only found my 15 jewels at D7 (call 396) and the first full jewel pass roughly doubled the boss arrival rate and raised damage by about 28%. Distinct ranks of one jewel type stack on one character (`might` 1+2+3+4 is about +80% attack multiplier on a sniper, `arcana` the same for casters, `fort` / `ward` for tanks), so spread them over distinct ranks. A hypothetical "four `might` and four `arcana` on every attacker" test took a 11% wall to 64%; this is the biggest untapped lever. Untested idea: point PT2–PT6 (they sit on destination 4, auto) at a deeper, fully cleared dungeon for higher-rank jewels.
2. **Build a digital twin and optimise offline.** Bundle the repo with esbuild (entry exporting `simulateExpeditionRuns`, `simulateApiSortieBatchForTesting`, `computeCharacterStats`, `deriveStatusFacts`, `getItemById`, `buildBattleLogData`; define `import.meta.env.*`, `__APP_VERSION__` etc.; a plugin for `?raw` markdown), load `sample_savedata/ALL_Exp8_v0.9.3_dev_20260816.kemoz` through `hydrateGameState` as the base state and patch in the live party, equipment (with `jewel: {key, rank}`), deity donations, gate flags (`dungeon*1000 + floor*10 + 4` for each cleared floor gate, `+604` boss gate, `+101` entry) and `defeatedBossExpeditions`. Replace `crypto.getRandomValues` with a seeded stream so that two loadouts are compared on the same random numbers. It reproduced the live forecasts within about 2–3 points (D7 5F gate 38.0 live vs 39.3 offline; D7 boss gate 11.3 vs 13.8; D8 2F 44.4 vs 46–48; D8 4F 30.5 vs 32–35; D8 5F 21.8 vs 20–22), so decisions made offline carried over. It costs zero API calls.
3. **Search the right scenario.** Run `simulationRun` once per stage and read `depthLimit.reachable` and `blockedByGate`: the gate is checked at room 3, so the run that counts is the one that ends at `<floor>f-3` as a Return. Model exactly that (cleared floors up to the previous gate). My first D7 search used the wrong floors and optimised for the wrong thing.
4. **Streak gates are cubic in the success rate.** Expected runs for k consecutive successes at per-run probability p is (p^-k - 1) / (1 - p). With about 26 runs per 12 h step (cycle about 27.5 min), p = 0.11 for k = 3 is about 32 steps, p = 0.145 about 15, p = 0.22 about 4.6. A "small" +3 points is worth more than most call savings, so keep optimising until p is roughly 0.3 for k = 3–4.
5. **Formation is a per-stage lever.** Hit potency for ranged and melee is 0.85^(row-1) (+0.1 for a composure holder); magic ignores the row. Front-line tanks (Laika Defender row 1, Kemo Command row 2) were best for the early floors, but at the elite walls the best order moved Command to row 1 and the snipers to rows 2–4 (D8 4F: 0.7% to 30%). A full 720-permutation sweep with gear rebuilt per order took about 25 minutes of CPU, cost 0 calls, and the change itself is 1 call (`commit/build/party/1`, `order`). Re-sweep at every new stage.
6. **Allocate tank armor in the right order.** The Command carrier (Kemo, 13 slots) should pick armors and shields first; the Defender carrier already has more physical defense than the enemy attack in most rooms (hits on Laika did 1–3 damage). Putting Kemo first gained +4 points at D8 3F. Move every `b.vitality+1` item to the two tanks (HP is quadratic in vitality).
7. **Hill-climb on the real simulator, not on a damage formula.** A greedy per-character optimiser with a damage proxy got me close; a random local search that evaluates 800–1,000 simulated runs on common random numbers (replace one slot from the pool, swap between characters, move a jewel) added another 5–10 points at several walls. Start it from the structured build. Moves that need a coordinated change (class, row, element) must be tried explicitly: sweep them as separate specs.
8. **Element per destination.** One elemental item per sniper decides the element (its multiplier is the sum of the distinct `e.*` values). D7: fire (Origami and Undead take x1.5; Origami is `thunder-null`). D8: thunder (Voidspawn fire x2/3, ice x2/3, thunder x1.3; Ghost has physical defense x3/5 and ice x1.5). A fire loadout at D8 left a 100k-HP room as a Draw at 93% damage.
9. **Know the terrain.** D7: decay (2% HP), chain-lightning, light and dark fields (x1.45 magic or physical for both sides), low gravity. D8: mana-burn (casters lose 2% HP per spell), gravity (x0.7 attacks, the 2F wall), transcendence (+1 ability levels for both sides, the 3F elite), gehenna (no deity bonuses on 4F), suppression (-1 level on reaction and timed abilities, Defender and Command drop to 1 on 5F), sanctuary (magic x0.67 on 6F).
10. **Precision stayed the best deity at every stage.** With accuracy mostly from the deity, the predisposition (`precise`) and the bows, the 17–27 attacks of a ninja keep a decay near 0.95–0.98. I tested every other deity with its real rank (donated gold) at D7 and D8 stages, and the other sniper predispositions at the D8 4F stage; all were worse (`perceptive`, `aggressive`, `inquisitive` by 5–10 points).
11. **Things that looked clever and were not**: converting snipers to casters (casters act late and mages die to first-strike volleys); ranger / ninja instead of ninja / ranger (first strike lost); ninja / ninja master; Procyonian for `a.illusion` (the lost raw attack costs more); pilgrim for M-Barrier on Laika (Defender lost); Deflection and Decompose items; a lord or guardian in the back row for Command / Defender; level-ups (about +4 points per 2–4 levels, while XP per level is 0.6–0.9 million).
12. **Use evidence reads sparingly.** `read/observation/diary?partyNumber=1` lists the entry ids with their triggers, and `read/expedition/1/latestBattleLog?logId=diary:<id>` returns that log; a `bossRare` entry marks a boss kill. `read/expedition/1/setting` shows `difficultyOffset.max` for the destination, which jumps from 0 to 48 after the D8 boss.
13. **Call economy.** Step in 12 h blocks (`commit/progress/elapsed 43200`) and read `clearGates` (`--pick` a path) only every 2–3 steps; if a gate opens in between nothing is lost, because the next stage's runs still earn XP and drops. Re-snapshot inventory and party (2 calls) only right before an optimisation. `removeEquipment` with a list plus one `equip` array re-slots a character in two calls; a replaced item drops its jewel back to the inventory, so plan the jewel moves after the equipment diff with a fresh party read, and do jewel replacements before plain attaches (`jewel_not_owned` otherwise). `sortie` (1 call, no clock time, stock 6) was never needed; it is the tool for the day when clock time matters more than calls.
14. **Offline work outlives the lease.** The lease expires after 15 idle minutes, so the first call after a long offline step fails with `control_lease_expired` and costs a `logIn` as well. Batch the offline work, then make several calls in a row.

## What I would do differently

- Check `category=jewel` at call 10 and run the jewel assignment before every wall; D6 (13 days) and the D7 5F gate would probably have been much shorter.
- Run the full formation sweep and the joint equipment-plus-jewel search at the first wall instead of the third.
- Keep the offline step count low between calls to avoid the 11 lease errors.
