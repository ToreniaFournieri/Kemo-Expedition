## Findings
1. **The gate scope hides the next elite (D5).** The 4th Elite gate route ends at 4f-3 (`endsAt: clearGate`), so the 4f re-plan built a fast fire-ranged party. That party could never beat the 4f-4 elite Salvata (Dragon, `r.fire` 0.5, fire reflect), and the 5f gate needs that win in every run. Result: 0 % route, 77 % defeat at 4f-4. Things that did not fix it:
   - 5 `max` GAs (progress start). The elite's HP share counts only 1/19 of `progress`, so the signal drowns in noise.
   - A seeded `max` GA with 10 GP/GX D5 builds.
   - `stagedEquipmentTuning` on all attackers. It found the bottleneck room 4f-4 but had no counter item (fire-protect-breaker `noItem`).
   - `directedBuild meleeBurst` (objective progress) moved to sword-saints (0.761 -> 0.777, Draws 14 %), and a GA took it to 0.794, but still 0 % success.

   What worked: the Lv24 7th slot. A `max` GA went 0 -> 41 %, then 39 -> 60 %, and the gate and the boss fell within 50 calls. The levelProbe had said Lv24 = 0 % (greedy fill only, no search). The loss vs GX at D5 was ~1.4 d and ~230 calls. **Tip:** at a new gate X, also meter the route through the X-4 elite before trusting the gate build.
2. **`successSignal: auto` is a good default for route gates.** It started on progress 15 times and switched to success in 7 of them, and the D7 boss gate went 2.6 -> 5.8 -> 14 %. Two limits:
   - At `max` (quickRuns 300) it switches only below 6.25 %. At 6-15 % (D7 boss gate) success-ranked searches were still noisy. I added GX's "below 15 %, first GA with objective progress" back.
   - **With an `objectiveGuard` it cannot help the boss.** The guarded success passes ranked by progress, but `verdict` stays on success. At a route rate of 0-0.4 %, every result was `noisy`/`noChange` and nothing was applied (D4, D6 try 1). Switching the guarded pass to `objective: progress` (verdict on progress) fixed it: D6 0.27 -> 0.64 -> 0.72 (route 2 %, kill on attack loop 2); D7 0.62 -> 0.91 (route 39 %, kill on loop 4).
3. **Guard ranking works.** In all 18 guarded searches, `guardStats.reached` was close to `confirmed` (e.g. 45/46, 57/67), and none returned a guard-caused noChange (GX had 4).
4. **seedBuilds never helped.** 728 seed entries over 90 GAs: every seed was `used`, and **0 were `inBest`**. Seeds carry no equipment and get a greedy fill, so they start far behind the evolved incumbent. Also, `seeds[].quickRate` was 0 for every D5 seed while the quick runs were ranked by progress. It seems to report the success rate, not the ranking score, so it cannot show whether a seed was close.
5. **The boss recipe that killed D6 and D7 bosses fast:**
   1. `max` unguarded bossDamage GA, to a share of 0.97-1.0 (boss-only kill 80-100 %). A `high` one only reached 0.85-0.89 at D7, with 86 % Draws (timeouts).
   2. `max` GA, objective `progress`, guard bossDamage >= 0.85 x share.
   3. Attack whatever the meter says.

   The D7 boss died 4 loops after the second try. D8: bossDamage 0.89 -> 0.995 (boss-only 95 %), guarded progress 0.41 -> 0.62, route 4.8 %. The boss died 85 calls after BOSS OPEN, on attack loop 4.
6. **The route meter swings more than its SE.** At the D8 5f gate, the same build (no change applied) metered 38 %, 14 %, and 30 % within an hour (300 runs, SE ~3 %). Something in the state (HP carry, condition?) changes the forecast. Re-plan decisions based on one meter can be wrong.
7. Real time again went to search: 90 GAs used ~5.9 h of the run. `max` averaged 325 s for a mean applied gain of +22 pt; `high` averaged 74 s for +38 pt.

## Tips for another AI
1. D1-D4: the GX skip rule plus `high` GAs at new gates, with `max` only when `E - k > 45`. About 460 calls to the D4 boss.
2. Before a gate re-plan, check the room that comes right after the gate floor. An elite there (D5 4f-4 Dragon: no fire) can make the next gate 0 %. If the gate GA gives a fire build at D5, plan for Lv24 or a non-fire build.
3. Re-plan right after slot unlock levels (Lv24, Lv30). Both broke walls this run. Do not trust levelProbe's "0 %" at those levels: it does not search.
4. Bosses: `max` bossDamage GA to share >= 0.97, then `max` progress GA guarded by bossDamage >= 0.85 x share, then attack. Do not use a guarded *success* objective at route < 1 %.
5. Below 15 % route success, use `objective: progress` for the first GA, then re-meter.
6. Do not bother with seedBuilds as they work now.

## Bugs / suggestions
- No game-side errors. Client issues (mine): my HTTP timeout (400 s) was shorter than a 420 s GA; I killed the chain while a re-plan was running and lost that GA's result.
- Suggestion 1, gate scope: a gate objective option that includes the next gate's first room (or the X-4 elite) would stop the gate GA from building a party that cannot pass the next gate.
- Suggestion 2, guarded success at tiny p: when `successSignal` ranks by progress and the final success difference is noise, also report (or optionally judge) the progress gain, or make a guarded `success` search fall back to a progress verdict when both rates are below the rare threshold.
- Suggestion 3, seeds: `quickRate` should report the ranking score (progress when the quick runs use progress). Consider letting a seed run a short equipment GA (a few generations) before it competes, or let a seed take the current equipment.
- Suggestion 4, levelProbe: at a slot-unlock level, a short search instead of the greedy fill (or a note saying the probe does not search) would avoid "0 %" at Lv24 when a search finds 41 %.
- Question: why does the route meter vary 14-38 % on an unchanged build (Finding 6)?
