## Why it was slow (honest diagnosis)
1. **Pool-limited walls.** At D6 5F/boss gate (about 1–2 % per run in the twin, 1.2 % in the live `simulationRun`) and D7 gates 5/boss, equipment-only GA from a cold start stalled at 0.2–2 %. Only **seeded** GA (start from the previous plan on a fresh export) plus new drops ever moved it: D6 boss gate 1.3 % → 8 %, D7 boss 0 % → 14.5 %. Each seeded cycle costs ~25 min real time and 10–25 calls.
2. **No element lever.** At D7 the pool held two ice arrows and no fire arrow/bow, so the "fire at D7, thunder at D8" lever from run 4 and Codex was unavailable (`searchItems` pool: `ice_offense` x2 arrows, `fire_offense` x1 sword only). This is a drop-luck dependency to note.
3. **Deity never changed.** I stayed on Goddess of Restoration (rank 1) for the whole run. All other runs ended on Precision rank 4 or Fertility. My twin deity test used the wall (sortie) objective with rank 0 and showed no gain, so I skipped it; the exact gate objective was never run per deity. This is the largest untested lever of this run. Note `deityDonations` here: Fertility 136k, Fortification 153k, Resonance 142k, Precision 114k gold, so ranks were available.
4. **Jewels arrived late** (4 jewels at day 15, ~10 at day 40, 16 free at day 54). I configured only PT2 (FULL auto equipment) and left PT3–PT6 default. The boss jump at D7 (0 % to 14.5 %) came mostly from placing the 16 jewels in one search.
5. **D2 and D4 delays:** default FULL auto equipment lost to the D2 boss at Lv 17 (needed a 12-call plan) and D4 gate 2 sat at 0 % until a 15-call plan.

## What worked (reusable)
1. `backup/export` (1 call) -> `twin.mjs` -> `wallopt3.mjs` -> `runplan.py`. Typical cycle: 1 export + 8–25 plan calls + 2–6 steps. `ev.mjs` (new) replays a plan on the export and prints exact stage rates; check it with a second seed before applying.
2. **Seed the GA with the last good plan** (`startPlan` argument). Cold GA at deep walls gave −6…−10 objective and ~1 %; seeded gave −3…−4 and 5–14 %.
3. GA gate objective with `WPROG` (new env var in `gate.mjs`, weight of room progress) helped little; the real gain is the pool and jewels.
4. Three seeds in parallel (6 cores) and take the best by `ev.mjs`: seeds differ by 2–5x in final success rate.
5. Boss fights: `bossonly` objective + `bossdmg.mjs` (damage fraction of the boss per arrival) gives a usable signal when clear rate is 0.
6. Live `simulationRun` matched the twin at D6 (1.2 % live vs ~1 % twin) and D8 gate 5.
7. Gates passed far faster than the forecast after a plan was applied: D6 gates 3–5 (forecast 54/32/13 %, total ~660 runs) passed in 4 steps, D8 gates 2/3/4 in 3 days. Forecast-based expectation overstates the time for streak gates; do not wait for the forecast to be "good enough".

## Bugs / gotchas
* **Server restart => `login_required` (401), not `control_lease_expired`.** `t.py` only retries on lease expiry, so `runplan.py` sent all 18 calls of a plan as 401 errors (wasted 18 calls). Suggest the client (and ideally a single clear error hint) handle `login_required` by logging in; or `ensure.sh` should log in after a restart.
* **Play Electron dies when idle** (seen once, a `logIn` hung 120 s). `ensure.sh` (restarts, now parameterised by `$W`) fixes it, but the first call afterwards needs an explicit `logIn`.
* `logIn` while already logged in returns 409 `control_unavailable` (11 times; I called it defensively before each plan, which wasted 11 calls; only log in on 401).
* **Diary retention vs. proof:** only the 12 newest entries of a party are kept; a `bossRare` entry is lost in ~5 h of in-game time once defeats pile up (`defeatOnly` notification mode). `read/expedition/1/latestBattleLog?logId=` then 404s. Suggest pinning boss-clear entries (or `defeatedBossExpeditions` timestamps) so a run can prove its kill time.
* `clearGates` is `[]` both before and after the D8 boss kill in my run (no `godGate` row appeared, unlike the Codex report), so gates cannot tell "boss pending" from "boss done"; `difficultyOffset.max` (0 → 48) or an export are the only cheap checks.
* Tooling: `planCalls` indexed the "before" snapshot by position when the order scenario was applied after the snapshot, producing 40–50 call plans; fixed in `wallopt3.mjs` (reorder before snapshot). Bash `timeout` > 10 min moves jobs to the background; poll files instead.

## Tips for the next AI
1. Order `[1,4,2,3,6,5]` from the first wall; keep Command (Kemo) first, snipers rows 2–4, Defender (Laika) row 5.
2. Set the deity early and test it on the exact gate objective: Precision (+accuracy) or Fertility. I skipped it and it is the first thing I would test.
3. Put FULL auto equipment on PT2 **and** PT3/PT4 early (6 calls each) for jewels; run the jewel pass (`category=jewel`) before every wall. Jewels doubled the D7 boss rate.
4. Never cold-start the GA at a deep wall; seed from the last plan. Launch 3 seeds in parallel and verify with `ev.mjs` and a different seed.
5. Do not wait for forecast > 30 %: apply a plan once the gain is several-fold, then step 4–6 and re-export.
6. Check `searchItems` for element items (fire arrows for D7, thunder for D8) before D7; if absent, consider spending steps at D6 until fire items drop.
7. Re-login explicitly after any Electron restart.
8. Store the victory evidence when it happens: read `diary?partyNumber=1` every 1–2 days near the boss, because the entry is evicted by defeat spam.

## Opportunities for future runs
* **Parallel boss preparation:** the wall at D6/D7 is a pool/jewel problem. Parties PT3–PT6 could farm jewels earlier; the twin cannot pre-compute jewel ranks, but a "jewel-first" policy (all six parties on FULL, deepest cleared dungeon) is untested.
* **Gold sink:** 2.1 M gold at the end (200k "saved gold" at day 78, 113k at day 22). The shop sells 7 items at 60–1440 gold per lineup and `paidShopRefresh` costs 200; a bulk "buy all enhanced items" call, or gold -> jewel/enhancement conversion, would turn idle gold into power. As is, shop use costs more calls than it returns.
* **API:** `equip` with `targetSlot` exists, but a batch endpoint ("apply set of slot->item") would cut 10–25 call plans to 1; batch calls were 274 of 545.
* **Elapsed step:** 12 h is the maximum; a `calculateToRealTime` step is the only way to move more than 12 h per call and it depends on wall clock, so a larger `elapsedSeconds` limit (e.g. 7 days) would save ~100 calls on quiet stretches.
* Strategy idea (untested): at D6/D7, alternate "farm D5/D6 floors for items while the plan is optimised for XP/drop rate" versus pushing the wall.
