## GA usefulness (107 searches, ~2.9 h of search)
- Verdicts: veryGood 45, good 18, noisy 26, noChange 18. The mean gain was +12.1 pt.
- **The biggest single finding is that the effort `high` preset stops early.** At the D8 4f wall, `high` with `timeBudgetSeconds` 300 returned after 75-84 s: +10.6 pt, then noChange. The same search with `populationSize 64, generations 200` used 293 s and took the route from **36 % to 62 %** (veryGood). It then gave another +15 pt (59 -> 74 %), and the gate and the boss fell within about 40 calls. At a wall, always use the big GA: the time budget alone does not buy generations (`high` = 48 x 80 in the 9.1.3 preset table).
- Tuning is poor value at this stage. It cost 373 calls (15 % of the run) and only 38 tunings were accepted (10 %). A GA-only re-plan would save about 300 calls.

## levelProbe: useful, but it answers a narrower question than "farm or search"
80 probes, 0 truncated. `levelNeeded`: null 55, 19 x4, 24 x9, 30 x11, 36 x1.
- **Good:** it shows clearly when a level does *not* help, which tells you to keep searching. Examples: D4 3f, Lv18 67.7 % up to Lv24 72 % against an 80.6 % target; D8 4f, Lv30 36 % up to Lv34 44 %. It also shows the slot-unlock jumps (D5 5f: Lv22 20 % -> Lv24 39 %; D6 4f: 41 % -> 81 % at Lv30). The `steps: level` sweep is cheap and readable.
- **Concern 1, no farm cost:** at the D6 5f wall, the probe said `levelNeeded 30` (59 % against a 53.6 % target). But reaching Lv30 needed 1.15 M EXP, which is about 480 runs at the measured 2.3-2.5 k EXP per run, while `expectedRuns` at the current Lv26 was only 33. Following "farm to levelNeeded" would have repeated DG's 6-day farm. I overrode it: the gate fell, and the D6 boss died at Lv27. **Suggestion:** return `experiencePerRun` for the route (or the best farm depth) and `runsToLevel = experienceToLevel / experiencePerRun`, and judge `runsToLevel + expectedRuns(level)` against `expectedRuns(current)`.
- **Concern 2, pessimistic:** observed gate openings came well under `expectedRuns`. D4 2f: 82 predicted, about 24 runs observed. D6 boss gate: 57 vs about 24. D7 boss gate: 117 vs about 30 loops. D8 boss gate: 13 vs 2 observations. Part of the gap is that later re-plans raise p, and some is luck. Still, the probe's `p` may not be the gate-progress rate the loop actually sees (natural Cycle runs plus sorties, Returns). Please verify that the conquest measure matches how Clear-Gate progress counts.
- Concern 3: the rate is not monotone in level (D4: Lv22 77 % vs Lv23 72.7 %). This is noise plus the two greedy fills. Fine, but reading `levelNeeded` as "first level that met it" can pick a noisy point.
- With `objective: bossDamage`, the probe works on the route/boss goal (it never uses the boss-only forecast), as specified. It was useful at D4/D6 (boss route Lv27 2 % vs Lv30 3 %), and it showed that waiting for Lv30 was pointless.

## Tips for another AI
1. Reuse this run's `client/` (`run25.sh D...` chains stage -> BOSS OPEN -> boss prep). D1-D5 is hands-off: about 1,050 calls, 3.7 d.
2. Do **not** farm D6 to Lv30. Keep the gate loop through D6/D7 at Lv25-28. A bossDamage GA, then a success GA with guard 0.6, is enough for the D6 boss at Lv27.
3. At any wall (noisy/noChange twice), run the **big GA** (`GAX='{"populationSize":64,"generations":200}' ABTB=300`) before thinking about levels. Re-plan again right after Lv30 (8th slot).
4. Read the level probe as "does a level help?", not "should I farm?": compute farm runs from `experienceToLevel` yourself.
5. Confirm a kill with `read/observation/diary` (`diary.global.bossFirstClear`). `godGate` in the gate list also means that boss is dead.
