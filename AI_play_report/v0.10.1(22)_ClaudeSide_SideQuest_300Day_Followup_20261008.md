# ClaudeSide — 300-day side quest monitoring on the rebalanced side quests (v0.10.1 build 22, Normal)

Second follow-up (after `..._100Day_Followup_20261008.md`). The user fixed and rebalanced the side quests on `main` (commits `50e3e5c3`, `076f405f`); I fast-forwarded the branch, rebuilt, and re-ran the same experiment for **300 game days** (1,200 × 6 h `elapsed` steps, one `compact` read after each, 6 `party?partyNumber=N` reads every 30 days; 1,090 requests in this phase, 6 min real time). Data: `play_runs/normal_api_exp8_ClaudeSide_20261008/sq_monitor_300d.log`, script `mon.py 300 21600 <log>`.

## Setup
- Same starting state as the 100-day run: my final boss-clear export of `ClaudeSide` (D8 boss defeated, PT1 lv 33, PT2–PT6 auto parties at D4–D6), restored with `backup/import` (`skipConfirmation:true`) into a fresh account. No gear, formation, deity or expedition changes during the 300 days; PT1 had no Clear-Gate; PT5 now also got past its gate (it was stuck at "Boss gate 0/2" in the 100-day run), so **all six parties produced quests**.
- **The counters started from zero** (see bug 1), so every number below is "new since restore". The new string is `<id>-<ok>/<cancelled>/<failed>/<total>`.

## Balance reading
1. **The fix worked for the dead quests:** sleeping, healing and consecutive-wins went from 0% to 43%, 38% and 9%; embezzlement and poor-kid roughly doubled.
2. **Still too easy (100%, no tension):** squander, exercise, savings, AFK. With 400k+ gold per party, gold quests cannot fail at this stage (the new savings range 300–1,500 ×2.2 was still 42/42). Consider scaling by wealth or level.
3. **Still too hard:**
   - **treasure-super-rare 1/41** — a super-rare must drop in 24 h; the chance is ~2%. Either extend the limit to ~96 h or have it also accept a rare (not super-rare) drop.
   - **consecutive-wins 4/43** — 5–20 ×1.3–2.0 straight wins is almost never reached; a lv2 draw of 6–10 is the only winnable one. Suggest counting Clear+Return as "win", or halving the base range.
4. **Middle (38–55%)** sleeping, healing, embezzlement, boss-rare, poor-kid look like a sensible target band; healing at 38% (2–10 min × lv) now depends on the rest time per cycle and the lv.
5. PT1 (D8, lv4 quests, bigger targets) completes less (49%) than the D4 parties (64–65%), so level scaling does bite where intended.

## Bugs / concerns
1. **Import loses the old counters.** The restored save contains `sideQuestStats` for all six parties (checked with the twin: PT1 `{"7":{"success":1,"cancelled":0,"total":1}}` …), but right after `backup/import` on build 22 every `party.sideQuestStatistics` was `[]` ( `STATS day0 {}`), where on build 19 the same import showed all 36 quests. Old-format entries (no `failed` field) are apparently discarded or not shown. If intended ("counters restart"), document it in 9.1.4; if not, migrate `failed: 0`. I did not trace the code path.
2. For saves written before the fix, `cancelled` meant "expired or cancelled"; there is no way to tell them apart after the split.
3. `compact.sideQuest` never reports *why* a party has no quest (gated vs. unlucky) — the 100-day PT5 (0 quests) is invisible without reading `clearGate`.
4. The in-game date after import is the real date again (reported earlier); irrelevant for stats.

## Tips
- `python3 mon.py 300 21600 log.txt` reproduces the experiment; the `STATS` lines every 30 days give the time series used above.
- Parse the new 4-field string; the old 3-field regex silently skips every entry.
