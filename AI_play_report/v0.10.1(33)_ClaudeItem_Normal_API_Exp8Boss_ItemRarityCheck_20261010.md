## What decided the run

1. **D6 was fast by luck plus farming.** After a 12 h farm at 2f-3 (tier-6 1,945, then 2,200) I applied a fresh proxyopt attacker plan and switched to `all`. The boss gate and the boss fell within about 8 runs, although the forecast was only ~13 % per run. The forecast is noisy (Upd saw the same); the live gate success was much higher than the 150-sample forecast. Breakers (6309) were in the plan only through the itemswap evaluation (2 copies owned) and were never needed.
2. **D7 was gear-limited until Lv30.** Boss-only kills stayed 0-9 % for 2.5 days of tier-7 farming (1,681 to 3,940); no GA, deity, order or element-weight lever helped. At Lv30 the new slot jumped boss-only kills to 90 % and reach to 85 %, and the boss fell on the second batch (Upd saw the same jump).
3. **D8 gates 4-5 and the boss gate** needed two 12 h tier-8 farms at 3f-3 (tier 8: 77, then 1,004, 1,810, 2,138). Gate 5 was 19 % per run on a fresh plan and fell once the plan reached ~35 % (proxyopt A, 5 calls). The boss gate and the boss then fell in 12 loops.
4. **Loops.** 1,080-1,800 s while gates were open, 2,520 s/k6 for farming (36 calls per 12 h). Skipping refused sorties wasted no calls (3 refused 409s were builds, not sorties).

## Rarity update audit (builds 32-33)

Working as specified:

- Compendium `itemRarity` filter accepts `common|uncommon|rare|epic|mythic|all`. Items, `searchItems`, shop entries all report `itemRarity`.
- Old `rarity` parameter is rejected with a clear 400 (`rarity` is not a known member); old values (`bossRare`) are rejected with the allowed list.
- `read/diary/{p}/diarySetting` returns `superRareThreshold`, `epicThreshold`, `mythicThreshold`, `rareThreshold` with valid options; no `bossThreshold`.
- No stale rarity key appeared in any of the 1,790 responses, including sortie rewards, expedition observations, diary reads, the shop and exports.

Concerns:

1. **Stale label in an item name.** Item 3316 is named `Orcinian Elite Rare Wand` in English (`src/i18n/en.ts:1888`, also `playing_guide/Playing_Guide_Item_compendium.md:147`). "Elite Rare" is the retired rarity name; the item is now plain `rare`. Suggest renaming it (for example "Orcinian Wand") in all languages.
2. **Build-33 D-source bonus set is hard to verify through the API.** 3304 Sharkskin Mantle shows `cBonus c.magical-defense+8`, `d.HP:30`, `d.magical_defense:50`, `r.thunder-6`; 3314 Whalewhisper Bow shows `c.ranged-NoA+5`, `c.accuracy+0.005`. The compendium shows base values with no marker that these are the "source A" set. I did not find a spec statement of the expected bonus list, so I could not confirm the change from the API alone.
3. **Compendium `revealed:false` entries** show only `itemId`, `category`, `itemRarity`, `tier`; that matches the spec intent but means a client cannot tell which of tier-3 rares are D-source before they drop.
4. **Item drop popups, clairvoyance rewards and Gods Battle `epic` progress** were not exercised: the clairvoyance read returned `available:false`, and no Gods Battle was run. Those parts of the rename are untested here.

## Useful tips for another AI

1. Build `dist` (`npm run build`), the twin (`twin/build.mjs`) and the emulator (`extract_autoequip.py` + `build2.mjs`) from HEAD, start Electron straight from the repo root with `BOKEMO_PLAY_USERDATA` and `BOKEMO_PLAY_DESCRIPTOR` in the scratchpad (no app copy needed), and set `BOKEMO_PLAY_DIR` to the run folder for `t.py`.
2. After the boss gate opens, `run.py` stops with "BOSS OPEN / no gates": that is when to plan for the boss, not to keep looping (D1 lost ~45 game minutes and 30 calls that way). `boss.sh tag d weights` (export, proxyopt, hpopt, boss-only damage and kills) does this in one command.
3. Re-plan with `plan.sh` at each stall (about 40 s, 5-15 calls). Plans A (proxyopt attackers) were usually enough; the GA seldom beat them. When a gate forecast is under 25 %, farm tier drops (`cad.mjs` to pick depth) instead of grinding the streak.
4. Lv30 is a hard breakpoint at D7 (a new slot). EXP: Lv29 to Lv30 took about 36 game hours of 4f-3/4f-4 farming; farming D7 at 4f-x gives about 160k EXP per 12 h in the live run (the farm emulator predicts about a third of that).
5. Use `STALL=0` with `stopOpen=0` for farm loops and boss attempts so `run.py` does not stop on two equal observations (a gate streak can look identical for hours).
6. Deity: Fertility on PT1 from D5 (set the PT holding it to `none` first); Fortification only at the D4 gates.
7. Boss element weights worth knowing: a `none:3` boss-only plan at D7 doubled boss kills (25 % vs 9 %) but could not survive the route (reach 6 %).

## Opportunities for future runs

- **D7 is still 4.2 days (33 % of the run).** Reaching Lv30 earlier is the clearest saving. EXP per run rises steeply with depth (`farm.mjs`: 3f-3 about 46k, `all` about 79k per 12 h by emulator), so an EXP-focused farm at the end of D6 or start of D7 may pay back.
- **Gate forecasts need a second opinion.** The twin reported 13 % for D6 gate 6 and the live run opened it within ~8 runs; at D8 gate 5 it reported 35 % and the live run needed 12 h. Run `gatecheck.mjs` (exact emulator) before long grinds.
- **Calls.** 610 elapsed + 537 sortie + 391 builds + 171 observations + 40 exports. The builds are the main avoidable cost (multi-character equip commit would cut them). Observing every 6 loops instead of 4 saves about 40 reads.
- Loops of 2,520 s were fine for farming; for gate attempts 1,800 s at D6 to D8 matched the natural cycle.
