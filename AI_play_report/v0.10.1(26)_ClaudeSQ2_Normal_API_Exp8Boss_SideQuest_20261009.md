## What decided the run
1. **Misread gate list at D2 cost 1 day**: `entryGate(3)+godEntry(2)` means the D2 boss is OPEN, not beaten. I stepped 2 blocks and tried to change destination. Boss beaten means a diary `bossFirstClear` or a `godGate`/`[]` list. (Fable's tip 4 says the same; read it twice.)
2. **D8 was lost on EXP, not plans**: arrived at Lv30 on day 28; the gate-2 plan was rated only after 5 days of farming at Lv31 (D7 `beforeBoss` gave only 66k EXP/12 h at Lv30, 7 blocks for one level). The same plan (35 calls, 91%) at Lv31 cleared gates 2–5 and the boss gate in 2.2 days. **Rating a plan on arrival at D8 would have saved about 6 days.** Farming is slower than a 35-call plan.
3. **`depthLimit Xf-3` carried D2, D4–D8 gates again** (zero build calls for D4 gates 2–5, D5 1–4, D6 1–4, D7 1–4).
4. **Per-gate weight scan was decisive**: D4 boss thunder 2 (57%) vs equal (36%); D5 gate 5 ice 2 (87%) vs equal (30%); D7 gate 5 fire 1.5 (64%) vs equal (45%); D8 equal (91%) vs thunder (94%) vs fire (80%).
5. D4 gate 1 / D5 gate 5 / D7 boss wasted 4–6 days at "progress 0" before I rated; rate the first time a gate does not move in 2 blocks.

## Tips for another AI
1. `open.py` + `cyc.py 2 43200 0` + `sortie 3` is enough for D1 at day ~1.3. Use `python3 g.py <sec>` (one step + one gate read, 2 calls) instead of `st.py` (its default arg is `sys.argv[1]` seconds; passing `1` errors).
2. Do not sortie after D3 (sorties only gave `Return/Draw`; EXP is the clock).
3. `pipe.sh x D F "weights"`: run equal + thunder-heavy + ice/fire-heavy in parallel (3 shells); plan C is HP+attackers. Check numbers: > 50% apply; < 10% farm EXP or change dungeon.
4. D8 chain at Lv31 with plan C: gates 2–5 fell in 1.2 days, boss gate and boss in 1 day. Rate at D8 arrival; apply it immediately at Lv30 if ≥ 60%.
5. `deity_in_use` for Fertility (held by PT3 from signup) cost 2 wasted calls; skip it unless the lever gains > 10 points (it gave +2 at D5).
6. Poll the boss with `g.py 10800` (3 h) when the gate list is `godEntry` only; `godGate n/3` means the boss fell.

### Balance reading
1. **Hard tier unchanged from ClaudeSQ (build 25) and the 300-day follow-up**: consecutive-wins 0/3 (0/6 across both runs), treasure-super-rare 0/4 (0/6 across both), poor-kid 1/3 (2/7). These three produce 9 of 11 failures. Two independent runs, same ordering, so I read it as real rather than noise.
2. **Easy tier 100%**: squander, exercise, donation, AFK, healing, sleeping, boss-rare, losers, savings (3/3 and 4/4 each). Healing/savings (changed in build 26 to Spec 1.1.9 ranges) completed 3/3 each; gold stocks (47k–240k per party) make gold quests trivially safe.
3. **Embezzlement 2/4** (with ClaudeSQ 3/4... total 5/8) is the only mid-difficulty quest; failures were PT1 and PT4 (both with the least gold).
4. **Frequency is the real constraint, not difficulty.** 42 quests in 37 days over six parties; the played party PT1 received exactly one, and it failed. PT1 sat on Clear-Gate streaks the whole time (Spec 5.1.2: no roll while a gate is active). Reward value (one jewel) is invisible in a speed run.
5. Idle PT4 received 10 quests (with PT4 at Lv19 and `lastDestination` 1) vs 0 in the previous run: the roll rate looks ~uniform and the difference is chance/gate state, so the "no quests when stuck on a gate" seen earlier is gate-state dependent, not a bug.
6. Statistics (per-party counts of `clear/return/draw/retreat/defeat`, `donatedGold`, `savedGold`) are consistent: PT4 `savedGold` 239,956 with `donatedGold` 0 matches savings 1/1 and donation never offered.

### Bugs and concerns
1. **Sortie batch stops at first Defeat, returns fewer outcomes, no remaining charge/HP** (same as earlier reports; I saw it once at D3).
2. **Gate list `[]` or `godEntry`-only is ambiguous** (boss open vs beaten vs cleared dungeon). `entryGate(next)+godEntry(current)` = boss open, `godGate n/3` = beaten. I misread it at D2 and lost a day. A `bossDefeated` boolean per party in `read/observation/expedition` would remove the ambiguity.
3. **Fertility held by PT3 from signup (`deity_in_use`)** wastes two calls per attempt; consider not pre-assigning deities to idle parties or documenting the default holders.
4. **Side quest stats: no timestamps, no per-quest reward, no `expired` vs `cancelled` distinction** (0 cancelled, unverifiable).
5. **Played party gets ~0 quests** (1 in 37 days) because it is always on a Clear-Gate; with quests tuned for rewards, the main party never sees them. Consider rolling during gate streaks with a longer deadline, or accept as design.
6. `st.py` (my tool): `python3 st.py 1` is read as a 1-second step; fixed by using `g.py`.
