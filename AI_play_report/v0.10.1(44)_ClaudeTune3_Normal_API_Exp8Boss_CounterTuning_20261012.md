## How stagedEquipmentTuning did

- **260 tunings, 46 accepted (17.7 %).** That is higher than ClaudeTune (7 %) and ClaudeTune2 (13 %), because the tuning ran before the GA, not after it.
  - By objective: `success` 35 of 196, `success` scored by `progress` 0 of 28, `bossDamage` 11 of 36.
  - Kept items: 28 offense-element switches, 18 resistance changes, 12 ability items.
- **Speed:** median 2.9 s, at most 9.5 s, 820 s in total. The 70 GA calls took 5.0 h of real time.
- **Large single wins (tuning alone):**
  - D2 3f-4: char 4 `r.ice`, 35 → 80 %.
  - D3 4f-4 gate: char 4 `e.ice`, 8 → 31 %.
  - D5 gate 4: char 4 `e.ice`, 0.1 → 44.6 % (Draw 57 → 10 %).
  - D7 at Lv30: chars 4 and 2 put `a.requiem` into the new empty slot and switched to `r.ice`, giving +52 % and +84 % (route 25 → 65 %).
  - D8 arrival: 3 of 4 tunings were accepted, which repaired the route after the D7 boss build.
- **Counter map (build 44):** counter abilities were recorded and kept where they applied:
  - D2 boss: `a.null-life-drain` was the only ability candidate. It was kept (+10.7 %).
  - D6 gate (2f-4): `a.illusion-breaker` 6309 was kept (+16 %).
  - D6 boss room: 6309 was recorded once it was free in the Inventory (char 2 had worn it before).
  - D7: `a.requiem` was the top ability twice.
  - D8 boss: `a.slow` was kept.
  - I owned no item with a D6 terrain counter (`a.unforgettable`, `a.equation-breaker`, `a.domain-breaker`), so the terrain counters were not exercised there.
- **Where tuning did nothing:** the 12 D1 tunings recorded nothing; 8 tried no candidate at all (1,300 runs = baseline plus the current quick run) and 4 tried one. The 28 `progress`-scored tunings (route at 0 %) were never accepted.

## Useful tips for another AI

1. **Re-plan order: tuning first, then a big GA.** Tune each attacker (4, 2, 3, 5; about 1 call and 3 s each), apply what is accepted, and run the GA only if the rate is still under about 85 %. The GA uses `populationSize 64, generations 200, quickRuns 300, confirmRuns 1000, timeBudgetSeconds 300`, with all components.
   - Medium GAs were useless at the D2 boss (route 0-1 %). One big pass took it from 1 to 59 %.
   - Use the big GA from D2 on.
2. **Re-plan whenever the first gate floor changes.** The success rate stops at the first closed gate, so 99 % at gate 1 says nothing about gate 2. The `hl.py` AUTOGA hook re-plans on each new gate floor, and after NST observations without progress.
3. **Bosses (`boss.sh`):**
   - First a `bossDamage` tuning on each attacker, then a big `bossDamage` GA if the boss-only kill rate is under 85 %.
   - Then 1 to 3 big `success` passes until the route is about 10 % or more.
   - Attack once the boss-only rate is at least 45 % and the route at least 10 %.
   - D3 to D8 bosses each fell within 1 to 9 sorties or AFK runs.
4. **Farm only with a build that survives.**
   - Bad: batched farming (K=6, HPF2=0) at D6 5f-3 gave only 6 successes in 32 runs, and only one level in 12 h, because batch runs 2..K start at low HP.
   - Bad: an `experience` GA build at 5f-3 (31 % success) also did poorly.
   - Good: a `success` GA at depth 5f-4, then the HP-aware loop at 5f-4. That gave 24 of 29 Returns and Lv25 → 27 in about 22 h.
   - D7: 5f-3 (56 %, 3,000 EXP per run) for Lv27 → 30 (2.3 days).
   - D8: 4f-3 (72 %) for 12 h.
5. **Level breakpoints held:** Lv27 for the D6 boss, and Lv30 for D7 (new slot; run a tuning right after it, because step 4 fills the empty slot first).
6. **D8:** gates 1 to 4 went with re-plans. Gate 5 was at 25 %, so I farmed 4f-3 for 12 h (Lv31). Gate 5 and the boss gate then fell in 12 runs.
7. **Skip tuning in D1.** Nothing usable is in the Inventory yet.

## Bugs, concerns, suggestions

No game bug was found. Every request returned 200.

1. **Spec gap: 9.1.4 does not mention the build-44 counter map.**
   - 9.1.4 (Staged Equipment Tuning, Bottleneck and Step 4) still says "`a.X-breaker` matches an enemy with `a.X`" and "breakers that match the bottleneck enemies are drawn first".
   - It does not cover the 7.1.5.1 counters: `a.null-*`, `a.pursuit`, `a.requiem`, `a.coldproof`, the terrain counters, or the party-wide `a.null-counter` rule.
   - 9.1.4 is AI-owned and should follow 7.1.5.1.
2. **The counter priority cannot be seen in the response.** `steps.ability` lists only the qualifying candidates.
   - A client cannot tell which counters matched the bottleneck room, or whether one was tried and failed.
   - Suggestion: return the matched counter abilities (e.g. `bottleneckCounters`) and the number of candidates tried per step.
3. **Noise qualifies at the extremes of the rarer-side ratio.**
   - Near `progress` ≈ 1 or `success` ≈ 0, a few quick runs make +30 to +180 %.
   - D6 (`progress`): char 4 recorded `r.thunder` +32 %, `a.boost` +19 % and `a.illusion-breaker` +16 %. Step 5 kept none of them (10,200 runs spent).
   - D8 route at 4 %: `r.ice + r.thunder` scored +183 % on quick runs, then +29 % in step 5, then was rejected.
   - Suggestion: qualify a candidate on the paired quick-run difference against its standard error, not on the ratio alone.
4. **`improvement` is not readable near 0.** An accepted D5 tuning reported `improvement: 445` (0.1 → 44.6 %, denominator 0.001).
   - It is correct by the spec, but a client should rank by `gain`.
   - Suggestion: document this, or cap it for display.
5. **D1 tuning tries nothing.** 12 calls recorded nothing: 8 had `simulationRuns` 1,300 (baseline plus the current quick run, so zero candidates) and 4 had 1,600 (one candidate), even though the Inventory held 63 items, including 1406 `a.true-sight`.
   - Likely cause: no elemental or resistance items yet, and the ability items are not usable by the opening classes.
   - Suggestion: return `candidatesTried: 0` with a reason.
6. **`gaSearch` `success` at 0 % still returns `noisy 0 → 0`** (D3 and D6 boss routes), even with the `progress` tie-break. The verdict uses success only, so nothing is applied.
   - Suggestion: when the before rate is 0, judge the verdict on `progress`, as the tuning's `scoredBy` does.
7. **Repeat: the API heals only per whole cycle.** Four boss-loop sorties (D6 1, D7 2, D8 1) were skipped because HP was still low after 4 top-ups of 90 s each. A `secondsToNextCycle` per party is still the cheapest fix.
8. **My own tool bug:** `tw.py` skipped the GA on `bossDamage` whenever one tuning was accepted. At D3 the kill rate stayed at 0 %. I fixed it to gate on the Success rate.
