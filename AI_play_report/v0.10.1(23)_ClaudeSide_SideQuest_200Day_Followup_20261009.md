## Balance reading
1. **Treasure-super-rare is now 0/30** in this run, 1/41 before, 1/11 before that — effectively impossible (a super-rare drop inside 24 h). It is a dead quest, like sleeping was.
2. **Consecutive-wins 2/29 (7%)** is the other outlier: targets of 8–25 straight wins versus the parties' win streaks. The ×1.2–1.6 multipliers did not move it (9% → 7%).
3. The synced sleeping (16 h) and healing (×1.2–1.6) values moved those to 73% and 70%; poor-kid to 63%; boss-rare 45%. These five (sleeping, healing, poor-kid, boss-rare, embezzlement 50%) now sit in a healthy 45–73% band.
4. Gold/time quests (squander, exercise, savings, AFK) are still 100%, i.e. no failure risk for any party (parties hold 400k+ gold). Donation/losers ~78%.
5. D8 PT1 stays below the D4–D5 parties (59% vs 61–72%), a mild level scaling effect.
