## Concept result: tuning parameters

The tuning has many parameters earlier runs left at their defaults (9.1.3 2-3-7). I ran paired tests: same `seed`, same save revision, nothing applied (tuning is read-only), logged in `tunevar_log.jsonl` (29 calls) by `client/tvar.py`.

| Variant | What it changes | Result |
|---|---|---|
| `cand3` (offense / defense / ability candidate count 3) | 3 candidates per step | Five paired cases (D5 route char 4, D7 route chars 4, 2, 3, 5): the accept / reject outcome was the same in all five; the recorded top candidates were identical in three. D5 char 4 scored +0.120 vs +0.084 for the default (a different second `e.ice` candidate made step 5); D7 char 3 recorded other defense candidates (both rejected). Trial count rose (offense 9 vs 6, defense 13 vs 8), about 15-30 % more runs. Mild gain, no harm. |
| `trial` (6 / 10 / 40 trial runs) | more trial runs per candidate | No difference where the default found nothing (D2 boss). Not tested elsewhere. |
| `q1000` (`quickRuns` 1000) | the winner's-curse test | **D8 gate 5 (chars 4, 2, 3, 5): the default (quick 300) recorded +36 to +45 % candidates (`r.ice+r.thunder` +36 %, `a.null-corrode` +44 %, `e.fire` +38 %); with `quickRuns` 1000 the same candidates fell to +3 to +15 %** and the tuning was rejected either way. This confirms the winner's curse of earlier reports with a controlled test. |
| `low` (`minImprovement` and `minGateImprovement` 0.03) | lower qualify bar | No effect in the one test (nothing to qualify). |
| `big` (all of the above) | all together | Same accept / reject outcome as the default in 5 of 5 pairs (D5 route char 4 accepted by both, +0.097 vs +0.084; D8 gate 5 chars 4, 2, 3, 5 rejected by both); 3-4x the runs (20 s instead of 5 s). |

Verdict: the step-5 / step-6 checks, not the parameters, decide the outcome. A wider candidate list never turned a reject into an accept in these tests, and a higher `quickRuns` only gave honest numbers. **The tuning is most useful when the bottleneck is a clear defect** (an element or a resistance switch, a counter item) and **for `bossDamage`**.

## Tips for another AI

1. **Heal exactly.** `hl2.py` sends one `elapsed` of `max(60, secondsToNextCycle)` per loop, then a sortie. Heal misses were 12 in 1,267 `elapsed` calls (1 %), all right after a reprice (`changeExpedition` or a build apply). 14 `elapsed` calls (1 %) were 150 s or shorter. One loop is about 1 real second, so loops are cheap and **GAs are the real-time cost**.
2. **Re-plan order that worked:** tuning first (about 5 s, 1 call per attacker, one in ten is accepted), then a big full-component GA (`populationSize` 64, `generations` 200, `quickRuns` 100, 180 s) only if the route is under 85 %. At D2 to D4 every new gate floor was fixed by one GA (veryGood 9 to 98 %). `quickRuns` 100 gives about 2.5x the evaluations; `quickRuns` 300 with `allDeities` at D8 gate 5 gave `noChange` too.
3. **During a grind, re-plan every 6 observations (36 loops, about 0.5 game day), not every 40.** A re-plan costs 0 game time. At D8 the route went 8 to 29 % in one GA pass after the grind had stalled at 3 to 7 % for days. `NST=6` in `hl2.py` is the right value (I used 40 at D8 and lost days).
4. **Route vs boss conflict (the main trap).**
   - A `bossDamage` GA gives a 95 to 100 % boss-only kill but can drop the route to 0 % (D5: 14.6 to 0 %, dying from room 1f).
   - A `success` GA scored by `progress` (route at exactly 0 %) is flat and, when it applies, **reverts the boss build** (D7: `veryGood` 0.405 to 0.773, boss-only share 0.98 to 0.45, kill 84 to 0 %).
   - What worked: (a) run the `bossDamage` GA again to restore the boss build, (b) get the route above 0 % (a success tuning, or a GA at a depth with success above 0), then (c) run `success` GAs, which are scored by `success` and keep the boss build (D5: 1.2 to 6.8 to 13 % with boss-only 95 %; D7: 2 to 3 to 11 % with boss-only 68 %).
   - Attack when boss-only is at least 45 % and the route at least 10 %. D5 fell on the 10th sortie, D6 on the 10th, D7 on the 1st, D8 on the 6th.
5. **Depth ladder for a dead route:** after the D5 boss GA, a `success` GA at depth `4f-3` took the route 6.6 to 73 %. But the ladder GA also took the boss-only kill to 0, so use it only for farming builds, not for a boss attack.
6. **Farm with a surviving build at the EXP-rich depth.** D6 `5f-3` (2,490 EXP/run even at 35 % success; drops 12/run) took Lv25 to Lv30 in 4.4 game days with `hl2.py` `K=1`, `STOPLV=30`, `NST=6` (264 sortie runs, GA every 36 loops: 33 to 44, 45 to 63, 72 to 79 %). Depth probe (2 calls per depth): D6 `4f-3` 73 % / 2,111 EXP; `5f-3` 33 % / 2,419; `5f-4` 10 % / 2,460. D5 EXP is only about 800-1,000 per run: do not farm there.
7. **Start the D6 farm as soon as gate 3 stops moving** (at Lv23-24), not after the boss-gate tries at Lv25. At Lv30 the route at `all` was 69 % and the 2-streak boss gate opened in 6 loops.
8. **Valid `depthLimit` values:** `all`, `4f-3`, `5f-3`, `5f-4` work; `5f-1`, `6f-1` and `6f-3` return 400 (undocumented).
9. **Skip the AUTOGA hook when the boss gate is open** (`hl2.py` fix: `if g!='Entry gate': autoga()`); my first D2 boss open wasted a 3 min GA.
10. **Use `tvar.py` only after the default tuning records candidates.** My 6 calls at the D2 boss, where `candidatesTried` was 0, were wasted.

- **D8 grind:** re-plan every 36 loops. Estimated saving: 3 to 4 days (the one late GA pass lifted the route 8 to 29 %).
- **D6:** farm from Lv23-24, not Lv25; reach Lv30 before the D6 boss (as here); this shaves about 1 to 1.5 days.

## Bugs, concerns, suggestions

No game bug blocked the run. Every request was 200 except the two invalid `depthLimit` probes.

1. **Valid `depthLimit` values are still undocumented** (third report in a row). Accepted: `all`, `4f-3`, `5f-3`, `5f-4`; refused with 400 `invalid_request`: `5f-1`, `6f-1`, `6f-3`. Suggestion: document the rule in 9.1.3, or return `validOptions` in the error.
2. **Route GA and boss GA overwrite each other** (repeat of Tune2, Tune3 and Tune4).
   - D5: `bossDamage` GA, route 14.6 to 0 %. D7: a `veryGood` progress-scored `success` GA took boss-only kill 84 to 0 %.
   - The verdict looks only at the route score, so the client cannot see that the boss build was lost.
   - Suggestion: `verification` should also report the boss-only share before and after, or `gaSearch` should take `minBossDamage` / `minSuccess` constraints.
3. **The `progress` verdict applied a build that was worse for the goal.** Same case as above: with the route at exactly 0 %, `scoredBy: progress` is flat and noisy for the kill. Suggestion: with `progress`, apply only if the boss-only share does not drop by more than its standard error, or require the client to confirm.
4. **`gaSearch` `noChange` still runs the whole budget.** 21 of 96 GAs returned `noChange` after the full 150-300 s (about 60 min real). Suggestion: stop early when the best has not changed for N generations (as the `bossDamage` GA already does at 56-100 s).
5. **Winner's curse is measurable** (new controlled data above): with `quickRuns` 300 the step 2-4 candidates read +36 to +45 %, with `quickRuns` 1000 they were +3 to +15 %. Suggestion: default `quickRuns` 1000 for tuning (it costs 20 s instead of 5 s), or qualify a candidate by its paired difference against the standard error.
6. **`candidatesTried` has no reason for 0.** At the D2 boss char 4 tried 0 offense and 0 defense candidates with 1 ability candidate, and nothing was recorded. A reason (`noItem`, `sameElementOnly`, `alreadyBest`) would let a client skip the call.
7. **Stale `secondsToNextCycle` after a reprice** (repeat of Tune4 item 1): 12 heal misses in 1,267 `elapsed`, all right after `changeExpedition` or `applyBuildResult`; none of these responses returns the new value.
8. **My own tool notes:** `hl2.py` ran AUTOGA at the boss open (fixed); `NST=40` in the D8 grind was a bad value; `tvar.py` needs a `candidatesTried` check first.
