## Strategy and what happened

I read the twelve existing reports before playing and recorded the [preplay strategy](../play_runs/2026-10-03_GateBurst/STRATEGY.md). The concept was burst damage: Kemo lord/lord and Laika guardian/guardian supporting three ranged attackers and one caster. Side parties supplied equipment and jewels. Half-day elapsed blocks limited observation calls; exact exports supplied offline forecasts. Only official API commits changed the live party.

The final level-33 formation was Laika, Kemo, Sota, Kuzunoha, Lop, Selfin. Sota and Kuzunoha were Ursan ninja/rangers; Lop was a Leporian sword-saint/ranger; Selfin was a Cervin alchemist/wizard. The final faith was Fertility, with thunder ranged attacks and roughly 25,000 party HP. Faith and formation changed when forecasts showed substantial benefits: Mirage helped Expedition 7, Precision helped an Expedition 8 gate, and Fertility helped the fifth gate.

## Tips for another AI

- Read battle logs before rebuilding. The dangerous fifth-floor Erne used physical ranged/melee attacks; assuming it was a magic problem led to unhelpful offline alternatives.
- Optimize the current gate, then retest for the full dungeon. A strong gate build need not be the best boss build. Formation and faith can matter more than a small stat increase.
- Concentrate support HP and physical defense while preserving attacker burst. Extra attack count and distinct Might/Focus jewel ranks were valuable. Different ranks can stack; duplicate identical ranks on one character are restricted.
- Check freshly acquired offensive jewels before doing a full rebuild. Two attachments improved the fifth-gate forecast at very low call cost.
- SEMI equipment leaves newly unlocked slots empty. Inspect slot counts around level 30 and 40; do not assume level 35 adds slots.
- Use charged instant sorties to finish a nearly completed gate streak or attempt an accessible boss at zero game time. Stop immediately on Clear; do not let a helper continue after victory.
- Test terrain responses end to end. Mana burn is per spell action, not per hit. The owned Mana Ward catalyst lowered this build's clear forecast because of lost damage, so it was rejected.
- Validate candidates with a separate random stream. Small forecast differences are noisy, and stat-only equipment scoring misses abilities and enemy behavior.
- Confirm the native boss flag and retained room-24 Clear log. A boss-rare diary title alone does not establish the clear.

## Opportunities for future runs

The largest opportunity is reaching the Expedition 8 fifth gate with the stronger ranged package earlier. Reuse the successful party concept, but derive gear from the actual owned pool rather than copying item strings blindly. Favor large, independently validated improvements and fewer marginal rebuilds: **320 build commits** dominated this run's call count. Preserve unchanged slots and jewel attachments from the start.

Use shorter elapsed blocks once the boss becomes accessible, and spend available instant charges before advancing time. A sequential forecast that models real gate streaks, encounter bags, healing and sortie stock would better predict time to unlock than independent full-health trials. Terrain and enemy summaries from previous reports should be carried into the initial plan, rather than rediscovered during a wall.

## Bugs and improvements

No confirmed gameplay bug or HTTP API error occurred. Display-free Electron startup failed with a segmentation fault in this environment; Xvfb allowed the official launcher to run. After the interruption, the runtime had stopped and two connection attempts failed before it was restarted with the same persisted account. These failures are recorded separately because the response ledger only records completed HTTP requests.

Suggested improvements: provide a supported headless launcher with clearer startup diagnostics; log transport failures as well as responses; support atomic multi-character equipment/jewel changes; expose an option to fill empty SEMI slots; and include gate/boss milestones and their game timestamps in elapsed receipts. Those changes would reduce recovery work, API calls and time overshoot.
