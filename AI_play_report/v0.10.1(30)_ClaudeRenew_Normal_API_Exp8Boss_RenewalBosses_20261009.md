## Concept

**"Renewal Bosses"**: build 30 gave every boss new modifiers. I used the ClaudeCharge "Charge-Clock" baseline with the same party and opening, and added:

- plan each renewed boss for its new weakness;
- rate the boss as soon as its gate opens;
- re-plan from a fresh export at every stall.

## What decided the run

1. **Very short early loops.** Before the D1 boss the charges refill in 1/2/4 min.
   - 1200 s + sortie 3 opened the D1 boss gate at 0.24 d. Charge used 3 h loops.
   - A pure short-loop policy never beats the boss, because SEMI auto-equip does not equip new drops. So every boss and every stall needs a plan.
2. **Gate progress is a consecutive streak.** See `applyClearGateOutcome`. A Draw, Retreat or Defeat resets it; a shallow Return neither counts nor resets.
   - A 6-streak at 25% is hopeless. Do not grind a gate below ~60%/run; farm drops and re-plan instead.
3. **Farm depth for drops.** Use `farm.mjs`, then pick the deepest depth with ≤5% Defeat:
   - D6: `4f-4`;
   - D7: `3f-3`.

   Use it with **1080 s + sortie 2** loops. Defeat ends a sortie batch, and HP 0 makes the next sortie fail with `party_exhausted`.
   - D6 runs went from 35 to 75 per 12 h, and tier-6 drops roughly doubled.
   - D7: tier-7 went from 583 to 2,150 in 24 h, and gate 5 went from 9% to 47%.
4. **D6 boss mechanics.** It casts 3 magical thunder hits (~20k) in COMBAT2, then soul-reap (<17% HP = death).
   - Party HP was 11–14k, so the party got exactly one attack round. Level and order did not help, and neither did deity or class levers (God of Cunning made it worse).
   - What broke it: more tier-6 drops, then a cold boss-only GA (4 calls, 18% from full HP), then **Goddess of Restoration** + depth `all`. The full-route kill rate was ~3%/run.
5. **D8: HP for the supports is the lever.** `hpopt` (W 0.2–0.5) plus thunder-3 attackers took gate 2 from 37% to 66%, then 73% with the GA. Gates 2–3 fell in 6 h.
   - Gate 5 (12–25%) and the boss gate (10–22%) were Retreat attrition (HP ≤30% after a win). They needed Lv32, about 2.7k tier-8 items and a defense plan on top (`mdopt.mjs` W 0.5).
6. **Deity is unique across parties.** A newly unlocked party takes a deity, e.g. PT3 got Fertility, and PT1's change then returns 409 `deity_in_use`. Move the other party's deity first (1 call).

## Useful tips for another AI

1. Build the dist from HEAD and rebuild the twin and emulator before signup (`twin/build.mjs`, `extract_autoequip.py` + `build2.mjs`). The bosses changed in build 30, so old bundles are wrong.
2. **Plan at boss-open and at every stall.**
   - `run.py` with `STALL=1` stops when two observations in a row are identical.
   - Then: `plan.sh tag d "f,f"` (gates), `bossfight.mjs` (boss), and a weight sweep. Each renewed boss wanted a different element weight (table above).
3. **Boss-only GA:** `OBJ=bossonly`, seeded from the best proxyopt plan. If that plan is useless, seed it from an empty plan (D6).
4. **Rate route attrition with `roomtab.mjs`.**
   - If losses come from one elite room: accuracy (Precision) or damage.
   - If they are Retreats spread across many rooms: support HP/defense (`hpopt` / `mdopt`).
5. Switch depth back to `all` once the boss-only kill rate is ≥15%. Use `4f-4`/`3f-3` only while farming.
6. A sortie after a Defeat wastes a call (`party_exhausted`, 186 calls this run). In long walls, prefer depths where Defeat is rare.

## Opportunities for future runs

- **Calls.** 1,024 elapsed + 1,024 sortie calls. 1080 s/k=2 loops cost ~6.7 calls per game hour.
  - A run that values calls should use 2520 s/k=3 during farming. That is ~2.9 calls/h for ~25% fewer runs.
  - It should also skip sorties when the last outcome was a Defeat.
  - My estimate: ~1,300 calls for ~+1 d.
- **D6 (6.4 d) is the biggest block.** Things to try:
  - farm D6 at `4f-4` from the moment D6 opens (I used `all` and `beforeBoss` for 2 days first);
  - test Restoration earlier.

  Probably 2 days can be saved.
- **D8 gate 5 / boss gate (2.6 d).** A route objective that models Retreat (HP ≤30%) would help: the GA objectives `gate2` only see success/failure, and stalled at 10–25%.
- The shop (`purchaseShopItems`) and Gods Battle were not explored. Gold was tiny (200) on PT1's global wallet.

## Bugs and improvement requests

No game bug blocked the run. The build-27 compact 500 and the missing D3–D7 `bossFirstClear` entries are fixed in build 30: the diary now lists D1–D8.

1. **`party_exhausted` sorties cost a call and return 409.**
   - It fires when the party HP is 0 after a Defeat and the elapsed step completed no natural run.
   - Suggestion: report `currentHp` and `chargeStock` in the `commit/progress/elapsed` response (and in the sortie response), so a client can skip the sortie.
   - Alternatively, let the sortie start from a rested party.
2. **Deity auto-assignment on party unlock can take PT1's planned deity** (`deity_in_use`, `heldByParty: 3`).
   - Suggestion: include the holder in `read/build/party`, or allow a swap in one call.
3. **Request:** add the party level and EXP to `read/observation/expedition`.
4. **Request:** a multi-character equip commit. Builds were 417 calls here.
5. **Tooling (not game):**
   - `wallopt3 OBJ=room` produced a plan that broke the D8 gate-2 route (0%). It optimizes one room and ignores the rest.
   - `setlv` + `bossfight` showed no change at D6 because the soul-reap threshold dominates.
