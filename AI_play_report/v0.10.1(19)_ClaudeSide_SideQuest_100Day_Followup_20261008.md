# ClaudeSide — 100-day side quest monitoring after the Exp 8 boss kill (v0.10.1 build 19, Normal)

Follow-up to `v0.10.1(19)_ClaudeSide_Normal_API_Exp8Boss_20261008.md`. Data: `play_runs/normal_api_exp8_ClaudeSide_20261008/sq_monitor.log` (every quest start/progress/end of all 6 parties, sampled every 6 game hours; `STATS` lines every 10 days), script `mon.py`.

## Setup (what differs from the run)
- The play Electron session was closed after the boss run and its user-data folder was deleted. I **restored the final export with `backup/import` (`skipConfirmation:true`)** into a fresh `ClaudeSide` account, then stepped 100 game days (400 × 6 h `elapsed`, one `compact` read after each, 6 `party?partyNumber=N` reads every 10 days; 508 → 886+ requests, ~500 for monitoring). The import rebased the wall clock (in-game time showed 2026-10-08 again); only elapsed days matter here.
- **No gear/formation/deity changes.** PT1 stayed at D8 (boss already defeated, no Clear-Gate), PT2–PT4, PT6 auto parties at D4–D5 with no gates; **PT5 sat on "Boss gate 0/2" for the whole 100 days and received 0 quests** (parties with an active Clear-Gate never roll, spec 5.1.2).
- This matches the first report's finding: quests only reach parties that are not gated. Now that PT1 was ungated, it got 33 quests in 100 days.

## What the monitor shows
1. **Frequency** is ~1 quest per 100 eligible returns, so a free D8 party (~50 returns/day) got ~0.33/day. Each id now appears 2–4 times per party; the bag cycles every ~1,300 draws. So the first report's "/1 everywhere" came from the low draw count, not a cap.
2. **Four tiers of difficulty** (this is the balance picture):
   - Free (100%): squander, exercise, AFK, savings. Squander/savings/donation targets (lv4, D8: 238–951 / 923 / 768–951 gold) need gold flow, and the party had 400k–1.5M gold per party, so they are trivial.
   - Mostly fine (80%): donation, losers.
   - Hard (25–31%): embezzlement (all 9 instances caught mid-flight showed progress 0 at their last sample, targets 60–182 lv2–4; the 3 successes finished between samples), treasure-boss-rare (progress seen: 0–3 of 1–4 in 16 h), poor-kid (best 19/39, 15/18, targets 14–45 in a 9 h limit).
   - Never/almost never: **sleeping 0/14, healing 0/12, consecutive-wins 0/13, treasure-super-rare 1/11**.
3. **Healing is mathematically unreachable at every level seen:** progress is ~0.4 min of "healing time" per game hour; the best of 12 reached 9 min in 16 h; targets were 10–25 min (lv2–4, spec range 5–20 × 1.3–2.0). Only the lowest lv1–2 draws (5–9 min) can succeed and no such draw occurred.
4. **Consecutive-wins is effectively unreachable:** targets 10–33 straight wins; the best progress observed in a 16 h window was 2 (D8) and 1 (D5). Because a Defeat/Retreat/Draw resets the streak and these parties win ~25–35% of cycles, 10+ in a row has ~0 odds.
5. **Treasure-super-rare** needs a super-rare drop within 24 h; 1/11 (the one success needed the party to roll one). Expected frequency of super-rares is far lower than 1 per day.
6. The Jewel reward (rank 1…tier) was paid 96 times. Because ~49% of accepted quests are cancelled, the quest system gives roughly 0.5 jewels per 100 returns.

## Bugs / concerns (new)
1. **`q.sleeping` can never advance (0/15 in the whole account, progress never > 0).** The sleepiness branch lives in `getApproxAfkTimeQuestProgressPerCycle` (`src/hooks/useGameState.ts` ~l.1961), but its caller (~l.3915) only runs for types in `TIME_BASED_SIDE_QUEST_TYPES` (`q.exercise`, `q.healing`, `q.AFK`), which excludes `q.sleeping`; `resolveSideQuestOutcome` is the only other path. So in the AFK/API path the sleeping branch is dead. Evidence is the 100% failure rate across all parties and levels; not confirmed by a code run. Suggest adding `q.sleeping` to the call condition or moving the sleeping check.
2. **Healing and consecutive-wins appear unbalanced** (0%; see above). Suggest healing progress per rest of ≥60 s, or smaller targets; and a wins target ≤5 at D1–4.
3. **Embezzlement is 3/12 and every instance sampled in flight sat at progress 0.** Unverified: it may simply need a rare event, or it may be another dead path like sleeping. Worth a unit test per quest type under `SIMULATE_AFK`.
4. **The statistics string cannot distinguish expiry from God-Battle cancel** (asked before). With 93 cancelled it is the main missing signal.
5. **Parties blocked on a gate get no quests for months** (PT5: 0 in 100 days, 0 after day 0). Consider a hint in `compact` that explains why `sideQuest` is `null` (`gated`).
6. `backup/import` via multipart worked first try with `skipConfirmation:true`, and the account restored all six parties; the restored clock showed the real date (2026-10-08) instead of the old in-game date. Worth noting in 9.1.4.10 that import rebases `inGameTime`.

## Tips
- Poll `compact` (1 call) per 6 h step: it carries every party's `sideQuest` string (`<q>-<lv>: <progress>/<target>-<timeLeft>h/<limit>h`); the `party?partyNumber=N` read is needed only for the statistics.
- `mon.py days stepsec` reproduces this experiment on any restored save.
